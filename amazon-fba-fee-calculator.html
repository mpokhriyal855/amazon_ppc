Amazon India Referral Fees Explained

Referral fees are category-based commission percentages charged on the total selling price of an item. Under official Amazon India seller pricing schedules, referral fees vary by selling price slabs (e.g. items under ₹300 often attract lower referral commission percentages than items priced above ₹1,000).

Actual Weight vs Volumetric Weight

ACTUAL WEIGHT (kg)    vs    VOLUMETRIC WEIGHT (L × W × H ÷ 5000)
BILLING WEIGHT = MAX( Actual Weight, Volumetric Weight )

Amazon India calculates fulfilment weight handling based on billing weight. If your product is light but bulky, the volumetric calculation (Length × Width × Height in cm ÷ 5000) will exceed physical weight, placing the shipment into a higher billing weight slab.

18% GST on Amazon Seller Fees

An 18% Goods and Services Tax (GST) is levied on all Amazon India fee components (referral fees, closing fees, weight handling, and storage). Registered GST sellers can claim Input Tax Credit (ITC) on these Amazon fee invoices to offset against their output tax liability.

Frequently Asked Questions

What fees does Amazon FBA charge in India? +

Amazon India FBA charges referral fees (category & price slab dependent), closing fees (item price tier dependent), FBA fulfilment fees (pick, pack, and shipping based on billing weight and distance), monthly storage fees (per cubic foot), and 18% GST on all fee components.

How is Amazon referral fee calculated in India? +

Referral fees are calculated as a percentage of the total selling price based on the product's category and selling-price slab under the official Amazon India seller fee schedule.

Does Amazon India charge FBA shipping fees on actual or volumetric weight? +

Amazon India uses billing weight, which is the higher of actual physical weight and volumetric weight (Length × Width × Height in cm ÷ 5000), rounded up to applicable weight slabs.

What is Amazon FBA Pick & Pack fee in India? +

Pick & Pack is the fulfilment handling fee for picking, packing, and processing individual sellable units at Amazon fulfilment centers prior to dispatch.

Is GST charged on Amazon seller fees in India? +

Yes, an 18% Goods and Services Tax (GST) is levied on all Amazon fee components (referral, closing, weight handling, and storage). Registered GST sellers can claim Input Tax Credit (ITC) on these fees.

Is FBA storage fee charged per item sold? +

No, storage fees are charged monthly based on the average daily cubic volume (cubic feet) occupied by your inventory stored in Amazon fulfilment centers, regardless of whether individual units sold.

Why can my Seller Central invoice differ from calculator estimates? +

Actual Seller Central charges may differ due to automated laser cube re-measurements of packaging, shifting customer shipping destinations (Local vs Regional vs National), promotional fee waivers, or storage ageing surcharges.

How does shipping zone affect Amazon India FBA costs? +

Amazon classifies shipments into Local (intracity/same hub), Regional (intra-region), and National (cross-country). Weight handling fees increase as shipping distance expands from Local to National.
