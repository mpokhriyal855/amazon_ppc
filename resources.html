Amazon PPC Metrics at a Glance

ACoS (Ad Cost of Sales)
(Ad Spend ÷ Ad Sales) × 100
Try Calculator →
ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)
Ad Sales ÷ Ad Spend
Try Calculator →
CPC (Cost Per Click)
Ad Spend ÷ Clicks
Try Calculator →
CTR (Click-Through Rate)
(Clicks ÷ Impressions) × 100
Try Calculator →
Conversion Rate
(Orders ÷ Clicks) × 100
Try Calculator →
TACoS (Total ACoS)
(Ad Spend ÷ Total Sales) × 100
