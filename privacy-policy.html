PPC Growth Expert Privacy Data Protection & DPDP Act Compliance

This Privacy Policy explains how and why PPC Growth Expert collects, uses, and shares personal information when you interact with or use our Site. It also explains the choices you have and how to contact us.

When we say "we", "us" or "our", we mean PPC Growth Expert, with its registered office at Sector-11, Noida, Uttar Pradesh, 201301, India, operating under the brand PPC Growth Expert. When we say "Site", we mean https://ppcgrowthexpert.com/ and all of its pages. When we say "Services", we mean the digital marketing and Amazon PPC advertising services we describe on the Site, along with our blogs, case studies, calculators and contact channels.

Our Site is an educational and informational website. It tells you about the services we provide and shares blogs, case studies and calculators. We do not collect any major or sensitive personal data. The only personal information you give us is the information you choose to share with us through our contact forms or WhatsApp.

Accessibility: If you wish to print this policy, please do so from your web browser or by saving the page as a PDF.

1. INFORMATION COLLECTION

a. Information You Provide to Us

We collect personal information only when you choose to give it to us. This happens when you:

  • Fill in a contact, enquiry or consultation form on the Site.
  • Contact us directly through WhatsApp, email or phone.

The table below shows what we collect and why.

Category of Information Examples Source Purpose of Collection
Contact Information Name, email address, phone number (and any other details you enter in the form, such as company name or website URL, if the form asks for them) Directly from you through our forms To respond to your enquiry, share information about our Services, schedule calls, and follow up on your request
Communication Information Messages and details you send us on WhatsApp, email or phone, including your WhatsApp phone number and profile name Directly from you, through WhatsApp or other channels you choose To answer your questions, provide quotes or proposals, and keep a record of our conversation
Technical and Usage Information IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, pages visited, time spent on pages, referring website, approximate location (city or country level) Automatically, through cookies and similar technologies To keep the Site secure and working properly, understand how visitors use the Site, and improve our content and Services

Providing information through our forms is voluntary. If you choose not to share it, you can still browse the Site, but we will not be able to respond to your enquiry.

b. Information We Do Not Collect

We want to be clear about what we do not do:

  • Calculators: Our Site includes calculators that help you estimate figures such as ad spend, return on investment or other marketing numbers. Any numbers or details you enter into a calculator are processed in your browser to show you the result. We do not store, save or send them to our servers.
  • We do not collect payment card details, bank account details, government identification numbers, passwords, health information or any other sensitive personal data through the Site.
  • We do not require you to create an account to use the Site.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

c. Information Collected Automatically (Cookies and Similar Technologies)

Like most websites, we use cookies and similar technologies when you visit the Site. Cookies are small text files placed on your device by your browser. They help the Site work, remember your preferences, and help us understand how the Site is used.

The types of cookies we may use are:

Type of Cookie Purpose Examples
Strictly necessary Needed for the Site to function and stay secure Session and security cookies
Analytics and performance Help us understand how visitors use the Site, such as which pages are most popular, so we can improve it Google Analytics / performance monitoring tools
Advertising and marketing Help us measure the effectiveness of our own advertising and show relevant ads about our Services on other platforms Google Ads, Meta Pixel

These cookies may be set by us or by trusted third-party providers, and they may collect information such as your IP address, device and browser details, and pages viewed. You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the Site work.

d. Non-Identifying and Aggregated Information

We may collect or create information that does not identify you directly, such as the total number of visitors to a page, general geographic regions, or overall traffic trends. We may use and share this aggregated information for analytics, reporting and marketing purposes. We will not try to re-identify it.

e. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers can send a "Do Not Track" signal to websites. Our Site does not currently respond to these signals. You can use your browser's cookie settings to manage tracking, as described above.

f. Children

Our Site and Services are intended for businesses and users who are 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us at anmolpokhriyal3200@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

2. USE OF INFORMATION

a. How We Use the Information We Collect

We use the information we collect only for the purposes below:

  • To respond to your enquiries, calls, WhatsApp messages and requests for information about our Services.
  • To schedule consultations, prepare proposals and follow up on your request.
  • To provide, operate and maintain the Site and its features, including our blogs, case studies and calculators.
  • To understand how visitors use the Site, so we can improve our content, design and Services.
  • To measure and improve the performance of our own marketing and advertising.
  • To send you updates, newsletters or promotional messages, but only if you have agreed to receive them. You can opt out at any time.
  • To keep the Site secure, and to detect and prevent spam, fraud and misuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

b. Our Legal Basis

We process your personal information based on your consent (for example, when you submit a form or message us), to respond to your request, for our legitimate business interests (such as keeping the Site secure and improving it), and to comply with the law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and rules made thereunder.

3. DATA RETENTION

We keep your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including to respond to your enquiry, to maintain our business records, and to meet legal, accounting or reporting requirements.

  • Form enquiries and communications: retained for up to 24 months from our last interaction with you, unless you become a client, in which case information is kept as long as needed to provide Services and meet legal obligations.
  • Cookie and analytics data: retained for the period set by the relevant tool, generally 14 to 26 months.

Once information is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it. You can also ask us to delete your information at any time (see Section 5).

4. INFORMATION SHARING AND DISCLOSURE

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We share information only in the limited situations below.

a. Service Providers

We use trusted third-party providers who help us run the Site and our business. They may access your information only to perform tasks for us and are not allowed to use it for other purposes. These may include:

  • Website hosting and maintenance providers.
  • Form, email and CRM tools used to store and manage enquiries (such as Google Workspace and secure email servers).
  • Analytics and advertising platforms such as Google Analytics, Google Ads, and Meta.
  • Communication platforms such as WhatsApp (operated by Meta), when you choose to contact us there.

b. Legal and Investigative Purposes

We may disclose information if required to do so by law, court order or government authority, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, property or safety, or those of our users or the public, or to prevent fraud or illegal activity.

c. Business Transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will make sure it continues to be protected under this policy or a policy providing similar protection.

d. Aggregated Information

We may share aggregated or anonymised information that does not identify you.

Communication Information WhatsApp (Meta), email providers; legal authorities if required by law No

5. YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS

You have control over your personal information. Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: ask for a summary of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete your personal information, subject to any legal requirements to keep it.
  • Withdrawal of consent: withdraw consent you have given us at any time. This does not affect processing done before you withdrew it.
  • Opt out of marketing: unsubscribe from marketing messages using the link in the message or by contacting us.
  • Grievance redressal: raise an enquiry or concern about how we handle your information (see Section 10).
  • Nominate another person: under Indian law, nominate someone to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

a. For Individuals in India

We handle personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it. You can exercise your rights by contacting us as described in Section 10. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India as provided under the Act.

b. For Individuals in the EEA, United Kingdom or Switzerland

If you are in these regions, you may also have the right to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

c. For Residents of California and Other US States

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising as defined by US state privacy laws. Residents of certain US states may have rights to know, access, correct and delete their information, and we will honour valid requests.

d. Opting Out of Cookies and Targeted Advertising

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. You can also opt out of interest-based advertising through tools such as https://www.aboutads.info/choices/ and the ad settings of the platforms you use. If you opt out, you may still see ads, but they may be less relevant to you.

e. How We Handle Requests

We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. We aim to respond within 30 days. We will not treat you differently for exercising your privacy rights. We may decline requests that are repetitive, unfounded or where the law allows or requires us to retain the data.

6. SECURITY

We take reasonable administrative, technical and physical steps to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. These include HTTPS/SSL encryption across the Site, restricted access to enquiry data, and secure storage with reputable providers.

However, no website or method of electronic transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not send sensitive information, such as passwords or financial account details, through our forms or WhatsApp.

7. CROSS-BORDER DATA TRANSFERS

We are based in India, but some of our service providers (such as analytics, hosting, email and WhatsApp) may store or process data on servers outside India. Where this happens, we take steps to make sure your information remains protected as required by applicable law, including by choosing reputable providers and using contractual safeguards where required.

8. LINKS TO OTHER SITES AND THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Our Site may contain links to websites and services that we do not own or operate, including WhatsApp, social media pages and embedded tools. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those third parties. When you click on a WhatsApp link or any other external link, your information is handled under that provider's own privacy policy. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, and we encourage you to read their policies.

9. CONSENT AND CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

By using our Site and submitting your information through our forms or WhatsApp, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site or submit your information.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version on this page and change the "Last Updated" date at the top. If we make material changes, we will provide notice through the Site or by email where appropriate. Please review this page regularly.

This online Privacy Policy applies only to information collected through our Site and our direct communication channels, and not to information collected offline.
