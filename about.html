Our Vision

PPC Growth Expert was created with a simple vision: to help Amazon sellers grow through smarter, data-driven advertising—not by simply spending more on PPC.

Founded by Anmol Pokhriyal, PPC Growth Expert was built to provide Amazon sellers with specialized PPC expertise, transparent communication, and strategies focused on sustainable growth.

We believe Amazon PPC should be treated as a growth engine, not just an advertising expense. Every keyword, search term, bid, placement, and campaign should have a clear purpose.

Our experience includes managing crore-level monthly Amazon advertising budgets and businesses with ₹100+ crore in total sales managed. This experience has shaped our approach to PPC: analyze the data, identify what is working, eliminate wasted spend, and scale the opportunities that can drive profitable growth.

What We Want to Change

Many Amazon sellers either manage PPC themselves without enough time and expertise or pay high agency fees without receiving the level of attention their account requires.

PPC Growth Expert aims to bridge that gap by providing specialized Amazon PPC management with direct communication, personalized strategies, transparent reporting, and cost-effective expertise.

Our Goal

Our goal is simple: help Amazon sellers spend smarter, grow faster, and build a more profitable advertising system.

Whether you are launching a new product, struggling with high ACOS, trying to improve ROAS, or managing a large-scale Amazon account, our approach is built around one principle:

Don't just spend more. Make every advertising rupee work harder.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is PPC Growth Expert different from a large Amazon agency?

Unlike large agencies where your account is handled by rotating juniors, here Anmol Pokhriyal personally manages your campaigns daily. You get direct WhatsApp access and complete transparency.

What is your minimum engagement period?

We work on a month-to-month basis with no lock-in contracts. We believe in earning your trust every month through results, not binding agreements.

What kind of brands do you work with?

We work with Amazon sellers across all categories — from new launches to established brands doing ₹1Cr+ monthly revenue. We specialize in brands that want aggressive, profitable growth.

How quickly can I expect results?

Most clients see measurable ACoS improvements within the first 2-3 weeks. Significant revenue growth typically happens within 60-90 days of optimized campaign management.

Yes! We offer a complimentary PPC audit where we analyze your current campaigns, identify wasted spend, and provide a detailed optimization roadmap — absolutely free.

Weekly performance reports covering ACoS, TACoS, ROAS, top-performing keywords, search term analysis, spend allocation, and growth recommendations — all shared via WhatsApp and email.
