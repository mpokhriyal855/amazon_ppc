Anmol Pokhriyal

Amazon PPC Specialist

Scaling Amazon Brands with Data-Driven Passion & Precision

Amazon Ads Certified 5+ Years Experience ₹1Cr+ Monthly Ad Spend 5x+ Proven ROAS

With more than 5 years of experience in the Amazon Seller Central, Amazon Vendor Central, Amazon PPC (Sponsored products, sponsored brands, sponsored display) and account management process for full-funnel accounts, Anmol Pokhriyal is an independent amazon PPC expert who helps brands to make their ad spend money work for them.

Anmol has managed more than ₹1 crore ad spends for brands on a monthly basis, driving the revenue of many brands from 0 to ₹9 crores+, with a proven ROAS of 5x and above (providing references upon request). Anmol takes care of your campaigns by focusing on the account structure, keywords research, and constant optimisation of ACOS/TACOS for ongoing improvement.

When you decide to work with Anmol as a freelancer, he takes over your account personally, day in and day out, focusing on your needs only.
