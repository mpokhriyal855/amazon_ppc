How is PPC Growth Expert different from a large Amazon agency?

Unlike large agencies where your account is handled by rotating juniors, here Anmol Pokhriyal personally manages your campaigns daily. You get direct WhatsApp access and complete transparency.

What is your minimum engagement period?

We work on a month-to-month basis with no lock-in contracts. We believe in earning your trust every month through results, not binding agreements.

What kind of brands do you work with?

We work with Amazon sellers across all categories — from new launches to established brands doing ₹1Cr+ monthly revenue. We specialize in brands that want aggressive, profitable growth.

How quickly can I expect results?

Most clients see measurable ACoS improvements within the first 2-3 weeks. Significant revenue growth typically happens within 60-90 days of optimized campaign management.
