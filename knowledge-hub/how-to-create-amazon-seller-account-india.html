Quick Answer · How Do You Create an Amazon Seller Account in India?

To create an Amazon seller account in India, visit the official Amazon India registration portal at sell.amazon.in and click "Start Selling". SECTION 02 · SELLER ELIGIBILITY & LEGAL ENTITIES

1. Who Can Create an Amazon Seller Account in India?

One of the most persistent misconceptions among new sellers is that you must operate a registered Private Limited company with a commercial office to sell on Amazon India. That is completely untrue. Amazon welcomes domestic entrepreneurs across almost every recognized legal constitution in India:

Table 1: Amazon India Seller Entity Eligibility Framework

Business Entity Type Can They Sell on Amazon.in? Primary Tax & Identity Documentation Best Suited For
Sole Proprietorship Yes (Most Common) Proprietor's Personal PAN, GSTIN in firm/trade name, matching business bank account Individual entrepreneurs, local shop owners, home-based sellers, artisans
Individual Seller Yes (Narrow Scope) Personal PAN, Enrolment ID (if selling exempt items like books), personal bank account Authors, hobbyists selling 100% GST-exempt physical books
Partnership Firm Yes Partnership Firm PAN, Registered Partnership Deed, Firm GSTIN, Current Bank Account Family businesses, traditional trading firms, wholesale partnerships

Diverse Seller Profiles on Amazon India

Regardless of your legal constitution, Amazon India accommodates varied commercial profiles:

  • Offline Retailers & Local Shopkeepers: Kirana store owners, electronics retailers, and apparel boutique founders taking their local physical catalog online.
  • Domestic Manufacturers & Industrial Clusters: Producers from manufacturing belts—such as Moradabad brassware, Ludhiana textiles, Surat synthetic fabrics, or Panipat home furnishings—transitioning from B2B wholesale to direct-to-consumer digital commerce.
  • D2C Brands & Private Labels: Emerging consumer brands building trademarked brand equity, custom packaging, and branded product experiences.
  • Home Entrepreneurs & Women Creators: Independent artisans producing handmade jewelry, organic crafts, and customized gifts from domestic home workshops.
SECTION 03 · PREREQUISITES & DOCUMENTATION

2. Documents Required to Create an Amazon Seller Account in India

The single most common reason seller registrations stall or face rejection is entering details that do not match official government tax records character-for-character. Before opening the registration portal, compile the following six verified items:

DOCUMENT 01 · PAN CARD

Permanent Account Number

Personal PAN for sole proprietorships; business entity PAN for Partnerships, LLPs, or Pvt Ltd companies. The name on the PAN must match your bank account and GST registration perfectly.

DOCUMENT 02 · ACTIVE GSTIN

Goods and Services Tax ID

A valid 15-digit GSTIN active on the national GST portal. Must be registered in the state from which your products will be picked up and dispatched.

CREDENTIAL 03 · ACTIVE BANK ACCOUNT

Settlement Bank Details

Bank account number and matching 11-character IFSC code. Must be in the name of the registered entity or proprietor. Keep a recent cancelled cheque or bank statement ready for verification.

CONTACT 04 · PHONE & BUSINESS EMAIL

Active Verification Channels

A dedicated mobile number capable of receiving SMS OTPs and a working business email address. Avoid using personal family emails shared across consumer shopping accounts.

LOCATION 05 · COMMERCIAL PICKUP ADDRESS

Warehouse / Office Pickup Location

The complete physical postal address where Amazon Logistics (ATS) couriers will arrive to collect packaged orders for Easy Ship. Must include building name, street, accurate 6-digit pin code, and contact person details. The state of this pickup address must match the state code of your GSTIN.

Additional Documents Requested for Gated Categories

While open retail categories (such as Apparel, Home Decor, Books, and Electronics Accessories) allow immediate listing upon account approval, certain specialized categories require Category Ungating before products can be activated:

  • Food & Grocery: 14-digit state or central license issued by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI).
  • Cosmetics & Beauty: Manufacturing licenses (Form 32/32A) or authorized brand distribution wholesale tax invoices.
  • Health Supplements & Medical Devices: Drug Controller General of India (DCGI) approvals or ISO certifications.
SECTION 04 · TAX COMPLIANCE & GST RULES

3. Can You Sell on Amazon India Without GST?

This is one of the most widely searched and frequently misunderstood questions in the entire Indian e-commerce landscape. The short, legally grounded answer is: for almost all commercial products, NO—you cannot sell on Amazon India without a regular GST registration.

The Legal Mandate: Section 24 of the CGST Act

In offline retail commerce, Indian businesses with an annual turnover below ₹40 Lakhs (for goods) are generally exempt from mandatory GST registration. However, Section 24(ix) of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act explicitly overrides this threshold for e-commerce. Under Indian law, any person or entity supplying goods through an electronic commerce operator (such as Amazon or Flipkart) is subject to compulsory GST registration, regardless of whether their annual sales are ₹10,000 or ₹10 Crore.

The Narrow Exception: 100% GST-Exempt Goods

Amazon India provides a specific registration pathway for sellers dealing exclusively in goods that carry a zero-rated (0%) GST rate under government notification. The primary verified commercial example of this is:

THE BOOK EXEMPTION PATHWAY

Printed physical books, brochures, maps, and literary publications carry a 0% GST classification. Authors, publishers, and book traders selling only printed books can register on Amazon India without a GSTIN by submitting their PAN and an Enrolment ID generated on the GST portal under the intra-state e-commerce exemption scheme.

Why Sellers Should Not Rely on "No-GST" Loopholes

  • Product Category Lock: If you register under the GST-exempt pathway, Amazon will restrict your account strictly to exempt categories. You cannot later add phone covers, clothing, or kitchen tools without first submitting a regular GSTIN.
  • Input Tax Credit (ITC) Loss: Without a valid GSTIN, you cannot reclaim the 18% GST charged on Amazon referral fees, shipping charges, and advertising expenses as Input Tax Credit, permanently depressing your profit margins.
  • Inter-State Sales Restrictions: Selling across state lines generally requires regular GST registration under Indian tax frameworks.

Official Tax Resource: To apply for a new GST registration or verify HSN code tax slabs, visit the official government GST Portal (gst.gov.in). Always consult a qualified Chartered Accountant (CA) or tax consultant for business-specific tax advice.

SECTION 05 · THE COMPLETE REGISTRATION WALKTHROUGH

4. Step-by-Step Process to Create an Amazon Seller Account in India

Below is the linear, step-by-step operational walkthrough to register an Amazon seller account in India. Follow this sequence exactly as presented in Amazon's current onboarding workflow to avoid verification delays.

STEP 01 OF 11

Visit the Official Amazon India Seller Registration Page

Open your web browser and navigate directly to the verified official Amazon India registration portal: https://sell.amazon.in/sell-online/seller-registration-guide.

On the homepage, locate the prominent orange "Start Selling" button. Be vigilant against unofficial third-party landing pages, paid referral blogs, or unauthorized agents charging registration fees. Amazon’s official registration URL will always begin with sell.amazon.in or sellercentral.amazon.in.

STEP 02 OF 11

Sign In or Create Your Amazon Business Account

You will be presented with a login screen. You have two options:

  • Option A (Recommended for Commercial Brands): Click "Create your Amazon account". Enter your official business name, corporate email address, dedicated mobile number, and a secure password.
  • Option B: Sign in using your existing Amazon customer credentials. While convenient, we strongly advise against using personal family shopping accounts for commercial businesses to avoid password-sharing complications and Single Sign-On (SSO) conflicts across company staff.

Amazon will immediately send an SMS One-Time Password (OTP) to your phone number and a numerical verification code to your email. Enter both codes to verify your identity.

STEP 03 OF 11

Enter Your GST Details or Select the Exempt Option

Next, the onboarding wizard prompts you to submit your tax identification. Enter your 15-digit GSTIN.

If you are registering exclusively to sell 100% GST-exempt items (such as printed physical books), select the radio button stating "I sell only 100% tax-exempt products (e.g. books)". You will then be prompted to enter your PAN and your GST Enrolment ID. For all standard commercial products (clothing, electronics, beauty, toys, home goods), entering a valid regular GSTIN is mandatory.

STEP 04 OF 11

Verify Your GST Information and Legal Business Details

Once you enter your GSTIN, Amazon’s system performs an automated real-time API query against the national GST portal. The system will automatically populate your Legal Entity Name and registered trade address.

Review this information with extreme care. The legal name displayed must match your PAN card and bank account records. If the API cannot find your GSTIN, check whether your registration was approved very recently (allow 24–48 hours for new GST records to populate across government databases) or whether your GST status is marked inactive.

STEP 05 OF 11

Choose Your Amazon Store Name

Your store name (also known as your Display Name or Seller Nickname) is the public identifier shoppers see alongside the "Sold by" attribution on product detail pages.

EFFECTIVE STORE NAMES

"UrbanCraft Naturals", "Apex Retail India", "PureLiving Kitchenware". Clear, memorable, brand-focused, and inspires commercial consumer trust.

CONFUSING STORE NAMES

"Shop_12948_online", "BestDealsCheapPrice", "XYZEnterpriseTrader". Looks amateurish, generic, and raises customer quality concerns.

Note: Selecting a store name does not grant trademark ownership. To protect your brand name legally, you must enroll in Amazon Brand Registry with a registered Indian trademark.

STEP 06 OF 11

Add Your Warehouse Pickup Address

Enter the physical location where your packaged inventory is prepared and stored. If you use Amazon Easy Ship, Amazon Transportation Services (ATS) delivery associates will visit this address daily to scan and collect customer packages.

Critical Tax Rule: The state of your pickup address must match the state code of the GSTIN submitted in Step 3. For example, you cannot enter a Maharashtra GSTIN (state code 27) and list a pickup warehouse located in Noida, Uttar Pradesh (state code 09) without adding Noida as an Additional Place of Business (APOB) on your GST certificate.

STEP 07 OF 11

Choose Your Shipping and Fulfilment Method

Amazon India provides three primary fulfillment pathways. Understanding the operational responsibilities of each is essential for choosing the right model for your business:

Table 2: Fulfilment Models Comparison (FBA vs. Easy Ship vs. Self-Ship)

Fulfilment Method Who Stores Stock? Who Packs Orders? Who Handles Doorstep Delivery? Prime Badge Status Best Fit For
Amazon Easy Ship Seller (Own facility) Seller (Amazon polybags) Amazon Logistics (ATS courier pickup) Standard / Regional Prime only Most beginners, local retailers, home-based businesses
Fulfilled by Amazon (FBA) Amazon FC Warehouses Amazon warehouse team Amazon Logistics (ATS) Guaranteed 100% Prime badge nationwide Fast-selling consumer goods, scalable D2C brands
Self-Ship (Merchant Fulfilled) Seller (Own warehouse) Seller (Private packaging) Third-party couriers (Delhivery, BlueDart) No Prime badge Fragile, hazardous, custom-built, or heavy goods

Consultant Recommendation: Almost all first-time Indian sellers should launch via Amazon Easy Ship. It avoids the upfront complexity of inbounding pallets into Amazon fulfillment centers while allowing Amazon's logistics network to manage doorstep delivery, transit tracking, and Cash on Delivery (COD) cash collections.

STEP 08 OF 11

Add Your Bank Account Details

To receive your marketplace earnings, you must link an active commercial bank account. Enter:

  • Account Holder Name: Must match your registered business legal name or proprietor PAN name exactly.
  • Bank Account Number: Current account (for companies) or savings account (for individual sole proprietorships).
  • IFSC Code: 11-character Indian Financial System Code of your bank branch.

Amazon utilizes this account for regular bi-weekly NEFT direct bank settlement disbursements. Ensure there are no typographical errors in your account number.

STEP 09 OF 11

Complete Tax Information and Product Tax Codes (PTC)

Amazon requires you to set a default Product Tax Code (PTC). This determines the GST percentage automatically calculated on customer checkout invoices (e.g. 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, or 28% GST).

Select the tax code that aligns with the primary Harmonized System of Nomenclature (HSN) code of your product catalog. If your catalog includes items across multiple tax slabs, you can override this default tax rate at the individual SKU listing level later.

STEP 10 OF 11

Create Your First Product Listing

Completing seller registration is not the same as having an active store. Your account goes live once you create at least one product listing:

  • Product Title: Clear, descriptive title incorporating primary search keywords (e.g. "Organic Herbal Neem Face Wash (150ml) for Acne & Oil Control").
  • Category Sub-Node: Select the precise category so shoppers can find your item via navigation filters.
  • Product Photography: At least one high-resolution main image on a 100% pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255), with the product filling 85%+ of the frame.
  • Selling Price & MRP: Ensure your selling price is inclusive of GST.
  • Stock Quantity: Enter the initial physical units available at your warehouse.
  • Product Barcode / GTIN: If your product does not carry a physical barcode (EAN/UPC), apply for a free GTIN Exemption in Seller Central.
STEP 11 OF 11

Review Your Details and Launch Your Store

The final screen presents a summary of your submitted store details: tax status, pickup location, fulfillment preference, bank details, and initial product draft.

Verify every field. Once satisfied, click "Launch Your Store". Amazon’s automated verification engine will process your submission. If your details require manual compliance review (e.g. FSSAI license verification or address document verification), your account dashboard will indicate "Verification in Progress" until approval is granted.

SECTION 06 · COMMERCIAL FEES & ECONOMICS

5. How Much Does It Cost to Create an Amazon Seller Account in India?

Let us dispel another widespread myth: it costs ₹0 to create an Amazon seller account in India.

Unlike international Amazon marketplaces (such as the United States, where a Professional Selling Plan costs $39.99 per month), Amazon India does not charge a mandatory monthly subscription fee to maintain an active seller account. Amazon operates on a pure pay-as-you-sell commercial structure. You only incur fees when a customer purchases your product.

The 4 Core Deductions on Every Completed Sale

  • Referral Fee: Category commission calculated as a percentage of the total sales price paid by the customer. This ranges from 2% (consumer electronics) to 18%–22% (fashion, jewelry, mobile accessories).
  • Closing Fee: A flat fixed fee per unit based on price tiers: ₹0–250, ₹251–500, ₹501–1000, and ₹1000+.
  • Weight Handling (Shipping) Fee: Distance-tiered delivery fee (Local, Regional, National) based on package volumetric gross weight under Easy Ship or FBA.
  • 18% GST on All Amazon Service Fees: Under Indian tax law, the central government levies 18% GST on all marketplace service charges (referral, shipping, and ad fees). Registered GST sellers can reclaim this as Input Tax Credit (ITC).

Official Fee Documentation: For the latest category fee schedule, review official fee tables at Amazon India Seller Pricing, or model your exact take-home profit using our Amazon Net Profit Calculator.

SECTION 07 · TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

6. Do You Need a Website to Sell on Amazon India?

No, you do not need an external website, e-commerce store, or domain name to sell on Amazon India.

Amazon is a completely self-contained retail ecosystem. When you list your products on Amazon, the marketplace automatically hosts your product detail pages, processes customer payments through its secure gateway, manages SSL security encryption, coordinates logistics pickups, and tracks customer delivery notifications.

When Does Having a Website Become Useful?

While a website is unnecessary for registration and daily operations, establishing a simple standalone brand website can provide secondary commercial benefits as your brand expands:

  • Trademark & Brand Registry Verification: Demonstrating active commercial brand presence during trademark applications.
  • Brand Building & Storytelling: Providing institutional credibility for wholesale B2B partners and retail distributors.
  • Multi-Channel Diversification: Ensuring your business maintains direct customer touchpoints alongside marketplace channels.

Always resolve issues through the original registration ticket or contact official Amazon Seller Support.

SECTION 09 · POST-LAUNCH PLAYBOOK

8. What Should You Do After Creating an Amazon Seller Account?

Receiving account approval is the start of your journey, not the finish line. To transform your newly registered account into a thriving commercial business, execute these eight foundational post-registration tasks:

TASK 01 · COMPLETE PROFILE

Configure Business Settings

Navigate to Settings > Account Info. Upload your official business logo, configure customer return policies, and set up secondary user permissions for staff or agency consultants.

TASK 02 · HIGH-CONVERTING LISTINGS

Visual & SEO Merchandising

Do not settle for basic listings. Upgrade with 6 high-resolution infographics, dimensional diagrams, benefit-driven bullets, and backend search terms under 250 bytes.

TASK 03 · UNIT ECONOMICS AUDIT

Calculate Net Profit Margins

Calculate referral fees, closing fees, packaging, and return allowances. Ensure your pre-ad gross profit margin is at least 25% to 35% to support paid customer acquisition.

TASK 04 · INVENTORY BUFFERING

Prevent Early Stockouts

Stockouts permanently destroy initial organic ranking momentum. Establish safety stock buffer levels based on supplier reorder lead times.

TASK 05 · TARGETED SPONSORED ADS

Kickstart Sales Velocity

Launch isolated Sponsored Products campaigns on exact-match buyer search terms. Maintain healthy Total Advertising Cost of Sales (TACoS) between 8% and 14%.

TASK 06 · COMPLIANT REVIEWS

Build Early Social Proof

Automate official "Request a Review" requests via Seller Central API or enroll in Amazon Vine. Never purchase fake reviews or insert cards offering cash rewards.

SECTION 10 · PITFALLS & RISK MANAGEMENT

9. Common Mistakes New Amazon Sellers Should Avoid

Over five years of consulting for domestic Amazon sellers, we consistently see new merchants make the same costly mistakes:

MISTAKE 01

Starting Registration Before Documents Are Ready

Halting onboarding midway to apply for a GSTIN or open a bank account, causing session timeouts and automated verification holds.

MISTAKE 02

Pickup Address / GST State Mismatch

Listing a warehouse pickup location in a state different from the registered GST state code without adding an APOB.

MISTAKE 03

Low-Grade Smartphone Photography

Uploading dim photos with household backgrounds instead of crisp 3D renders or clean pure-white background images.

MISTAKE 04

Operating on Negative Net Margins

Pricing products without calculating referral fees, closing fees, 18% GST on fees, and customer return allowances.

MISTAKE 05

Letting Hero ASINs Stock Out

Allowing top-selling products to remain out of stock for weeks, permanently destroying accumulated organic search rank.

MISTAKE 06

Running "Set-and-Forget" PPC Ads

Enabling broad and auto ad campaigns without weekly negative keyword harvesting, bleeding marketing capital on waste clicks.

MISTAKE 07

Over-Relying on Self-Ship

Avoiding Easy Ship or FBA to save minor fees, thereby sacrificing the Prime badge and cutting organic conversion rates in half.

MISTAKE 08

Engaging in Price Wars

Slashing prices below cost to win the Buy Box against competitors with deeper pockets or lower manufacturing costs.

MISTAKE 09

Ignoring Account Health Alerts

Treating performance notifications as spam until a suspension notice arrives requiring an urgent Plan of Action appeal.

MISTAKE 10

Using Review Manipulation Inserts

Placing cards inside boxes offering ₹50 cashback for 5-star reviews, triggering automated algorithmic account bans.

MISTAKE 11

Neglecting Customer Returns (VOC)

Failing to review Voice of Customer return feedback, allowing preventable sizing or packaging defects to destroy review averages.

MISTAKE 12

Lacking Peak Festive Preparation

Waiting until two weeks before the Great Indian Festival to prepare stock, getting blocked by congested inbound appointment windows.

SECTION 11 · ONBOARDING TIMELINES

The time required to complete registration and see your products live on Amazon India depends heavily on your document readiness and product category:

SCENARIO A · RAPID APPROVAL (24 TO 48 HOURS)

Clean Automated Verification

Your PAN, GSTIN, and bank account match character-for-character. You sell in an open category (Apparel, Home, Books). The government API verifies your records instantly, and your first listing activates within 24 to 48 hours.

SCENARIO B · MANUAL COMPLIANCE (3 TO 5 DAYS)

Category Ungating & Document Review

Your product is in a gated vertical (Grocery/FSSAI, Health Supplements, Beauty), or Amazon requests secondary address verification (electricity bill or commercial lease). Human compliance specialists review submissions within 3 to 5 business days.

Practitioner Advice: Avoid changing submitted information repeatedly while verification is marked "Pending", as each modification resets the compliance review queue.

SECTION 13 · SPECIALIST MARKETPLACE SUPPORT

13. Frequently Asked Questions About Creating an Amazon Seller Account

1. How can I create an Amazon seller account in India? +

To create an Amazon seller account in India, visit sell.amazon.in, click 'Start Selling', log in or create a business Amazon account, submit your PAN and active GSTIN, choose your store name, add your warehouse pickup address, select a fulfillment method (FBA, Easy Ship, or Self-Ship), enter verified bank details, and create your first product listing.

2. Is it free to create an Amazon seller account in India? +

Yes, creating an Amazon seller account in India is completely free. There are no upfront registration fees or monthly subscription fees for standard sellers. Amazon operates on a pay-as-you-sell model, charging a Referral Fee (percentage of sale price), Closing Fee (flat fee per price tier), and fulfillment/shipping fees only when an item sells.

3. What documents are required to register as an Amazon seller? +

The mandatory documents include: 1) Active Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN); 2) Permanent Account Number (PAN) matching your business entity or personal name; 3) Active bank account in the entity name with IFSC and proof (cancelled cheque or statement); 4) Dedicated mobile number and business email address; and 5) Commercial pickup address.

4. Can I sell on Amazon India without GST? +

Under Section 24 of the CGST Act, e-commerce sellers are generally required to possess mandatory GST registration. The only narrow exception is if you sell exclusively 100% GST-exempt goods, such as unbranded physical books. If you plan to sell any taxable product, a valid regular GSTIN is mandatory.

5. Can an individual start selling on Amazon? +

Yes. An individual can register as a sole proprietorship using their personal PAN and a registered GSTIN issued under their trade name. You do not need to incorporate a Private Limited company to start selling on Amazon India.

6. Can I sell on Amazon without a website? +

Yes. You do not need an external website or online store to sell on Amazon. Amazon provides the complete storefront, product detail pages, checkout payment gateway, and customer order management infrastructure via Amazon Seller Central.

7. Can I use my personal bank account for Amazon seller registration? +

For sole proprietors selling under an individual PAN, a personal savings bank account is accepted if the account holder name matches the PAN and GST registration. However, for Partnerships, LLPs, and Private Limited companies, a dedicated business current account in the registered entity name is strictly mandatory.

8. How long does Amazon seller account verification take? +

If your PAN, GSTIN, and bank account information match perfectly and pass automated verification, account activation can occur within a few hours to 24–48 hours. If manual document verification or category ungating (e.g., FSSAI for food) is required, it may take 2 to 5 business days.

9. Can I sell products from my home? +

Yes, many Indian sellers operate home-based businesses. You can list your residential address as your registered business address and pickup location, provided it matches your GST registration certificate and is serviceable by Amazon Logistics (ATS) for Easy Ship courier pickups.

10. Can a small business or sole proprietorship sell on Amazon? +

Absolutely. Sole proprietorships, MSMEs, local retailers, and home entrepreneurs make up a substantial portion of the 20+ lakh registered sellers on Amazon India. The registration process is identical, requiring proprietor PAN, GSTIN, and bank details.

11. How do I list my first product on Amazon? +

After logging into Seller Central, go to Catalogue > Add Products. If your product is unique, click 'I'm adding a product not sold on Amazon', enter the product title, select the exact category, upload high-resolution images on pure white backgrounds, add bullet points, set price and inventory quantity, and submit for launch.

12. What should I do if my GST verification fails? +

First, verify that your legal trade name, PAN, and address on the GST portal (gst.gov.in) match your Seller Central inputs character-for-character. Ensure your GST status is active and not pending. If the issue persists, upload a clear PDF copy of your GST registration certificate (Form REG-06) for manual review by Amazon Seller Support.

13. Can I sell without having my own brand or trademark? +

Yes. You can sell unbranded or generic products by applying for a 'Generic' brand approval or GTIN Exemption, or you can resell existing branded products if you are an authorized distributor with commercial brand invoices.

14. How do I receive payments from Amazon? +

Amazon disburses payments directly into your registered bank account via National Electronic Funds Transfer (NEFT) on a standard 14-day bi-weekly settlement cycle, after deducting applicable marketplace referral fees, shipping charges, and a temporary reserve for customer returns.

15. What should I do after creating my Amazon seller account? +

Immediately complete your Seller Central profile, optimize product detail pages with keyword-rich titles and infographics, calculate product profitability using fee calculators, maintain stock buffers to avoid stockouts, enable automated compliant review requests, and test initial Sponsored Products PPC campaigns.
