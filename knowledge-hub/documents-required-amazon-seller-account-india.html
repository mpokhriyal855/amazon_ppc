Quick Answer · What Documents Do You Need Before Registering on Amazon India?

To register as an Amazon seller in India, you need four core items ready at hand: 1) Valid PAN Card (personal PAN for individual/sole proprietors; entity PAN for companies, LLPs, or partnerships); 2) Active GSTIN (legally mandatory under Section 24 of the CGST Act for all taxable goods; exempt goods like unbranded physical books are the sole exception); 3) Active Indian Bank Account with valid IFSC code (savings account permitted for proprietors; current account required for incorporated firms); and 4) Active Mobile Number and Working Email for OTP and 2FA authentication. Additional documents like Certificate of Incorporation, FSSAI licenses, or brand authorizations apply conditionally based on your legal entity and product category.

SECTION 01 · EXECUTIVE AUDIT CHECKLIST

1. Quick Checklist: Documents and Details Required for Amazon Seller Registration

Starting an online business on Amazon India represents one of the fastest ways to reach over 99% of India's serviceable pin codes. However, thousands of aspiring sellers experience delayed approvals, rejected KYC verifications, or temporary account freezes simply because they attempt registration without having their foundational documents properly aligned.

The registration process on the official Amazon India portal (sell.amazon.in) is 100% digital and paperless. In most initial registration steps, you do not physically upload heavy bundles of paper; rather, you input verified government tax identification numbers and banking coordinates that Amazon validates in real time via automated API handshakes with the Income Tax Department and the Goods and Services Tax Network (GSTN).

To ensure your registration proceeds smoothly in under 15 minutes, assemble the core credentials summarized in the master checklist below before you begin:

You only need to upload scanned PDF copies or image files if Amazon's automated verification systems flag a character mismatch, or if you apply for category approval (such as an FSSAI certificate for food products). Preparing digital PDF scans in advance guarantees you will not be caught off guard.

SECTION 02 · TAX IDENTITY VERIFICATION

2. PAN Card: Why Is PAN Required for Amazon Seller Registration?

The Permanent Account Number (PAN), issued by the Indian Income Tax Department under Section 139A of the Income Tax Act, 1961, serves as the fundamental legal anchor of your commercial identity on Amazon.in. You cannot create an active seller account under any circumstances without providing a valid 10-character alphanumeric PAN.

Why Amazon Asks for PAN Details

Amazon requires your PAN for three interconnected statutory and operational reasons:

  • Legal Entity Identification: Verifies that the individual or corporate entity registering the account is a legitimate, recognized legal person under Indian jurisprudence.
  • Section 194-O Income Tax Compliance: Under Indian tax law, e-commerce operators like Amazon are legally mandated to deduct Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) at 1% (or applicable notified rates) on the gross amount of sales made through the marketplace. This TDS is deposited directly with the Government of India against your specific PAN, allowing you to claim credit when filing your annual income tax returns.
  • Cross-Verification with GSTN: Your 15-digit Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN) contains your 10-digit PAN within digits 3 through 12. Amazon's verification algorithms cross-reference these two databases to ensure tax alignment.

Proprietorship PAN vs. Corporate Entity PAN

A common point of confusion among first-time entrepreneurs is whether they need to apply for a "business PAN" before selling. The answer depends strictly on your legal entity structure:

Business Structure 4th Character of PAN Operational Rule on Amazon India
Sole Proprietorship / Individual P (Person) Use the proprietor's personal PAN. A sole proprietorship is not a distinct legal entity from its owner; your business does not have a separate PAN.
Partnership Firm F (Firm) Must use the firm's dedicated PAN card issued in the partnership firm's registered name. Do not submit an individual partner's personal PAN.
Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) F or L Must use the LLP's dedicated PAN card issued by the Income Tax Department following incorporation with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA).
Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd) C (Company) Must use the corporate PAN card issued in the company's incorporated legal name. Do not enter the Managing Director's personal PAN.
NAME MISMATCH HAZARD

Exact Character Alignment: When entering your legal business name on Amazon Seller Central, it must match the official name registered in the Income Tax database character-for-character. If your PAN card reads "Rajesh Kumar Sharma", entering "R. K. Sharma" or your brand trade name will trigger an automated API verification failure. To verify your exact legal spelling, check your profile on the official Income Tax Department Portal.

SECTION 03 · STATUTORY TAX REGULATION

3. GSTIN: Is GST Registration Mandatory to Sell on Amazon India?

The question most frequently asked by new entrepreneurs is: "Can I start selling on Amazon India without a GST number?"

The short, definitive answer is: If you sell taxable commercial goods, GST registration is legally mandatory from rupee one.

The Legal Reality: Section 24 of the CGST Act

Under traditional offline brick-and-mortar retail in India, small traders enjoy an exemption threshold—they are generally not required to register under the Goods and Services Tax (GST) regime until their aggregate annual turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh (for goods in standard states) or ₹20 lakh (in special category states).

However, this turnover threshold does not apply to e-commerce marketplace sellers. Under Section 24(ix) of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017, persons who supply goods or services through an electronic commerce operator (such as Amazon or Flipkart) are subject to compulsory registration, irrespective of whether their annual turnover is ₹10,000 or ₹10 crore.

The Exception: Selling Eligible 100% GST-Exempt Products

Amazon India provides a specific onboarding workflow that permits sellers to register without a GSTIN only if they deal exclusively in products that carry a 0% GST rate under Indian tax classification.

The primary commercial category qualifying for this exemption is unbranded physical books (including literary, educational, and printed texts), along with certain raw, unbranded agricultural products, fresh fruits, and handloom items. If you choose this path:

  • You must provide your personal PAN and declare during onboarding that you are selling strictly GST-exempt products.
  • Amazon restricts your product catalog: you cannot add any taxable item (for instance, you cannot bundle a pen, bookmark, or notebook with a book, as stationery is taxable).
  • If you ever decide to expand your product catalog into taxable merchandise (such as apparel, electronics, toys, or kitchenware), you must immediately obtain a GSTIN and update your Seller Central tax profile before publishing those listings.
Seller Situation Statutory GST Requirement on Amazon India
Selling standard consumer goods (Apparel, electronics, cosmetics, home decor, toys, packaged foods) Mandatory GSTIN required. You cannot list taxable goods without entering a verified, active 15-digit GSTIN during registration.
Selling exclusively eligible GST-exempt goods (Printed books, unbranded educational maps, specific raw agricultural goods) Exempt from GSTIN. You may register using your PAN only. You must maintain 100% catalog compliance with GST exemption lists.
Planning to sell both taxable and exempt products Mandatory GSTIN required. The presence of even one taxable SKU in your catalog brings your entire business under compulsory GST registration.
Already registered under GST for an offline business Input existing GSTIN. Simply submit your current 15-digit GSTIN. Ensure your pickup warehouse address is registered as a Principal or Additional Place of Business.

Section 52: Tax Collected at Source (TCS)

In addition to sales tax compliance, Amazon is legally obligated under Section 52 of the CGST Act to collect Tax Collected at Source (TCS) at the rate of 1% (0.5% CGST + 0.5% SGST, or 1% IGST) on the net value of taxable supplies made through the platform. Amazon deposits this TCS with the government under your GSTIN every month. You can easily view and claim this amount as an input tax credit against your monthly GST liability on the Official GST Portal.

SECTION 04 · COMMUNICATION & OPERATIONAL SECURITY

4. Mobile Number and Email Address: Dedicated Setup Best Practices

While submitting an active phone number and an email address may appear trivial compared to tax registrations, these two coordinates form the foundational security infrastructure of your Amazon seller operations.

Why Amazon Requires Dedicated, Active Contact Details

  • Real-Time OTP Verification: Every critical transaction—including password resets, updating bank account coordinates, adding user permissions, and signing courier manifests—triggers a one-time password (OTP) via SMS.
  • Mandatory 2-Step Verification (2FA): Amazon Seller Central enforces multi-factor authentication. You will enter your password plus an SMS code (or authenticator app token) each time you log in from an unrecognized browser or device.
  • Critical Performance & Policy Notifications: If a customer files an A-to-z Guarantee claim, if an intellectual property infringement notice is submitted, or if your Late Dispatch Rate (LDR) approaches suspension thresholds, Amazon sends immediate notifications via email. Missing an email notification because it went to an inactive inbox can result in account suspension within 48 to 72 hours.
  • Logistics Coordination: Under Amazon Easy Ship, delivery associates frequently contact the designated pickup phone number when arriving at your premises to collect outgoing parcels.
PRO STRATEGY: SEPARATE CONSUMER & SELLER CREDENTIALS

Do Not Use Your Personal Shopping Email: Many beginners make the mistake of registering their Amazon Seller Central account using the same personal Gmail address they have used for years to order personal items on Amazon.in. If your consumer account ever experiences billing disputes, compromised credentials, or gift card blocks, your entire seller business access can be jeopardized. Always create a dedicated business email address (such as seller@yourbrand.com or orders.yourbrand@gmail.com) and an operational SIM card dedicated exclusively to your e-commerce operations.

SECTION 05 · FINANCIAL SETTLEMENT INFRASTRUCTURE

5. Bank Account Details Required for Amazon Seller Registration

Amazon is an active commercial marketplace, not an escrow wallet. Every sale made on Amazon.in culminates in a direct electronic funds transfer into your verified domestic bank account. Under Amazon India’s standard disbursement schedule, net proceeds (customer payments minus marketplace referral fees, logistics charges, and a temporary reserve for customer returns) are disbursed on a 14-day bi-weekly settlement cycle via National Electronic Funds Transfer (NEFT) or RTGS.

Core Banking Fields Required During Registration

When navigating the banking configuration screen in Seller Central, you must provide three precise data points:

  • Account Holder's Legal Name: The exact name registered with your commercial bank branch.
  • Bank Account Number: Your complete 9-to-18-digit account number (re-entered twice to prevent typographical errors).
  • IFSC Code (Indian Financial System Code): The exact 11-character alphanumeric code identifying your specific bank and branch location (e.g., HDFC0001234, SBIN0005678).

Automated Penny-Drop Verification

To eliminate banking fraud and prevent accidental typos that could misroute seller disbursements, Amazon India employs an automated Penny-Drop Verification system powered by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) and Instant Money Payment System (IMPS).

Within minutes of you entering your bank account coordinates, Amazon's payment gateway initiates a micro-deposit of ₹1.00 into your account. The automated system instantly cross-references the account holder's name returned by your bank's Core Banking Solution (CBS) against the legal entity name entered on your PAN card and GST certificate. If the names align, your bank account status turns to "Active / Verified" immediately.

Can You Use a Savings Account, or Is a Current Account Mandatory?

This is another major misconception among new sellers. Many believe they must spend weeks opening an expensive commercial current account before they can even register on Amazon. The operational reality depends on your business structure:

Account Type Permitted on Amazon? Operational Best Practices & Legal Constraints
Personal Savings Account YES (For Sole Proprietors & Individuals) Permitted for individual sellers and sole proprietorships, provided the account holder name strictly matches the proprietor's PAN card. Suitable for testing early demand and initial launch phases.
Commercial Current Account MANDATORY (For LLPs & Companies)
STRONGLY RECOMMENDED (For Proprietors)		 Strictly mandatory for incorporated entities (Partnerships, LLPs, Private Limited Companies) in the registered firm's name. For sole proprietors, opening a current account under your business trade name prevents personal and business fund commingling and simplifies GST filing and income tax audits.

Updating Bank Details & The 3-Day Security Hold

You can update or change your bank account at any time via Seller Central by navigating to Settings > Account Info > Bank Account Details. However, to protect sellers from unauthorized account takeovers or phishing attacks, Amazon enforces a strict 3-day mandatory security hold on all funds disbursements whenever banking coordinates are modified. Any disbursement scheduled during those 3 days will be paused until the security window lapses. For complete official guidelines, review the Amazon Seller Central Bank Account Reference (G851).

SECTION 06 · GEOGRAPHIC & LOGISTICS ALIGNMENT

6. Business Address and Pickup Address Documents

During registration, Amazon prompts you for two distinct addresses: your Registered Business Address and your Pickup Address. While these two locations can be identical, they serve entirely different legal and operational functions.

Understanding the Three Tiers of Commercial Addresses

  • Principal Place of Business (PPoB): The primary legal registered address specified on your GST registration certificate (Form GST REG-06). This is the address where your official financial records, statutory registers, and tax communications reside.
  • Additional Place of Business (APoB): Any secondary commercial location—such as a storage warehouse, manufacturing unit, branch office, or an Amazon Fulfilment Centre (FBA warehouse)—that is legally endorsed on your GST certificate.
  • Pickup Address (Fulfillment Location): The physical street address where Amazon Transportation Services (ATS) courier vans arrive to pick up customer-bound packages under Amazon Easy Ship.
CRITICAL GSTIN & PICKUP STATE RULE

The State Match Requirement: In India, the first two digits of your 15-digit GSTIN represent your state code (e.g., 07 for Delhi, 09 for Uttar Pradesh, 27 for Maharashtra). Your Amazon Easy Ship pickup address must reside in the exact same state as your GSTIN. For example, if your GST registration is based in Noida, Uttar Pradesh (State Code 09), you cannot specify a pickup warehouse located in Delhi (State Code 07). If you ship inventory from multiple states, you must register a separate GSTIN for each operating state.

Supporting Address Proof Documents

While your address details are initially populated from your GSTIN database profile, Amazon Seller Support or the automated Risk Investigation Team may request supporting documentation during onboarding or Video KYC. Acceptable documents include:

  • Electricity or Utility Bill: Must be issued within the last 60 days, clearly displaying the applicant's or entity's name and full street address.
  • Registered Rent / Lease Agreement: Formally executed rent deed accompanied by a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the property owner and the owner's latest utility bill.
  • Municipal Property Tax Receipt: Recent paid property tax assessment document or municipal tax challan.
  • Bank Account Statement: Official bank statement issued within the last 90 days, bearing the entity's complete name and address, verified by a physical branch stamp or digital bank signature.
SECTION 07 · GOVERNMENT TAX COMPLIANCE

7. Additional Documents Required for GST Registration

It is critical to distinguish between Amazon seller registration (which takes 15 minutes online) and GST registration (which is a sovereign government process administered on gst.gov.in).

Amazon does not issue your GST number; you must obtain your GST Registration Certificate (Form GST REG-06) from the government first, and then input your 15-digit GSTIN into Amazon Seller Central.

The documents required to secure a GST registration vary according to your legal business constitution:

Required Document Why the GST Portal Requires It Applicable Entity Structure
PAN Card of Applicant / Entity Primary identification anchor; forms digits 3–12 of the resulting GSTIN. All Business Structures (Personal PAN for Proprietorship; Firm/Company PAN for others)
Aadhaar Card of Signatory Mandatory for biometric Aadhaar authentication to eliminate physical site inspections. Proprietor, all partners, designated partners, or authorized managing directors
Passport-Size Photographs Visual identity verification of the legal applicant or authorized representatives. Proprietor, partners, designated partners, or managing directors
Bank Account Proof Verification of business financial channel; cancelled cheque, bank passbook front page, or bank statement. All Business Structures
Principal Address Proof Electricity bill, legal ownership deed, or registered rent agreement with owner NOC. All Business Structures
Partnership Deed Legal constitution defining profit-sharing, partner liabilities, and signing authorities. Partnership Firms & Limited Liability Partnerships (LLP)
Certificate of Incorporation (COI) Statutory proof of corporate existence issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA). LLPs, Private Limited Companies, and Public Limited Companies
Board Resolution / Authorization Letter Formal declaration designating the specific individual authorized to sign GST returns. Partnership firms, LLPs, and corporate companies

Once approved on the GST portal (typically within 3 to 7 working days following successful Aadhaar authentication), download your official Form GST REG-06. This 3-page certificate contains your 15-digit GSTIN, legal name, trade name, and registered business address, all of which will be entered during Amazon Seller Central setup.

SECTION 08 · ENTITY-SPECIFIC ONBOARDING MATRIX

8. Documents Required for Different Types of Amazon Sellers

Amazon accommodates sellers across the entire spectrum of Indian commerce—from solo home bakers and artisan handicraft makers to multinational consumer packaged goods (CPG) enterprises. However, the legal documentation you must present corresponds directly to your registered business entity.

A. Individual Seller (Narrow Applicability)

An Individual account is intended for solo persons who are selling eligible 100% GST-exempt products (e.g., physical books or unbranded handicraft art) without a registered trade name:

  • Identity Proof: Personal PAN card of the individual.
  • Contact: Personal active mobile number and email ID.
  • Banking: Personal savings bank account in the applicant's exact PAN name with valid IFSC code.
  • Logistics: Residential pickup address serviceable by Amazon ATS.

B. Sole Proprietorship (Most Common Model)

Over 75% of domestic e-commerce sellers in India operate as Sole Proprietorships. It offers the lowest compliance burden while providing full commercial authorization to sell taxable goods:

  • Tax Identity: Personal PAN card of the sole proprietor (proprietorships do not possess separate PAN cards).
  • GST Registration: GSTIN issued in the proprietor’s legal name, with the commercial brand or firm listed as the Trade Name.
  • Banking: Bank account in the proprietor's name (savings permitted) or commercial current account in the registered Trade Name.
  • MSME Recognition (Optional): Udyam Registration certificate (udyamregistration.gov.in) establishing enterprise credibility.

C. Partnership Firm

Formed when two or more individuals co-own and operate a commercial venture under the Indian Partnership Act, 1932:

  • Entity PAN: Dedicated PAN card issued in the registered name of the Partnership Firm (4th character F).
  • Partnership Deed: Formally executed and notarized partnership agreement.
  • GSTIN: GST certificate registered in the partnership firm's legal name.
  • Authorized Signatory: Authorization letter signed by all partners designating the managing partner to operate the Amazon account.
  • Banking: Commercial current bank account opened in the exact name of the partnership firm.

D. Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)

A hybrid corporate structure registered under the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008, providing operational flexibility alongside limited personal liability:

  • Entity PAN: Dedicated PAN card of the LLP.
  • Incorporation Proof: Certificate of Incorporation issued by the Registrar of Companies (ROC) / Ministry of Corporate Affairs.
  • LLP Agreement: Registered LLP Agreement outlining designated partners' powers.
  • Designated Partner KYC: PAN and Aadhaar of the designated partner operating Seller Central.
  • GSTIN & Banking: GST certificate and commercial current bank account in the LLP's incorporated name.

E. Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd)

The preferred vehicle for high-growth e-commerce ventures, D2C venture-backed brands, and established manufacturing enterprises:

  • Corporate PAN: Dedicated corporate PAN card of the company (4th character C).
  • Incorporation Documents: Certificate of Incorporation (CIN), Memorandum of Association (MOA), and Articles of Association (AOA).
  • Board Resolution: Formally passed Board Resolution on company letterhead authorizing a specific director or manager to operate the Amazon Seller Central account.
  • Director Identification: Director Identification Number (DIN), PAN, and Aadhaar of the authorized operating director.
  • Corporate Banking: Corporate current account in the company's full legal name with cancelled commercial cheque.
  • Corporate GSTIN: Active GST certificate in the company's incorporated entity name.
SECTION 09 · REGULATORY COMPLIANCE & UNGATING

9. Product Category-Specific Documents and Approvals

A critical realization for new sellers is that completing Amazon seller registration does not automatically grant unrestricted permission to sell every product cataloged on Amazon.in.

To safeguard customer health, safety, and brand integrity, Amazon India enforces strict "gating" mechanisms on specific regulated merchandise categories. If you plan to sell products within these verticals, you must submit specific regulatory approvals, commercial licenses, or manufacturer invoices before your listings can go live.

Product Category Mandatory Regulatory License Required Verification Documents
Packaged Food, Grocery & Health Supplements FSSAI License / Registration (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India) Valid 14-digit FSSAI license certificate in the applicant's legal name, clear product packaging photos showing the FSSAI logo, nutritional panel, and manufacturer details.
Electronics, IT Hardware & Power Banks BIS CRS Registration (Bureau of Indian Standards) Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS) test reports, R-number verification from BIS labs, and packaging bearing the official standard mark.
Children's Toys (Electric & Non-Electric) BIS ISI Certification (Safety of Toys standard IS 9873 / IS 15644) BIS valid manufacturing license (CM/L number), third-party lab safety test certificates, and physical packaging displaying the ISI mark.
Cosmetics, Skincare & Ayurvedic Items State Drug Control License / Ayush License Cosmetic manufacturing or wholesale drug license (Form 20B/21B), FDA/Ayush certification for proprietary Ayurvedic formulations.
Reselling Branded Consumer Goods Brand Authorization & Purchase Proof Commercial tax invoices from the manufacturer or authorized distributor (dated within 180 days, displaying seller's name and address) or an official Brand Authorization Letter.
Private Label / Custom Brand Trademark Registry (IP India) Trademark application acknowledgement (TM) or registered trademark certificate (®) to qualify for Amazon Brand Registry and GTIN Exemption.
PRE-ORDER INVENTORY RULE

Verify Before You Purchase: Never order thousands of units of inventory from a factory or wholesale market until you have verified category approval inside Seller Central. You can test category requirements by clicking Catalogue > Add Products in your dashboard and searching for similar ASINs to see if an "Apply to Sell" button appears.

SECTION 10 · LEGAL STRUCTURE MYTHBUSTING

10. Do You Need a Company Registration Certificate to Sell on Amazon?

One of the most pervasive myths circulating among aspiring Indian e-commerce entrepreneurs is that you must hire a lawyer, spend ₹15,000 to ₹25,000, and incorporate a Private Limited Company or LLP before you can sell on Amazon India.

The definitive answer is an emphatic NO.

Amazon India has zero requirement mandating that sellers operate as incorporated corporate entities. A solo entrepreneur working from home, a neighborhood retail shopkeeper, or an artisan can register and sell legally on Amazon using a simple Sole Proprietorship.

Sole Proprietorship vs. Private Limited Company

To understand which path makes financial and operational sense for your stage of growth, review this strategic comparison:

Evaluation Factor Sole Proprietorship Private Limited Company
Formation Cost ₹0 to ₹1,500 (Free via GST portal / Udyam) ₹10,000 to ₹25,000 (ROC fees, digital signatures, name approval)
Annual Compliance Minimal (Only standard GST returns & personal ITR) Heavy (Mandatory statutory audit, MCA annual filings, board meetings)
Personal Liability Unlimited (Proprietor is personally liable for business debt) Limited (Personal assets protected by corporate veil)
External Equity Funding Not possible (Cannot issue shares or bring in VC capital) Eligible (Can issue equity shares to angel investors and VCs)
Amazon Selling Rights 100% Identical (Same Buy Box algorithms, FBA access, & PPC tools) 100% Identical

Leveraging Udyam MSME Registration

If you launch as a Sole Proprietorship, we strongly recommend registering for an Udyam MSME Certificate via the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises portal (udyamregistration.gov.in). It is 100% free, completely paperless (linked to your Aadhaar and PAN), and provides instant government-recognized proof of enterprise existence that commercial banks accept for opening business current accounts.

SECTION 11 · ONBOARDING PITFALLS & TROUBLESHOOTING

11. Common Document Mistakes That Can Delay Amazon Seller Registration

Amazon’s automated verification algorithms process thousands of applicant credentials daily. Even an imperceptible typographical error or a mismatched character between government databases can trigger automated rejections, placing your account into manual review queues that take days or weeks to resolve.

Common Mistake Why It Causes Onboarding Rejections Preventive Action & Correction
Name Discrepancy on PAN vs. Bank Penny-drop verification fails if the CBS banking name does not match the PAN name character-for-character (e.g., missing middle names). Ensure your bank account holder name is updated to reflect your exact PAN card name before initiating registration.
Entering Trade Name Instead of Legal Name Entering your commercial store name (e.g., "Apex Electronics") in the PAN legal entity field triggers an instant API verification rejection. Always enter your official personal legal name for proprietorships, or exact incorporated name for companies.
Pickup Pincode in Different State than GSTIN Under Indian tax law, goods cannot be dispatched from an unregistered state under your primary GSTIN. Ensure your Easy Ship pickup address resides in the exact state represented by the first two digits of your GSTIN.
Using a Personal Shopping Email / Phone If your consumer account has historical billing issues or two-factor login conflicts, seller registration can hang. Establish a clean, dedicated commercial email inbox and mobile phone number dedicated solely to business operations.
Uploading Blurred or Cropped Document Images Automated Optical Character Recognition (OCR) systems reject illegible scans, cropped edges, or black-and-white photocopies. Upload high-resolution, full-page, unedited color PDF documents or high-clarity smartphone scans showing all four corners.
Expired Utility Bills as Address Proof Amazon guidelines strictly mandate that electricity, water, or gas utility bills must be issued within the last 60–90 days. Always provide the latest month's paid utility bill displaying the exact business street address.
Attempting to Sell Food / Toys Without Licenses Listing inventory without required FSSAI or BIS registrations results in instant listing suppression and potential account suspension. Complete category approval workflows and upload mandatory compliance certificates prior to creating product listings.
Submitting Inactive or Suspended GSTIN If your GST registration status on gst.gov.in is "Suspended" due to non-filing of returns, Amazon’s API will reject registration. Verify that your GSTIN status is "Active" on the public GST portal search tool before applying on Amazon.

SECTION 15 · COMPREHENSIVE SELLER FAQS

15. Frequently Asked Questions (15 Direct Answers)

Here are straightforward, verified answers to the 15 most common questions entrepreneurs ask regarding documents and compliance for Amazon India:

1. What documents are required for an Amazon seller account in India?

The essential credentials are: 1) Active Permanent Account Number (PAN) of the individual proprietor or incorporated entity; 2) Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN) for taxable commercial goods; 3) Active domestic savings or current bank account with IFSC code; 4) Operational mobile phone number and dedicated business email address; and 5) Complete registered business address and pickup warehouse address.

2. Is PAN card mandatory for Amazon seller registration?

Yes. A valid PAN is strictly mandatory for every Amazon seller in India. It verifies legal identity, enables compliance with Income Tax TDS provisions under Section 194-O, and connects to government tax databases. You cannot complete initial registration without submitting an active PAN.

3. Is GSTIN compulsory to sell on Amazon India?

Yes, GSTIN is legally compulsory under Section 24(ix) of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017 for any business supplying taxable goods via an e-commerce platform. The standard ₹40 lakh offline turnover exemption does not apply to online marketplace sellers.

4. Can I sell on Amazon without GST?

You can register without a GSTIN only if you deal exclusively in 100% GST-exempt products under Indian tax law, such as unbranded physical books, certain raw agricultural produce, and specific unbranded handloom textiles. If you list even one taxable item (such as notebooks, apparel, or electronics), GSTIN becomes mandatory immediately.

5. Can I use my personal PAN for Amazon seller registration?

Yes, if you register as a Sole Proprietor or Individual seller. In Indian law, a sole proprietorship does not possess a separate legal identity from its proprietor, so your personal PAN is the official tax anchor. If registering as a Partnership, LLP, or Private Limited Company, you must enter the entity's dedicated PAN.

6. Is a current bank account compulsory for Amazon sellers?

No, a current bank account is not universally compulsory. Sole proprietors and individual sellers can link an active personal savings account, provided the name strictly matches their PAN card. However, for LLPs and Private Limited Companies, a current bank account in the entity's registered corporate name is legally required.

7. Can I use a savings account to receive Amazon payments?

Yes. Sole proprietors and individual sellers can receive bi-weekly Amazon sales disbursements into a personal savings account, provided it passes automated penny-drop verification and the account holder name matches their registered legal identity.

8. Do I need a company registration certificate to sell on Amazon?

No. You do not need a Certificate of Incorporation (COI) or a registered private limited company. The vast majority of sellers in India operate legally and successfully as Sole Proprietors using their personal PAN and an active GSTIN.

9. Can a sole proprietor sell on Amazon India?

Yes. Sole proprietorship is the single most common business structure on Amazon India. You only need your personal PAN, an active GST certificate (Form REG-06) showing your trade name, an active bank account, and a verified pickup address.

10. What address proof is required for Amazon seller registration?

Amazon verifies your business address primarily through your official GSTIN database records. If secondary proof is requested during KYC verification, acceptable documents include electricity bills issued within 60 days, municipal property tax receipts, registered rent agreements with owner NOC, or bank statements showing your physical address.

11. Do I need a business email address?

While a domain-specific email (e.g., info@yourbrand.com) is not mandatory, Amazon strongly advises creating a dedicated email account (even a clean, separate Gmail address) dedicated solely to your Amazon business, completely separated from personal shopping accounts.

12. Can I register as an Amazon seller without a website?

Yes, absolutely. You do not need an independent e-commerce website, domain name, or external web store. Amazon provides the complete storefront infrastructure, customer traffic, secure payment gateways, and order processing backend.

13. What documents are required for a private limited company?

A Private Limited company requires: 1) Corporate PAN card; 2) Certificate of Incorporation (CIN) from MCA; 3) MOA and AOA; 4) Board Resolution authorizing a designated operating representative; 5) Directors' KYC (PAN & Aadhaar); 6) Corporate Current Bank Account with cancelled cheque; and 7) Company GSTIN.

14. Are additional documents required for restricted product categories?

Yes. Regulated product verticals require specialized licenses: an FSSAI license for food and supplements, BIS ISI / CRS certifications for toys and electronic hardware, drug/cosmetic licenses (Form 20B/21B) for medical and skincare items, and manufacturer tax invoices or brand authorization letters for reselling gated brands.

15. What should I do if Amazon rejects my documents?

Carefully review the rejection notice inside Seller Central. The most common reasons are character-level name mismatches between PAN and bank account, blurred document scans, or address discrepancies. Re-verify your legal details against official records on incometax.gov.in and gst.gov.in, and upload unedited, full-page color PDF documents for manual review.
