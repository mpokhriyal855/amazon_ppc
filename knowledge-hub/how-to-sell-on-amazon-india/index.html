QUICK ANSWER · HOW TO START SELLING ON AMAZON INDIA

To sell on Amazon India (Amazon.in), gather your PAN card, an active GSTIN (mandatory for most taxable goods; limited exceptions apply for 100% GST-exempt items and qualifying intra-state micro-sellers per platform rules), an active bank account matching your legal entity name, a mobile number, email address, and a serviceable pickup address. Register for ₹0 monthly subscription fee at sell.amazon.in, complete document and Video KYC verification, list products via existing ASIN matching or GTIN/GTIN exemption, pick a fulfilment mode (FBA, Easy Ship, or Self-Ship), price after Referral, Closing, Shipping fees, and 18% fee GST, and start fulfilling orders via Seller Central.

KEY TAKEAWAYS BEFORE YOU BEGIN
  • ₹0 Fixed Monthly Subscription Fee: Amazon.in charges per-order fees (Referral, Closing, Shipping) plus 18% fee GST only when orders sell (optional FBA storage fees apply if inventory rests in Amazon warehouses).
  • Model Unit Economics Before Ordering Inventory: Compute pre-ad contribution margin after COGS, product GST, Referral %, Closing/Weight Handling fees, and a 5–10% return buffer before locking capital.
  • Fulfilment Controls Trust & Conversion: FBA delivers automatic Prime badges and 1–2 day metro speed, while Easy Ship lets startups test demand without bulk warehouse transfers upfront.
  • Organic SEO and PPC Compound Each Other: Clear mobile titles, compliant white-background images, and 249-byte backend terms establish indexation; margin-capped Sponsored Products drive early sales velocity.
  • Account Health & Stock Consistency Safeguard Rank: Keeping Order Defect Rate strictly below 1% and reordering before stockouts protects Featured Offer eligibility and search placement.

1. What Is Selling on Amazon India?

Selling on Amazon India (Amazon.in) means listing physical consumer or business products on India’s largest third-party e-commerce marketplace so shoppers across metro cities and rural pincodes can discover, pay for, and receive your goods. Under Indian e-commerce regulations, Amazon India operates as an open marketplace intermediary: third-party sellers remain the legal merchants of record who own inventory, set selling prices, issue tax invoices to buyers, and manage compliance.

Amazon’s infrastructure handles the heavyweight consumer side—trusted checkout, UPI/card/Cash-on-Delivery (COD) payment collection, search discovery, customer messaging workflows, courier networks, and structured dispute resolution—while giving every registered merchant a single control tower called Amazon Seller Central.

Understanding where your business fits among seller models helps determine your capital structure, GST setup, and margin upside:

  • Individual Seller / Solo Entrepreneur: Tests small-batch inventory, local crafts, books, or curated products using streamlined sole-proprietorship compliance.
  • Manufacturer: Produces goods directly in India (e.g., textiles, steelware, skincare, furniture, handicrafts) and captures retail margins previously shared with wholesalers.
  • Wholesaler / Authorised Distributor: Buys existing national brands in bulk and supplies steady inventory on existing ASINs where winning the Featured Offer (Buy Box) drives velocity.
  • Reseller / Arbitrage Merchant: Sources fast-moving compliant SKUs with valid GST invoices and brand authorisation letters across multiple open categories.
  • Private-Label Seller: Sources customised or contract-manufactured products sold under an original brand name, differentiated through packaging, features, and listing design.
  • Brand Owner (D2C / Established Enterprise): Protects IP via Indian Trademark registration and Amazon Brand Registry, controlling catalog authority, A+ Content, Brand Stores, and full-funnel advertising.

2. Who Can Sell on Amazon India?

Amazon India allows Indian-resident individuals and legally registered domestic business entities to register as third-party sellers. Being allowed to open an account, however, does not mean every seller can immediately list every product—certain regulated categories (such as packaged food, cosmetics, electronics, or national third-party brands) require documentary approvals before listings go live.

Seller Entity Type Best Suited For Typical Tax & Banking Profile
Individuals & Sole Proprietors First-time founders, craft makers, home-based resellers, single-person D2C startups. Personal PAN + Proprietorship GSTIN (if taxable); savings/current bank account matching legal/trade name.
Partnership Firms & LLPs Co-founded brands, regional distributors, and growing mid-market wholesale teams. Firm/LLP PAN card + Partnership Deed / LLP Agreement + Business GSTIN + Current Account.
Private Limited Companies Funded D2C brands, large manufacturers, multi-state FBA operators. Corporate PAN + COI + MOA/AOA + Board Resolution (if requested) + GSTIN(s) + Corporate Current Account.
Manufacturers & Wholesalers Factories transitioning from offline distributor networks to direct retail margin. Standard GST entity docs plus factory/brand proof, trademark certificate, or GS1 EAN barcodes.
Small Businesses, Artisans & Women Founders Handloom clusters, local weavers, micro-enterprises qualifying for Amazon Karigar, Saheli, or Launchpad. Entity KYC plus relevant Udyam MSME / artisan / SHG documentation where applicable.

3. Requirements to Start Selling on Amazon India

Before clicking “Start Selling” on Amazon.in, organise all statutory identity, tax, and banking documents carefully. Most registration delays happen because a single spelling mismatch or blurred PDF triggers automated KYC holds.

GST Registration, Exempt Goods & Enrolment ID Nuance

Under Section 52 of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017, e-commerce marketplace operators in India must collect Tax Collected at Source (TCS) on taxable marketplace supplies. Consequently, having an active GSTIN is the standard legal requirement for selling taxable commercial goods across Amazon India, and the first two digits of your GSTIN must match the Indian state where your primary pickup or FBA warehouse is located.

Two statutory nuances are frequently misunderstood by beginners:

  • Exclusively 100% GST-Exempt Products: Sellers dealing strictly in 100% tax-exempt items (such as printed books, maps, or specified unbranded agricultural produce) can onboard with a valid PAN and exempted declaration, provided they do not mix even a single taxable item (such as gift wrap or a plastic bookmark) into the catalog.
  • Intra-State Micro-Seller Notification / Enrolment ID: Recent GST Council notifications allowed qualifying small suppliers (within statutory turnover limits, supplying solely within their home state and not making inter-state B2C sales) to apply for a GST Enrolment ID under specific conditions. In practice, national inter-state selling and multi-state FBA storage on Amazon.in still practically require a full Regular GSTIN. Always verify your exact category, turnover, and Enrolment ID eligibility inside Seller Central and with a qualified Chartered Accountant before onboarding.

PAN Card, Identity Proof, Bank Account & Contact Details

4. Step-by-Step Amazon India Seller Registration Process

If your documents are scanned clearly and names align across PAN, GSTIN, and your bank mandate, submitting your Seller Central registration takes 20 to 30 minutes, with Identity/Video KYC checks typically clearing within 2 to 7 working days:

  1. Check Serviceability & Documents: Confirm your pickup pincode supports Amazon Easy Ship (if starting self-fulfilled) and keep clean PDF/JPEG scans of PAN, GST REG-06, ID proof, and cancelled cheque ready.
  2. Visit the Official Registration Portal: Go to sell.amazon.in (official Amazon India Seller Central onboarding site) and select Start Selling.
  3. Create Your Amazon Business Login: Enter a dedicated business email address, mobile number, and strong password, keeping Two-Step Verification (2FA) active.
  4. Verify Mobile Number via OTP: Confirm the one-time password sent to your primary business phone.
  5. Enter Legal Business Name & Entity Type: Copy the exact Legal Name printed on your PAN / GST registration—avoid informal abbreviations.
  6. Select Store Display Name & Category: Pick an available customer-facing storefront name (this can usually be edited later unless trademarked) and choose your primary retail category.
  7. Configure Pickup Address: Enter your exact warehouse, office, or dispatch street address matching your GST place-of-business record.
  8. Verify GSTIN or Tax Exemption Status: Enter your 15-digit GSTIN (verified in real time against GSTN records) or complete the exempt/Enrolment pathway if eligible.
  9. Select Preferred Shipping Method: Choose whether to enable Easy Ship, Self-Ship, or express interest in Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA).
  10. Submit Bank Account Details: Enter Account Holder Name, Account Number, and IFSC code; Amazon deposits ₹1 or requests statement validation to confirm payout routing.
  11. Update Default Product Tax Code (PTC) & Signature: Set default GST tax slabs accurately (or assign per SKU) and upload a crisp digital image of your authorised signature for automated buyer GST invoices.
  12. Complete Seller Identity & Video Verification: Join Amazon’s scheduled or instant browser video KYC call in good lighting with your original PAN and identity card on hand.
  13. List Your First Product & Verify Two-Factor Backup: Once Seller Central shows your account status as Active, add your first compliant listing and configure a secondary backup phone for uninterrupted login access.

5. Understanding Amazon Seller Central

Amazon Seller Central (sellercentral.amazon.in) is the daily operations dashboard for every Amazon India seller. Beginners do not need to master every submenu on Day 1; focus first on these eight core modules:

1. Catalog

Add new products, match existing ASINs, apply for GTIN exemptions, submit category approvals, and fix suppressed listing drafts.

2. Inventory

Edit stock counts and prices (Manage All Inventory), track FBA stock health, create FBA inbound shipments, and clear stranded inventory.

3. Pricing

Review Pricing Health warnings, configure Business (B2B) quantity discounts, and spot Featured Offer (Buy Box) price gaps.

4. Orders & Returns

Confirm daily Easy Ship pickups, print thermal shipping labels and GST invoices, track pending COD orders, and inspect return reasons.

5. Advertising & A+ Content

Launch Sponsored Products, Brands, and Display campaigns inside Campaign Manager; create Coupons, Deals, and Brand Stores.

6. Growth Opportunities

View Amazon’s category-specific recommendations, high-demand search keywords, and FBA selection suggestions.

7. Reports

Download Business Reports → Detail Page Sales and Traffic (Sessions & Unit Session %), Payments statements, and GST Tax Document Library files.

8. Performance & Account Health

Monitor Order Defect Rate, Late Dispatch Rate, Policy Warnings, Voice of the Customer (NCX), and submit SAFE-T reimbursement claims.

6. Choosing What to Sell on Amazon India & Sourcing Strategy

Product selection decides roughly 70% of your profitability before you launch a single ad. New sellers frequently pick trendy gadgets with brutal competition, heavy shipping slabs, or wafer-thin margins after GST and returns.

Practical Criteria for Product & Category Evaluation

  • Pre-Ad Margin Cushion (≥ 25% of Selling Price): If your product costs ₹300 and sells for ₹499 including GST, referral, shipping, and fee GST will leave almost nothing for PPC ads or damaged returns. Target healthy selling price bands (often ₹499–₹2,499) where fixed closing and shipping slabs stay small relative to order value.
  • Compact & Lightweight Dimensions: Amazon bills weight handling on whichever is higher: dead weight or volumetric weight. Keeping package weight ≤ 500g and avoiding bulky cardboard saves substantial per-unit logistics cost.
  • Low Breakage & Size-Return Exposure: Apparel and footwear carry high return rates on Indian COD orders; non-fragile Home & Kitchen organisers, Office supplies, Pet accessories, or specialised hardware tools generally yield cleaner net profit.
  • Open vs Gated / Regulated Categories: Open categories let compliant sellers publish swiftly, whereas Packaged Foods/Supplements require FSSAI licenses, Toys and covered Electronics require BIS registration, Cosmetics may require CDSCO/manufacturing credentials, and branded reselling demands authentic GST distribution invoices.
  • Legal Metrology Declarations: Packaged consumer goods sold in India must comply with Legal Metrology rules (MRP, net quantity, country of origin, month/year of manufacture/import, packer/importer details, and customer care contact).

Sourcing Strategy Options in India

Once a category looks viable, match your sourcing pathway to your capital and differentiation plan:

  • Own Manufacturing Workshop: Strongest long-term pricing power if you control production tolerances, raw material consistency, and barcode-ready outer packaging from Day 1.
  • Domestic Factory / OEM Contracting (Private Label): Source custom-branded variations from Indian manufacturing hubs (e.g., Delhi NCR, Moradabad, Surat, Rajkot, Tiruppur, Ludhiana) with proper GST tax invoices and custom master cartons.
  • Authorised Brand Distributorship (Reselling): Buy established consumer brands directly from company distributors—always retaining GST tax invoices and Letters of Authorisation so you can resolve authenticity reviews immediately.

7. How to List Products on Amazon India

Depending on whether your product already exists in Amazon.in’s catalog, you will follow one of two workflows inside Catalog → Add Products:

Listing Method When to Use Mandatory Inputs & Rules
Method A: Match an Existing ASIN Reselling an identical branded item already listed on Amazon.in by the brand or distributors. Search ASIN/EAN, click Sell this product, set SKU, price, condition, and stock. Supply authentic distributor invoices if brand-gated. Never latch onto a competitor’s trademarked private-label ASIN.
Method B: Create a New ASIN Launching a proprietary private-label product, customised bundle, or unique unbranded SKU. Obtain an authentic GS1 India EAN/UPC barcode (or apply for GTIN Exemption for private-label/generic items), choose accurate Browse Node, upload full catalog data & images.

Mandatory Anatomy of a Compliant Amazon.in Listing

  • Product Title: Clear, accurate, free of promotional hype (“Best”, “#1”, “Free Shipping”). Front-load Brand + Product Type + Core Material/Size/Pack Count.
  • Bullet Points (5 Key Features): Lead with buyer benefits, dimensions, compatibility, box contents, and care instructions formatted cleanly for mobile skimming.
  • Main Image Standards: Pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255), product filling ~85%+ of frame, crisp lighting, zero watermarks, badges, or text overlays.
  • Secondary Images (5–6 Slots): Dimensions graphic, close-up quality/material shot, lifestyle usage photo, packaging/what’s-in-the-box frame, and comparison chart.
  • Variations (Parent-Child): Group genuinely identical sizes, weights, or colours into one variation family so customer reviews aggregate cleanly—never attach unrelated products just to borrow reviews.

8. Amazon Fulfilment Models in India: FBA vs Easy Ship vs Self-Ship

How you store, pack, and dispatch orders directly affects shipping costs, Prime badge conversion, and Return-to-Origin (RTO) rates. Most sellers start with Easy Ship to validate demand, then graduate winning hero SKUs to Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA).

Operational Factor Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) Amazon Easy Ship Self-Ship (MFN)
Storage & Packing Stock stored in Amazon warehouses; Amazon picks, packs, and dispatches 24/7. You store and pack orders at your own warehouse or shop. You store, pack, and arrange pickup completely independently.
Delivery Courier Amazon Transportation Services (ATS) & Prime network. Amazon Easy Ship pickup boy collects packed parcels daily. Third-party couriers (Delhivery, Blue Dart, Shiprocket, India Post, etc.).
Prime Badge Access Automatic Prime Badge on active FBA inventory. Standard delivery; limited Prime access via Local Shop / Seller Flex only if eligible. No Prime badge; slower delivery promise reduces buyer trust.
Cost Structure FBA pick-pack/weight handling + monthly cubic storage fee. Your own packing cost + Easy Ship weight/zone fee. Your packing + 3PL courier bill (often costlier for remote COD orders).
GST State Requirement (APOB / VPOB) Expanding inventory to FBA warehouses in other states requires GST registration / APOB/VPOB in each stocked state. Single home-state GSTIN works for Pan-India deliveries shipped from your home warehouse. Single home-state GSTIN covers your dispatch warehouse nationwide.
Best Use Case Proven SKUs, competitive categories, festive sales, and multi-city speed. New sellers validating demand, heavy/fragile local items, made-to-order goods. Oversized/bulky furniture or pincodes not covered by Easy Ship pickup.

9. Amazon SEO & High-Converting Product Listings

Amazon’s search engine does not rank pages for reading time or backlinks—it ranks listings based on purchase likelihood. When shoppers search a term like “stainless steel insulated water bottle 1 litre”, Amazon weights keyword relevance alongside historical Click-Through Rate (CTR), Unit Session Percentage (Conversion Rate), consistent in-stock velocity, and delivery speed to the buyer’s pincode.

Practical On-Page SEO & CRO Checklist for Amazon.in

  • Primary Keyword Placement: Put your highest-intent root keyword within the first 70–80 characters of the title so mobile shoppers immediately confirm relevance.
  • 249-Byte Backend Generic Keywords: Add synonyms, spelling variations, use-cases, and relevant Hindi/Hinglish search phrases (for example, garam pani bottle or kapda sukhane stand) separated by spaces with no commas or competitor brand names, staying strictly under 249 UTF-8 bytes.
  • Visual Conversion Engineering: On mobile screens (where most Indian shoppers browse), the main image and first two secondary infographic slides decide whether a visitor bounces. Show product scale, material thickness, and primary problem solved visually rather than burying specs in dense text.
  • A+ Content (Brand Registered Sellers): Replace basic description text with rich comparison tables, cross-sell catalog grids, and lifestyle banners to answer buyer objections before they read competitors’ pages.

10. Pricing Strategy and Winning the Featured Offer (Buy Box)

On Amazon.in, the white purchase panel housing “Add to Cart” and “Buy Now” is officially called the Featured Offer (widely known as the Buy Box). When multiple resellers compete on the same ASIN—or even when a brand owner wants Featured Offer eligibility—price discipline and account metrics govern who wins the click.

  • Total Landed Price vs MRP: Always price inclusive of shipping charges at or below your printed Legal Metrology Maximum Retail Price (MRP). Pricing higher than MRP or significantly above historical reference prices triggers Pricing Health suppression—stripping your Featured Offer button entirely.
  • Featured Offer Drivers: Competitive landed price, FBA / Prime delivery speed, Order Defect Rate strictly below 1%, Late Dispatch Rate below 4%, and steady in-stock availability.
  • Protecting Your Price Floor: Rather than slashing selling price every time a newcomer drops ₹10, calculate your firm break-even price floor upfront and use digital Coupons selectively when testing conversion elasticity without eroding 30-day deal reference prices.

11. Amazon India Selling Fees & Profit Calculation

Amazon India charges fees primarily when an order is placed or when FBA stores your goods. Under schedules published on sell.amazon.in/fees-and-pricing, every seller’s unit economics combine four core Amazon charges plus product GST and return reality:

  • 1. Referral Fee: A category- and price-band percentage applied on the total selling price paid by the buyer (typically spanning roughly 2% to ~17%+ across categories, with specific low-ASP fee waivers periodically notified by Amazon—always confirm live category percentages in Seller Central’s Revenue Calculator).
  • 2. Closing Fee: A fixed rupee fee per item determined by selling-price brackets (₹0–₹300, ₹301–₹500, ₹501–₹1,000, above ₹1,000) and fulfilment program (FBA, Easy Ship, Self-Ship).
  • 3. Weight Handling / Shipping Fee: Charged per unit based on Local (same city), Regional (same zone), or National shipping distance and slab weight—calculated using the greater of Dead Weight or Volumetric Weight (Length × Breadth × Height in cm ÷ 5000).
  • 4. Storage & Pick-Pack (FBA Only) + 18% GST on Amazon Fees: FBA items incur monthly cubic-foot storage charges (plus aged-inventory surcharges if unsold too long). Crucially, Amazon adds 18% GST on all its service fees; regular GST-registered sellers can normally offset that 18% fee GST via Input Tax Credit (ITC) against output tax liability (confirm with your CA).
NET UNIT PROFIT
Pre-GST Revenue − COGS − Amazon Fees − Return Buffer − Ad Spend

Set aside buyer output GST first (Selling Price ÷ (1 + GST%)) before comparing against landed COGS.

BREAK-EVEN ACOS
(Pre-Ad Contribution ÷ Selling Price) × 100

Maximum campaign ACoS ceiling before an ad-driven unit loses contribution cash.

VOLUMETRIC WEIGHT (KG)
(Length × Breadth × Height cm) ÷ 5000

Amazon charges weight handling on the higher of actual parcel weight or volumetric weight.

12. How Amazon Sellers Receive Payments

Amazon India settles net proceeds directly to your verified bank account via NEFT/RTGS rather than holding funds indefinitely—provided your bank mandate is active and account performance remains clean.

  • Settlement Cycle & Delivery Reserve: Eligible delivered order proceeds typically disburse on a 7-day settlement cycle (often subject to Amazon’s post-delivery return window reserve, commonly DD+7 for newer or certain account tiers). Always verify your exact disbursement schedule under Reports → Payments.
  • Statutory Tax Deductions (TCS & TDS): On taxable sales, Amazon deducts statutory e-commerce TCS (Section 52 CGST, claimable in your GST Electronic Cash Ledger) and Income Tax TDS (Section 194-O, visible in Form 26AS / AIS against your PAN).
  • Account Level Reserve: Amazon temporarily holds a rolling buffer when processing open A-to-z Guarantee claims, chargebacks, or sudden order-volume spikes on new accounts.
  • Monthly CA Reconciliation: Download MTR / B2C Tax Reports and Seller Fee Tax Invoices from Seller Central every month so your Chartered Accountant reconciles gross sales, return credit notes, TCS, TDS, and 18% Input Tax Credit cleanly.

13. Managing Orders, Inventory and Returns

Consistent daily operational execution protects both your profit margin and your search rankings:

  • Order Dispatch Routine (Easy Ship / Self-Ship): Print packing slips and GST buyer invoices cleanly, seal corrugated boxes with corner protection to survive regional transit, and hand packages over inside the scheduled Easy Ship pickup slot to avoid Late Dispatch Rate penalties.
  • Stockout Prevention: Running completely out of stock erases organic keyword velocity. Track daily sales speed against supplier manufacturing plus FBA check-in lead time, keeping an Inventory Performance Index (IPI) comfortably above 400 to preserve warehouse storage headroom.
  • Handling Returns, COD RTO & SAFE-T Claims: Cash-on-Delivery orders expand reach across Tier-2/3 cities but increase Return-to-Origin (RTO) rejections. Track Voice of the Customer (NCX) feedback weekly to fix unclear product specs or weak packaging. When an Easy Ship or Self-Ship order returns damaged, used, or missing components, capture unboxing photographs immediately and file a SAFE-T claim inside Seller Central’s eligible claim window.
REORDER POINT FORMULA & ILLUSTRATIVE WORKED EXAMPLE
Reorder Point = (Average Daily Units Sold × Total Lead Time in Days) + Safety Buffer Stock

Hypothetical Example: If an FBA hero SKU averages 15 units/day, supplier production plus Amazon inbound check-in takes 20 days, and you hold a 10-day safety buffer (150 units), your reorder threshold is (15 × 20) + 150 = 450 units. Place your next factory purchase order the day remaining sellable stock hits 450 units.

16. Introduction to Amazon Advertising (Amazon PPC)

Running Amazon Ads is not compulsory, yet in competitive categories on Amazon.in, the first screen of mobile search results is dominated by Sponsored placements. Amazon PPC runs on a cost-per-click (CPC) auction across three main formats:

  • Sponsored Products (Core Engine): Keyword and ASIN-targeted ads appearing directly inside search results and competitor product pages. Start here with separated Auto Discovery and Manual Exact/Phrase campaigns.
  • Sponsored Brands (Brand Registered Sellers): Top-of-search banner and video ads showcasing your brand logo and multiple catalog items—powerful for capturing category-level search intent and defending your brand name.
  • Sponsored Display: Retargets shoppers who viewed your listing without buying, or defends your own product pages against rival cross-sell placements.
ACoS (AD COST OF SALES)
(Ad Spend ÷ Ad Revenue) × 100

Lower ACoS means stronger direct campaign efficiency. Always benchmark against unit Break-Even ACoS.

ROAS (RETURN ON AD SPEND)
Ad Revenue ÷ Ad Spend

Measures gross revenue generated per ₹1 spent on ads (e.g., 4.0x ROAS equals 25% ACoS).

TACoS (TOTAL ACOS)
(Ad Spend ÷ Total Business Sales) × 100

Reveals whether advertising lifts organic rank and overall catalog profitability—not just paid clicks.

Manage spend against profitability rather than clicks alone. When high-converting search terms emerge in Auto or Broad campaigns, graduate them into dedicated Exact Match campaigns and negate zero-order cash-burn queries weekly.

18. Common Mistakes Beginners Must Avoid

Beginner Mistake Why It Destroys Cash Flow or Account Standing Smarter Practitioner Habit
1. Estimating profit using only referral fee Ignoring closing fees, 18% fee GST, volumetric shipping, and COD returns turns “30% margin” into real losses. Model every cost item upfront in a unit economics sheet before placing factory orders.
2. Attaching offers to private-label ASINs Reselling onto another brand’s registered ASIN without distribution invoices triggers instant IP/Counterfeit blocks. Verify trademark ownership or buy directly from authorised distributors with GST invoices.
3. Running single “all-in” Auto campaigns Blends irrelevant broad clicks with winners, burning budget while obscuring which SKUs make money. Separate campaigns by SKU margin tier, isolate Exact Match heroes, and add negatives weekly.
4. Mixing taxable and GST-exempt SKUs carelessly Adding one taxable accessory voids GST-exempt seller eligibility, risking compliance suspension. Obtain an active home-state GSTIN unless 100% of your catalog legally qualifies as exempt.
5. Letting hero SKUs stock out repeatedly Outages beyond 7–14 days erase historical sales velocity and hand Page-1 rank to rivals. Reorder strictly at your calculated Reorder Point including a 10-to-15-day safety cushion.
6. Publishing dull mobile images or copied bullets Weak CTR forces higher CPC bids just to earn clicks, wrecking TACoS from Week 1. Invest early in bright white-background renders and 3–4 clear infographic callout images.

Amazon India Seller Glossary (Key Terms Explained)

Reference definitions for the core acronyms and marketplace terminology used throughout Amazon Seller Central India and this Knowledge Hub guide:

1. ASIN (Amazon Standard Identification Number)
Unique 10-character alphanumeric catalog ID assigned by Amazon to every distinct product listing.
2. SKU (Stock Keeping Unit)
Seller-defined internal inventory tracking code mapped to an ASIN offer inside Seller Central.
3. Seller Central
Web dashboard (sellercentral.amazon.in) where merchants manage listings, stock, orders, pricing, payments, and ads.
4. Featured Offer (Buy Box)
The white purchase box with “Add to Cart” / “Buy Now” awarded to offers meeting price, speed, and account health benchmarks.
5. FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon)
Service where sellers send bulk stock to Amazon warehouses for storage, packing, Prime delivery, and returns handling.
6. Amazon Easy Ship
Hybrid fulfilment where sellers store and pack orders locally while an Amazon associate collects and delivers parcels.
7. Self-Ship (MFN)
Merchant-fulfilled shipping using independent third-party couriers while uploading valid tracking IDs in Seller Central.
8. GSTIN
15-digit Goods and Services Tax Identification Number tied to your business PAN and state dispatch address.
9. HSN Code
Harmonised System of Nomenclature tax classification code determining which GST slab rate applies to your SKU.
10. TCS (Section 52 CGST)
Tax Collected at Source deducted by e-commerce operators on taxable sales, credited into your GST cash ledger.
11. TDS (Section 194-O)
Income tax deducted at source by e-commerce operators on gross marketplace sales, claimable against annual tax returns.
12. APOB / VPOB
Additional or Virtual Place of Business address added under state GST to legally stock FBA goods in multiple states.
13. GTIN / EAN & GTIN Exemption
Global product barcodes (GS1 EAN/UPC) used to create catalog listings, or official brand waiver to list without barcodes.
14. Referral Fee
Category percentage commission charged by Amazon per sold unit based on total buyer-paid price.
15. Closing Fee
Fixed rupee transaction slab per sold item varying by selling-price bracket and FBA/Easy Ship channel.
16. Weight Handling Fee
Fulfilment shipping fee determined by regional/national zone and the higher of actual or volumetric parcel weight.
17. A10 / COSMO Ranking Signals
Amazon’s product search relevance and customer-intent systems weighting CTR, conversion velocity, and Prime speed.
18. Unit Session Percentage (CVR)
Units ordered divided by listing page sessions—one of the strongest indicators of listing quality and price competitiveness.
19. ACoS & ROAS
Ad efficiency metrics: ACoS = (Ad Spend ÷ Ad Revenue) × 100; ROAS = Ad Revenue ÷ Ad Spend.
20. TACoS (Total Advertising Cost of Sales)
Ad Spend ÷ Total Brand Revenue (Ad + Organic)—tracks whether paid ads grow profitable overall volume.
21. ODR (Order Defect Rate)
Percentage of orders with negative feedback, A-to-z claims, or chargebacks; must stay strictly below 1%.
22. IPI Score (Inventory Performance Index)
Amazon FBA health gauge tracking sell-through speed, stranded stock, and overstock exposure.
23. SAFE-T Claim
Seller Assurance for E-Commerce Transactions claim filed to recover value when Easy Ship / Self-Ship returns arrive damaged or switched.
24. Amazon Brand Registry
Trademark verification program unlocking A+ Content, Brand Stores, Vine, Sponsored Brands, and counterfeit takedown tools.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Clear, practical answers to the top questions founders raise around selling on Amazon India:

1. Can I sell on Amazon India without GST?+

Generally, selling taxable commercial goods on Amazon.in requires a valid GSTIN because marketplaces deduct TCS under Section 52 of the CGST Act. Limited exceptions exist only if your entire catalog consists of 100% GST-exempt items (such as printed books or eligible unbranded agricultural goods) or qualifies under specific Enrolment ID rules for restricted intra-state non-GST suppliers permitted during onboarding. Always verify your exact business case with a Chartered Accountant.

2. How much investment is required to start selling on Amazon India?+

Creating an Amazon India seller account costs ₹0 in fixed monthly subscription charges. Real startup capital goes toward GST/trademark setup, sample testing, initial stock, packaging, and launch ads. Illustratively, micro resellers often start with ₹20,000–₹45,000, while focused single-SKU private-label brands usually budget ₹75,000–₹1,75,000 to hold clean inventory plus a 60-day PPC runway.

3. What documents are required to register as an Amazon seller in India?+

You need an active PAN card, an active GSTIN certificate matching your pickup state (unless selling exclusively exempt goods), an active bank account statement or cancelled cheque matching your legal entity name, identity proof for Video KYC (Aadhaar/Passport/Voter ID), a business email ID, mobile number, and a serviceable physical pickup address.

4. How does Amazon FBA work compared to Easy Ship in India?+

Under Easy Ship, you store stock at your home/office, pack orders daily, and hand them to an Amazon courier at your doorstep. Under Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA), you ship stock in bulk to Amazon warehouses so Amazon stores, packs, delivers, and handles customer service—earning the Prime delivery badge that drives higher conversion rates.

5. How long does Amazon seller registration and verification take?+

Filling out the onboarding form on sell.amazon.in takes 20–30 minutes. Automated checks plus Seller Identity Video Verification normally conclude within 2 to 7 working days when document legal names, bank account holders, and addresses align without discrepancies.

6. Can I sell products on Amazon India without owning a trademark or brand?+

Yes. You can resell genuine existing brands (keeping valid GST distributor invoices on file) or launch generic/unbranded items using a GTIN Exemption. However, filing an Indian trademark and enrolling in Amazon Brand Registry is essential if you want to protect private-label products and unlock A+ Content and Brand Stores.

7. How and when do Amazon India sellers receive payments?+

Net proceeds from delivered orders are credited directly to your verified bank account via NEFT/RTGS on a regular payout cycle (typically every 7 days for eligible funds), subject to delivery/return reserve periods and deductions for referral/closing/shipping fees, 18% fee GST, Section 52 TCS, and Section 194-O TDS.

8. What is Amazon India’s total selling fee percentage?+

There is no single flat percentage—your deduction combines a category-based Referral Fee, a selling-price Closing Fee, and a zone/weight-based Shipping Fee (plus FBA cubic storage if stocked in FBA), all attracting 18% GST on seller fees. Depending on price point and box size, combined Amazon charges commonly range from ~18% to ~32%+ of selling price; check Seller Central’s Fee Preview for exact live rates.

9. Do beginners need Amazon PPC ads from Day 1?+

Paid ads are optional, not mandatory. However, new ASINs start without historical sales velocity, so top organic slots go to established sellers. Investing a controlled daily budget into Sponsored Products Exact Match and Auto discovery accelerates initial orders and keyword relevance much faster than waiting passively.

10. What is the difference between ACoS, ROAS, and TACoS?+

ACoS measures Ad Spend ÷ Ad Sales (cost ratio of paid campaigns alone). ROAS is the inverse (Ad Sales ÷ Ad Spend). TACoS measures Ad Spend ÷ Total Business Sales (paid plus organic), revealing whether ad spend is compounding organic sales and overall business profit.

11. What happens when a buyer cancels Cash-on-Delivery or returns a damaged item?+

Customer-cancelled or undeliverable COD (RTO) parcels return to your warehouse or FBA node and reverse the order credit; certain logistics fees may apply depending on return reason. If an Easy Ship or Self-Ship item returns damaged or switched, document unboxing photos/video and submit a SAFE-T reimbursement claim before the deadline in Seller Central.
