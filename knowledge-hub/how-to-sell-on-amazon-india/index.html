Quick Answer · How to Start Selling on Amazon India

Gather your PAN card, active GSTIN (required for most taxable goods; limited exceptions exist for 100% GST-exempt items and qualifying intra-state micro-sellers), an active bank account matching your legal name, phone, email, and a pickup address. Register for ₹0/mo at sell.amazon.in, clear Video KYC (2–7 days), list via existing ASIN match or GTIN/exemption, choose FBA, Easy Ship, or Self-Ship, price after fees + 18% fee GST, and manage orders inside Seller Central.

CHAPTER 01 · MARKETPLACE OVERVIEW

1. What Is Selling on Amazon India?

Amazon.in acts as a third-party marketplace intermediary: you remain the legal merchant who owns stock, sets prices, and issues buyer GST invoices, while Amazon supplies traffic discovery, UPI/card/COD checkout, courier logistics, and Seller Central controls.

  • Individual / Solo Seller: Tests curated goods or local crafts via sole-proprietorship compliance.
  • Manufacturer: Captures retail margins directly from factory production (textiles, steelware, skincare, home decor).
  • Wholesaler / Reseller: Supplies steady stock on existing brand ASINs with valid GST distributor invoices.
  • Private-Label & Brand Owner: Differentiates packaging and defends IP through Trademark + Amazon Brand Registry.

CHAPTER 02 · ELIGIBILITY

2. Who Can Sell on Amazon India?

Indian individuals and registered entities can onboard on Amazon.in, though regulated categories (packaged foods, cosmetics, electronics, third-party brands) require additional approvals before listing.

Seller Entity Type Best Suited For Tax & Banking Setup
Individuals / Sole Proprietors First-time founders, craft makers, single-owner resellers. Personal PAN + Proprietorship GSTIN (if taxable) + matching bank account.
Partnership Firms & LLPs Co-founded D2C brands and regional wholesale teams. Firm/LLP PAN + Deed/LLP Agreement + Business GSTIN + Current Account.
Private Limited Companies Funded consumer brands, factories, multi-state FBA operators. Company PAN + COI/MOA/AOA + GSTIN(s) + Corporate Current Account.
Artisans & MSME Micro-Units Handloom weavers, local crafts, women-led businesses (Karigar/Saheli). Standard KYC plus Udyam MSME / artisan credentials if applicable.
CHAPTER 03 · GST & KYC DOCUMENTS

3. Requirements to Start Selling on Amazon India

Under Section 52 of the CGST Act, marketplaces collect Tax Collected at Source (TCS) on taxable sales—making an active home-state GSTIN standard for commercial sellers. Exceptions apply strictly if selling exclusively 100% GST-exempt goods (e.g., printed books) or eligible intra-state micro-sellers holding a permitted GST Enrolment ID (national selling and multi-state FBA still practically require Regular GSTIN).

Requirement Verification Rule Common Rejection Cause
PAN & GSTIN (REG-06) Legal entity PAN + active GSTIN matching your warehouse state. Name/state mismatch or mixing a taxable SKU onto an exempt catalog.
Bank Account Cancelled cheque or statement showing IFSC + matching entity name. Account holder differs from PAN/GST legal/trade name.
Identity, Phone & Address Aadhaar/Passport/Voter ID + unique mobile/email + serviceable pickup pincode. Glare on laminated IDs or reusing credentials tied to an old account.
CHAPTER 04 · STEP-BY-STEP ONBOARDING

4. Seller Registration Process (13 Steps)

With clean scans ready, online submission at sell.amazon.in takes ~25 minutes, and automated KYC + Video Verification usually clear in 2 to 7 working days:

  1. Confirm Easy Ship pincode serviceability and prepare PAN, GST REG-06, ID proof, and bank scans.
  2. Open sell.amazon.in → click Start Selling and create a dedicated business login.
  3. Verify business mobile OTP and enable Two-Step Verification (2FA) plus a backup phone.
  4. Enter exact Legal Name & Entity Type matching your PAN/GST record letter-for-letter.
  5. Set your storefront display name, primary retail category, and GST-matching pickup address.
  6. Validate your 15-digit GSTIN (or tax-exempt / Enrolment pathway) and pick FBA/Easy Ship preference.
  7. Submit bank details + IFSC, default Product Tax Code (PTC), and clear digital signature scan.
  8. Complete Seller Identity Video Verification with original PAN/ID and publish your first SKU.

CHAPTER 05 · CONTROL TOWER

5. Understanding Amazon Seller Central

Seller Central (sellercentral.amazon.in) runs daily store operations. Focus early on these high-impact modules:

1. Catalog & GTIN

Create listings, match existing ASINs, request GTIN exemptions, and submit category approvals (FSSAI/BIS).

2. Inventory & FBA Shipments

Adjust stock/prices, resolve stranded inventory, and schedule bulk FBA warehouse check-ins.

3. Pricing & Buy Box Health

Catch MRP reference alerts, configure B2B bulk tiers, and protect Featured Offer eligibility.

4. Orders & Returns

Schedule Easy Ship courier pickups, print GST buyer invoices, and audit return reasons.

5. Advertising & A+ Content

Launch Sponsored Products/Brands, Coupons, Lightning Deals, and Brand Registry A+ pages.

6. Reports & Tax Library

Export Business Reports (Sessions/CVR), settlement sheets, and monthly GST/TCS tax invoices.

CHAPTER 09 · DISCOVERABILITY & CRO

9. Amazon SEO & High-Converting Listings

Amazon’s search algorithm ranks listings by purchase likelihood—weighing keyword relevance alongside mobile Click-Through Rate (CTR), Unit Session Percentage (CVR), stock continuity, and pincode delivery speed.

  • Front-Loaded Title: Put your highest-volume root phrase inside the first 70–80 characters for mobile visibility.
  • 249-Byte Backend Search Terms: Pack space-separated synonyms and Hindi/Hinglish phrases (garam pani bottle) under 249 UTF-8 bytes without commas or rival brands.
  • Infographic & A+ Visuals: Highlight dimensions, box contents, and comparison tables cleanly—see our Amazon Algorithm Explained (2027) guide and Listing Quality Grader.
CHAPTER 10 · BUY BOX DISCIPLINE

10. Pricing Strategy & Winning the Featured Offer

  • Respect Legal MRP: Landed price (item + shipping) above MRP or recent reference levels causes immediate Pricing Health suppression.
  • Buy Box Levers: Competitive total price, Prime delivery promise, ODR strictly <1%, LDR <4%, and reliable stock depth.
  • Use Coupons Smartly: Protect your 30-day deal reference floor with temporary digital Coupons rather than constant list-price drops.
CHAPTER 11 · UNIT ECONOMICS ENGINE

11. Amazon India Selling Fees & ₹799 Profit Math

Per sell.amazon.in/fees-and-pricing, total deductions combine a category Referral Fee (%), price-tier Closing Fee, zone/slab Weight Handling Fee (higher of dead or volumetric weight), optional FBA Storage, plus 18% GST on Amazon fees (typically offsettable as Input Tax Credit by regular GST sellers).

NET UNIT PROFIT
Pre-GST Revenue − COGS − Amazon Fees − Return Buffer − Ad Spend

Strip buyer output GST first (Price ÷ (1 + GST%)) before deducting landed COGS.

BREAK-EVEN ACOS
(Pre-Ad Contribution ÷ Selling Price) × 100

Max ACoS percentage an ad order can tolerate before losing contribution cash.

VOLUMETRIC WEIGHT (KG)
(Length × Breadth × Height cm) ÷ 5000

Amazon charges shipping weight on the greater of actual vs volumetric box weight.

CHAPTER 12 · SETTLEMENTS & TAXES

12. How Amazon Sellers Receive Payments

Amazon India disburses net proceeds directly via NEFT/RTGS to your verified bank account once mandate checks and performance thresholds clear.

  • 7-Day Payout Cycle & Delivery Reserve: Eligible delivered order balances typically transfer every 7 days, subject to post-delivery return window holds (commonly DD+7 on newer or specific tiers). Track exact dates under Reports → Payments.
  • Statutory TCS & TDS: Amazon deducts CGST TCS (Section 52, claimable in your GST Electronic Cash Ledger) and Income Tax TDS (Section 194-O, reflected in Form 26AS / AIS against your PAN).
  • Account Level Reserve: Amazon temporarily withholds a rolling buffer during open A-to-z claims, chargebacks, or sharp new-account order spikes.
  • Monthly CA Reconciliation: Export MTR / B2C Tax Reports and Seller Fee Tax Invoices monthly to reconcile sales, returns, TCS/TDS credits, and 18% Input Tax Credit cleanly.
CHAPTER 13 · ORDERS, INVENTORY & RETURNS

13. Managing Orders, Inventory and Returns

  • Dispatch Routine (Easy Ship / Self-Ship): Attach GST buyer invoices, pack crush-resistant boxes cleanly, and hand parcels over before pickup cutoffs to protect your Late Dispatch Rate.
  • Stockout Prevention: Running out of inventory resets organic keyword velocity. Balance supplier + FBA inbound lead times while keeping your IPI score comfortably above 400.
  • Returns, COD RTO & SAFE-T Claims: Cash-on-Delivery boosts Tier-2/3 reach but increases RTO refusals. Audit Voice of the Customer (NCX) weekly, shoot unboxing photos for damaged/switched Easy Ship or Self-Ship returns, and file SAFE-T claims within deadline.
REORDER POINT FORMULA & ILLUSTRATIVE WORKED EXAMPLE
Reorder Point = (Average Daily Units Sold × Total Lead Time in Days) + Safety Buffer Stock

Hypothetical Example: If an FBA hero SKU sells 15 units/day, production + inbound check-in takes 20 days, and you carry a 10-day safety buffer (150 units), your reorder trigger is (15 × 20) + 150 = 450 units.

CHAPTER 15 · SCALING PLAYBOOK

15. How to Increase Sales on Amazon India

Profitable scale rests on five structured levers—not blind price slashing:

  1. Fix Conversion Rate (CVR) First: A 15% CVR listing earns twice the orders per paid click of a 7.5% page. Sharpen main imagery and resolve review objections via A+ Content.
  2. Build Compliant Reviews: Enrol brand launches in Amazon Vine and trigger Seller Central’s Request a Review after delivery.
  3. Run Targeted Promos: Green Coupons raise search CTR, while Lightning Deals and festive events (see our Great Indian Festival Playbook) amplify existing winners.
  4. Expand Regional FBA Nodes: Split inventory across North, West, and South hubs to drop shipping slabs and offer 1–2 day Prime delivery.
  5. Layer PPC + Off-Amazon Attribution: Combine high-intent search ads with external traffic (How to Increase Sales on Amazon) to compound organic rank.

CHAPTER 16 · ADVERTISING & TACOS ENGINE

16. Introduction to Amazon Advertising (Amazon PPC)

While ads are optional, Sponsored placements dominate first-screen mobile search on Amazon.in. Amazon PPC runs a cost-per-click (CPC) auction across three core formats:

  • Sponsored Products: Keyword and ASIN-targeted search and product-page ads—run separate Auto Discovery and Manual Exact/Phrase campaigns.
  • Sponsored Brands (Brand Registered): Headline banner and video placements featuring your brand logo and multi-SKU showcase to defend brand intent.
  • Sponsored Display: Retargets listing browsers who bounced without buying and defends your own detail pages from rival ads.
ACoS (AD COST OF SALES)
(Ad Spend ÷ Ad Revenue) × 100

Direct campaign cost ratio. Always keep below unit Break-Even ACoS.

ROAS (RETURN ON AD SPEND)
Ad Revenue ÷ Ad Spend

Gross ad revenue per ₹1 spent (e.g., 4.0x ROAS equals 25% ACoS).

TACoS (TOTAL ACOS)
(Ad Spend ÷ Total Business Sales) × 100

Measures whether paid ads compound organic rank and catalog profit.

CHAPTER 17 · BRAND REGISTRY & IP

17. Brand Building and Brand Protection on Amazon

Filing an Indian wordmark or logo trademark via IP India qualifies you for Amazon Brand Registry—often even with a Pending filing receipt—unlocking growth tools and anti-hijacker controls:

CHAPTER 18 · PITFALLS TO AVOID

18. Common Mistakes Beginners Must Avoid

Beginner Mistake Why It Hurts Cash Flow or Health Practitioner Habit
1. Pricing on Referral Fee alone Ignoring closing fees, 18% fee GST, volumetric slabs, and COD returns wipes out thin margins. Model every deduction in a unit economics sheet before ordering stock.
2. Attaching offers to private-label ASINs Mapping onto trademarked listings without authorised GST invoices triggers instant IP blocks. Verify brand ownership or source solely from authorised GST distributors.
3. One catch-all Auto campaign Mixes unprofitable broad clicks with winners and burns budget silently. Split campaigns by SKU margin tier, isolate Exact heroes, and negate weekly.
4. Mixing taxable & exempt goods Even one taxable SKU voids exempt-seller status, risking tax compliance holds. Secure a home-state GSTIN unless 100% of your catalog is legally exempt.
5. Repeated hero SKU stockouts Outages beyond 7–14 days erase sales velocity and cede Page-1 rank. Trigger POs strictly at your calculated Reorder Point + safety buffer.
6. Weak white-bg hero renders Low search CTR inflates CPC bids and hurts TACoS from Week 1. Shoot crisp mobile-first renders and clear 3–4 slide benefit infographics.

Portal (sellercentral.amazon.in) for listings, orders, payouts, and ads.
4. Featured Offer (Buy Box)
Main “Add to Cart / Buy Now” slot won via price, speed, and account health.
5. FBA
Fulfillment by Amazon: Amazon stores, packs, ships Prime orders, and handles returns.
6. Amazon Easy Ship
You store & pack locally; Amazon’s courier picks up and delivers parcels.
7. Self-Ship (MFN)
Seller stores, packs, and dispatches via integrated 3PL couriers (VTR > 95%).
8. GSTIN
15-digit Goods and Services Tax ID tied to your PAN and dispatch state.
9. HSN Code
Product tax classification code setting your SKU’s GST rate slab.
10. TCS (Section 52 CGST)
E-commerce Tax Collected at Source, credited in your GST Cash Ledger.
11. TDS (Section 194-O)
Income tax deducted on gross marketplace sales, claimable in ITR / AIS.
12. APOB / VPOB
Additional / Virtual Place of Business added to GST for multi-state FBA hubs.
13. GTIN / GTIN Exemption
GS1 EAN/UPC barcode or Amazon brand waiver allowing barcode-free listings.
14. Referral Fee
Category percentage commission charged per unit on total selling price.
15. Closing Fee
Fixed rupee charge per order varying by price bracket and fulfilment channel.
16. Weight Handling Fee
Shipping charge set by delivery zone and greater of dead vs volumetric weight.
17. A10 / COSMO Signals
Amazon’s search ranking systems rewarding relevance, CTR, CVR, and Prime speed.
18. Unit Session % (CVR)
Units ordered divided by listing sessions—key gauge of page conversion strength.
19. ACoS & ROAS
Ad Spend ÷ Ad Revenue (ACoS) and Ad Revenue ÷ Ad Spend (ROAS).
20. TACoS
Ad Spend ÷ Total Business Revenue (Paid + Organic), tracking holistic ad impact.
21. ODR (Order Defect Rate)
Share of orders with claims, negative feedback, or chargebacks; limit < 1%.
22. IPI Score
FBA Inventory Performance Index measuring sell-through and overstock health.
23. SAFE-T Claim
Reimbursement claim for Easy Ship / Self-Ship items returned damaged or switched.
24. Amazon Brand Registry
Trademark verification unlocking A+, Brand Stores, Vine, and IP takedown tools.
FAQ ACCORDION · 12 KEY ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Can I sell on Amazon India without GST?+

Selling taxable commercial goods on Amazon.in generally requires an active GSTIN due to Section 52 CGST TCS collection. Limited exceptions exist solely for 100% GST-exempt goods (such as printed books) or eligible intra-state non-GST Enrolment ID rules available at onboarding. Always confirm with a Chartered Accountant.

2. How much investment is required to start selling on Amazon India?+

Seller account registration costs ₹0 in fixed monthly fees. Illustrative capital goes into compliance, initial stock, visuals, and ads: micro resellers often test with ₹20,000–₹45,000, while single-SKU private-label brands typically budget ₹75,000–₹1,75,000 including a 60-day PPC runway.

3. What documents are required to register as an Amazon seller in India?+

Keep an active PAN card, home-state GSTIN certificate (for taxable goods), active bank statement or cancelled cheque in the identical legal name, Video KYC ID (Aadhaar/Passport/Voter ID), email, phone number, and serviceable pickup address.

4. How does Amazon FBA work compared to Easy Ship in India?+

With Easy Ship, you store and pack orders yourself while Amazon’s courier handles pickup and delivery. With FBA, you ship bulk stock to Amazon warehouses so Amazon stores, packs, delivers, and handles returns—unlocking the high-converting Prime badge.

5. How long does Amazon seller registration and verification take?+

Online registration takes 20–30 minutes, while automated verification and Seller Identity Video KYC typically clear in 2 to 7 working days once legal names, bank details, and addresses align cleanly.

6. Can I sell products on Amazon India without owning a trademark or brand?+

Yes—you can resell authorised brands (with valid GST invoices) or launch generic products via GTIN Exemption. For private-label brands, however, trademark filing and Brand Registry are vital for A+ Content and hijacker defence.

7. How and when do Amazon India sellers receive payments?+

Net proceeds transfer via NEFT/RTGS directly to your verified bank account—typically on a 7-day cycle for eligible delivered orders—after fees, 18% fee GST, Section 52 TCS, Section 194-O TDS, and applicable delivery reserves.

8. What is Amazon India’s total selling fee percentage?+

Your deduction combines a category Referral Fee (%), price-tier Closing Fee, zone/weight Shipping Fee, and optional FBA Storage—plus 18% GST on Amazon fees. Together this typically totals ~18% to ~32%+ of selling price; check Seller Central Fee Preview for live rates.

9. Do beginners need Amazon PPC ads from Day 1?+

Ads are optional, not compulsory. Still, Sponsored Products occupy top mobile slots, so controlled Exact Match and Auto discovery budgets help new listings generate clicks and sales velocity far faster than organic waiting alone.

10. What is the difference between ACoS, ROAS, and TACoS?+

ACoS is Ad Spend ÷ Ad Revenue (campaign cost ratio); ROAS is Ad Revenue ÷ Ad Spend; and TACoS is Ad Spend ÷ Total Business Revenue (Paid + Organic), measuring overall brand-level profitability.

11. What happens when a buyer cancels Cash-on-Delivery or returns a damaged item?+

RTO/returned orders reverse order proceeds and may incur logistics fees. When Easy Ship or Self-Ship items return damaged or switched, document unboxing photos/video immediately and file a SAFE-T claim within Seller Central’s window.
