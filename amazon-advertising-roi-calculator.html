Why ROAS Is Not The Same As Advertising ROI

ROAS measures gross revenue divided by ad spend. However, revenue is not profit. Advertising ROI measures the net profit generated relative to the ad spend invested.

Advertising ROI = (Net Profit Attributable After All Variable Costs & Ads ÷ Ad Spend) × 100
