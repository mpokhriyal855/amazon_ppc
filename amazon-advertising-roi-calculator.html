QUICK SUMMARY

ROAS tells you how much advertising revenue was generated for each ₹1 spent. Advertising ROI goes one step further by accounting for the contribution available after product and variable selling costs, then comparing remaining contribution with the advertising investment.

⚠️ A 4x ROAS does NOT mean 300% profit.

ADVANCED STRATEGY CONCEPT

⚠️ ROI % vs. Absolute Contribution

Optimizing purely for higher ROI percentage can encourage under-spending. A smaller spend may yield 100% ROI (₹10k spend → ₹10k profit), while scaling spend to ₹50k at 50% ROI yields ₹25k net contribution.

The ultimate goal is maximizing total net contribution rupees, not just ROI percentage alone.

FINAL TAKEAWAY

Revenue Is Not Profit — Connect Your Ads to Product Economics

ROAS answers how much revenue ads generated. Advertising ROI answers whether enough contribution remained after product costs and advertising spend. The goal is not simply the highest ROAS or highest ROI percentage — it is profitable advertising that produces meaningful absolute contribution.

How to Use the Amazon Advertising ROI Calculator

Enter your advertising spend and ad-attributed sales, then add either a pre-ad contribution margin percentage or itemized product costs. The calculator measures revenue efficiency using ROAS and ACoS, then calculates the contribution remaining after advertising and your Advertising Contribution ROI.

Amazon Advertising ROI vs. ROAS

Metric Formula What It Measures
ROAS Ad Sales ÷ Ad Spend Top-line revenue efficiency per ₹1 spent.
Advertising ROI (Contribution After Ads ÷ Ad Spend) × 100 Net contribution return remaining after product costs & ads.

Common Mistakes in Amazon Ad Profit Calculations

  • Ignoring Product Costs: Calculating `(Sales − Spend) / Spend` ignores landed COGS and Amazon fees.
  • Treating ROAS as Profit: Assuming a 4x ROAS equals 300% profit when pre-ad margin is only 20%.
  • Optimizing ROI % Alone: Under-spending to achieve 100% ROI percentage instead of maximizing absolute net rupees.
