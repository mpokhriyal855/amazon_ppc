How Amazon India Profitability Is Calculated

Selling on Amazon India requires factoring in multiple fee slabs, product manufacturing costs, logistics, and PPC spend to understand true unit economics.

Net Profit = Selling Price - (COGS + Referral Fee + Closing Fee + FBA Fee + Ad Spend + Shipping + Tax)

Key Cost Components Explained

  • Referral Fee: Category-based commission charged by Amazon (typically 5% to 18%).
  • Closing Fee: Fee based on selling price slabs.
  • FBA Fulfilment Fee: Weight and dimension-based pick, pack, and delivery cost.
  • Ad Spend per Sale: Your Amazon PPC cost to generate a purchase (ACoS × Selling Price).
How this calculation works & assumptions +
This model calculates unit contribution margin based on user-entered costs. It assumes all variable costs per unit are accounted for and does not include fixed overhead costs like software subscriptions or staff salaries.

Amazon India Profitability FAQs

What is a good net profit margin on Amazon India? +
A healthy Amazon India net margin typically ranges between 15% and 25% after PPC advertising and Amazon fees.
Does this include GST input credit? +
You can enter net GST cost or zero if you claim 100% input tax credit (ITC) on Amazon marketplace fee invoices.
