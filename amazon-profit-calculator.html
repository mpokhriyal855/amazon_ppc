    What is an Amazon Profit Calculator?

    Direct answer: An Amazon Profit Calculator is a tool that subtracts Amazon selling fees, fulfillment costs, product cost, advertising spend and return losses from your selling price to show the net profit and net margin you actually keep on each unit sold.

    Most sellers know their selling price and their product cost. The gap between those two numbers feels like profit — and it almost never is. Between the two sit a referral fee, a closing fee, a fulfillment fee, GST on those fees, packaging, return shipping and, on a competitive marketplace, advertising. An Amazon seller calculator exists to make that gap visible before you commit to a price, a launch or a PPC budget.

    This tool goes one step beyond a standard Amazon fee calculator. It also answers the question that decides whether a product can survive on Amazon at all: after all of that, how much of my selling price is still available to spend on ads? That number is your break-even ACOS, and it is the single most useful figure an Amazon PPC manager works with.

    How to calculate Amazon seller profit

    Formula: Net Profit = Selling Price − Product Cost − Amazon Fees − Advertising Cost − Return/Loss Cost − Other Costs.
    Net Profit = Selling Price − Product Cost − Amazon Fees − Ad Cost − Returns − Other
    Net Margin = Net Profit ÷ Selling Price × 100
    Contribution = Selling Price − everything except advertising

    Work in this order and the maths stays honest:

    1. Start with the price the customer pays. Not your MRP, not the pre-discount price — the actual transacting price.
    2. Remove product-side costs: landed unit cost, packaging, inward freight, labelling.
    3. Remove Amazon fees: referral, closing, fulfillment, storage, plus GST on those fees if you cannot claim input credit.
    4. Remove return losses: return rate × (return shipping + unsellable value).
    5. What remains is your contribution margin — the money advertising is allowed to eat.
    6. Remove advertising last. What survives is real net profit per unit.

    Advertising is deliberately last. It is the only cost on the list you can change tomorrow morning, which is why it deserves its own decision rather than being buried in a blended cost figure.

    What Amazon fees affect your profit?

    An Amazon selling fee calculator has to handle four fee families. Rates vary by category, price slab, weight and zone, so treat the numbers in the tool as configurable assumptions and confirm your own in Seller Central.

    FeeWhat it isDriven by
    Referral feeAmazon's commission on the saleCategory and price slab
    Closing feeFixed fee per orderPrice slab and fulfillment method
    Fulfillment feePick, pack and deliveryWeight, size tier, shipping zone
    Storage feeSpace your stock occupiesVolume × months stored (FBA)
    GST on feesTax charged on Amazon's feesApplicable GST rate

    Two details cost sellers real money. First, referral fee percentages usually change at price thresholds, so a ₹1,010 price can carry a materially higher fee than ₹990. Second, if you are GST-registered you generally claim input credit on Amazon's fees — the calculator lets you switch GST between "cost" and "recoverable" so your margin is not understated.

    How does Amazon FBA affect profit?

    With Fulfillment by Amazon you hand over storage, picking, packing, delivery and most return handling. You pay for it through a pick & pack component, a weight handling component that scales with the heavier of dead weight or volumetric weight, and a storage charge for every month the unit sits in the fulfillment centre.

    FBA usually improves conversion rate through Prime badging and faster delivery promises, and a higher conversion rate directly raises the CPC you can afford. So an Amazon FBA calculator that only compares fees will mislead you. Model it properly: compare FBA fees and the conversion rate FBA earns you against Easy Ship fees at your current conversion rate. Slow-moving stock is where FBA turns expensive — storage compounds monthly while the referral fee does not.

    How does Amazon Easy Ship affect profit?

    With Easy Ship you hold and pack the stock yourself, and Amazon collects from your location and delivers to the customer. You avoid storage fees entirely and pay a weight- and zone-based shipping fee instead of the full FBA stack. The trade-offs are operational: your packing quality drives your damage returns, and you carry the working capital of inventory sitting at your own warehouse.

    As a rough rule, Easy Ship tends to suit low-velocity, bulky or seasonal products, while FBA suits fast-moving, compact, high-repeat items. Run both through the calculator with the same product and read the break-even ACOS for each — that comparison is more decisive than the fee difference alone.

    How is Amazon PPC cost included in profit?

    Advertising enters unit economics as a percentage of revenue, not as a fixed rupee amount. If your ACOS is 20%, then ₹200 of every ₹1,000 in ad-driven sales goes to Amazon Ads. In this Amazon PPC calculator, per-unit ad cost is calculated as:

    Ad Cost per unit = Selling Price × ACOS ÷ 100

    This assumes the unit was sold through advertising. In a real account, some sales are organic, so blended ad cost per unit sold is lower than ACOS suggests — which is exactly what TACOS measures. Use the strict version for launch planning and pricing decisions: it is the conservative case, and a product that survives it will survive reality.

    What is break-even ACOS?

    Direct answer: Break-even ACOS is the maximum percentage of sales that can be spent on Amazon advertising before advertising reduces the product's profit to approximately zero.
    Break-even ACOS = (Profit before advertising ÷ Selling Price) × 100

    Worked example: a ₹1,000 product with ₹700 of non-advertising costs leaves ₹300 of contribution. Break-even ACOS is 300 ÷ 1,000 = 30%. Spend 30% of sales on ads and you make roughly nothing. Spend 45% and you are buying orders at a loss, no matter how good the sales chart looks.

    This single number decides your bidding posture. Above roughly 30% break-even ACOS you have room to compete on high-intent keywords. Below 15%, advertising can only work on tightly targeted, high-converting terms — and the answer is usually to fix price, cost or conversion rate before spending more. A structured Amazon PPC audit normally starts here rather than with bids.

    How do you calculate maximum CPC?

    Formula: Maximum CPC = Selling Price × Target ACOS × Conversion Rate.
    Max CPC = 1,000 × 20% × 10% = ₹20

    Read it as a chain: a click is only worth what it converts into. At a 10% conversion rate, ten clicks produce one order worth ₹1,000; if you are allowed to spend 20% of that, you have ₹200 for ten clicks — ₹20 per click. Halve the conversion rate to 5% and your maximum CPC halves to ₹10, with nothing else changed.

    What is TACOS?

    Direct answer: TACOS (Total Advertising Cost of Sales) is ad spend divided by total revenue — ads plus organic — expressed as a percentage. ACOS judges the campaign; TACOS judges the business.
    TACOS = (Ad Spend ÷ Total Sales) × 100

    A healthy scaling brand often shows steady or rising ACOS with falling TACOS: advertising is buying rank and reviews, and organic sales are compounding underneath. Rising TACOS with flat revenue is the warning sign — you are renting sales rather than building them.

    How do returns affect Amazon profit?

    A return costs more than the refund. You lose the return shipping, you may lose the unit if it comes back unsellable, and the labour of processing it is real even when it is invisible. Model it per unit sold:

    Return cost per unit sold = Return Rate × (Return Shipping + Unsellable Loss)

    Amazon Profit Calculator — FAQs

    Short answers to what Amazon sellers ask most about fees, ACOS and profit.

    What is an Amazon Profit Calculator?
    A tool that subtracts Amazon fees, fulfillment costs, product cost, advertising and return losses from your selling price to show net profit and net margin per unit.
    How do I calculate Amazon profit?
    Net Profit = Selling Price − Product Cost − Amazon Fees − Advertising Cost − Return Loss − Other Costs. Divide by selling price for net margin.
    What fees does Amazon charge sellers?
    Mainly a referral fee (category-based commission), a closing fee (fixed per order), a fulfillment fee (FBA or Easy Ship), storage fees for FBA, and GST on those fees.
    How does Amazon FBA affect profit?
    FBA adds pick & pack, weight handling and storage fees, but usually lifts conversion rate through Prime delivery — which raises the CPC you can afford.
    How does Easy Ship affect Amazon profit?
    Easy Ship removes storage fees and charges a weight- and zone-based shipping fee. You keep inventory and packing in-house, so your working capital and damage rate matter more.
    What is break-even ACOS?
    The maximum share of sales you can spend on ads before profit reaches approximately zero. Above it, every advertised order loses money.
    What is TACOS?
    Total Advertising Cost of Sales: ad spend ÷ total (ad + organic) sales × 100. Falling TACOS while revenue grows is the sign of healthy scaling.
    How do I calculate maximum CPC?
    Maximum CPC = Selling Price × Target ACOS × Conversion Rate. At ₹1,000, 20% target ACOS and 10% CVR, your ceiling is ₹20 per click.
    What conversion rate do I need for profitable Amazon PPC?
    Required CVR = CPC ÷ (Selling Price × Target ACOS). Higher CVR allows higher CPC bids while holding your target margin.
