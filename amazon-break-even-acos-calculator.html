What does this mean?
If your PPC campaign ACoS is lower than your Break-Even ACoS, your ad sales generate profit.

What Is Break-Even ACoS?

Break-Even ACoS is equal to your pre-advertising profit margin percentage. It tells you the exact point at which your ad spend equals your profit, resulting in zero net profit or loss on ad sales.

How Is It Calculated?

Break-Even ACoS % = (Pre-Ad Profit Margin per Unit ÷ Selling Price) × 100
