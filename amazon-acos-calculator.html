OPTIMIZATION STRATEGY

How Can I Improve My Amazon ACoS?

Improving ACoS means improving the relationship between advertising spend and attributed sales. The right action depends on where efficiency is being lost.

1. Traffic Quality

  • Search terms audit
  • Keyword relevance check
  • Product targeting alignment
  • Negative keyword expansion

Stop paying for traffic that has little realistic chance of purchasing your product.

2. CPC & Bids

  • Actual CPC vs Bid alignment
  • Bid adjustments by match type
  • Placement modifier testing
  • Target-level performance bids

Reduce excessive click costs where performance does not justify them, while protecting high-value traffic.

3. Conversion Rate

  • Main image optimization
  • Title & bullet clarity
  • Review rating & count health
  • Competitive pricing & coupons

Relevant paid traffic becomes expensive when the product page cannot convert it efficiently.

4. Budget Allocation

  • Campaign profitability scaling
  • Profitable target isolation
  • Budget-limited winner expansion
  • High-spend weak area trimming

Move available budget toward stronger opportunities instead of treating every campaign equally.

LOWER ACOS IS NOT ALWAYS THE ONLY GOAL

A campaign with a slightly higher ACoS may still create more valuable sales or support a strategic growth objective. Evaluate ACoS together with margin, ROAS, TACoS, conversion and total sales.

METRIC COMPARISON

ACoS, ROAS and TACoS — What's the Difference?

Metric Answers Formula
ACoS How much ad spend was required relative to attributed ad sales? (Spend ÷ Ad Sales) × 100
ROAS How much attributed ad revenue was generated per ₹1 spent? Ad Sales ÷ Spend
TACoS How large is advertising spend relative to total Amazon sales? (Spend ÷ Total Sales) × 100
ACoS and ROAS describe the same ad-spend/ad-sales relationship from opposite directions. TACoS introduces total sales for a wider business view.

ACoS Formula

ACoS = (Ad Spend ÷ Ad Sales) × 100
Worked Example:
Ad Spend = ₹15,000 | Ad Sales = ₹60,000
ACoS = (15,000 ÷ 60,000) × 100 = 25%
ROAS = 60,000 ÷ 15,000 = 4.0x

What Is a Good Amazon ACoS?

There is no single ACoS percentage that is good for every Amazon seller. The appropriate level depends on your contribution margin, product price, category competitiveness, conversion rate, product lifecycle, campaign objective, and growth strategy.

FINAL TAKEAWAY

Don't Optimize ACoS in Isolation

ACoS tells you how much advertising spend was required relative to attributed ad sales. It does not, by itself, tell you whether the campaign is profitable, scalable or strategically valuable.

ACoS + ROAS + BREAK-EVEN ACOS + CONVERSION + TACOS = BETTER PPC DECISIONS

The goal isn't simply the lowest possible ACoS. The goal is advertising efficiency that supports profitable, sustainable Amazon growth.

What Is Amazon ACoS?

ACoS stands for Advertising Cost of Sales. It measures advertising spend as a percentage of sales attributed to advertising.

Why Does ACoS Increase?

  • CPC rises due to increased bid competition
  • Conversion rate falls due to listing or stock issues
  • Irrelevant traffic increases through broad targeting leakage
  • Placement modifiers push top-of-search bids excessively high
  • Listing competitiveness weakens relative to category peers
  • Budget shifts toward unproven or lower-intent keywords

Is Lower ACoS Always Better?

No. Lower ACoS improves advertising efficiency mathematically, but the business impact depends on sales volume, margin and campaign objective.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

ACoS Calculator FAQs

What does Amazon ACoS mean? +
ACoS (Advertising Cost of Sales) represents the percentage of attributed ad sales spent on advertising. For example, spending ₹25 to generate ₹100 in ad sales equals a 25% ACoS.
How do I calculate Amazon ACoS? +
Divide total ad spend by total attributed ad sales, then multiply by 100. (e.g. ₹25,000 ÷ ₹1,00,000 × 100 = 25%).
Is a lower ACoS always better? +
Not necessarily. A lower ACoS means higher ad efficiency per sale, but extremely low ACoS targets can restrict total ad sales volume, BSR keyword ranking velocity, and brand growth.
What is break-even ACoS? +
Break-even ACoS is the exact ACoS percentage equal to your product contribution margin before advertising. At break-even ACoS, your ad sales generate zero net profit and zero net loss.
What is the difference between ACoS and ROAS? +
ACoS and ROAS describe the same metric from opposite directions. ACoS is expressed as a percentage (Spend ÷ Sales), while ROAS is a multiplier (Sales ÷ Spend). A 25% ACoS is equal to a 4.00x ROAS.
Why is my ACoS high? +
High ACoS is caused by three core factors: high Cost Per Click (CPC), low conversion rate (CVR), or irrelevant search term targeting that spends budget without producing orders.
