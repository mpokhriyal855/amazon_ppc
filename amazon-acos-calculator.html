What Is Amazon ACoS?

Advertising Cost of Sales (ACoS) is a core metric used to measure the efficiency of your Amazon PPC campaigns. It represents the percentage of attributed ad revenue spent on advertising.

How Is ACoS Calculated?

The formula for Amazon ACoS is:

ACoS = (Total Ad Spend ÷ Total Attributed Ad Sales) × 100

What Is a Good ACoS on Amazon?

There is no single universal "good" ACoS. A healthy target depends entirely on your product margin, strategy, and business goals:

  • Profitability Target: Keep ACoS below your Break-Even ACoS threshold.
  • Product Launch Target: Accept higher ACoS (40%–80%) to generate sales velocity and review momentum.
  • Brand Defense Target: Maintain lower ACoS (10%–20%) on high-converting branded search terms.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

ACoS Calculator FAQs

What does ACoS stand for on Amazon? +
ACoS stands for Advertising Cost of Sales. It indicates what percentage of your direct advertising revenue was spent on PPC ad clicks.
How is ACoS different from ROAS? +
ACoS is expressed as an inverse percentage (Ad Spend ÷ Ad Sales), whereas ROAS is expressed as a multiplier (Ad Sales ÷ Ad Spend). A 25% ACoS is equal to a 4.0x ROAS.
Why is my ACoS rising suddenly? +
Rising ACoS is usually driven by higher Cost Per Click (CPC), reduced conversion rates, increased bid competition, or seasonal search behavior changes.
