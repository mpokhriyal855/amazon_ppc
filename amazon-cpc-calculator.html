What does this mean?
CPC measures traffic acquisition cost. Compare CPC with your conversion rate to determine average Cost Per Acquisition (CPA).

What Is Amazon CPC?

Cost Per Click (CPC) is the actual amount charged by Amazon when a shopper clicks your sponsored ad.

Formula:

CPC = Total Ad Spend ÷ Total Ad Clicks
