Complete Guide to Amazon CPC & Bid Management in India

Cost-per-click is the price you pay each time a shopper clicks your Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands or Sponsored Display ad. Bidding above your break-even CPC burns margin on every click; bidding too far below it starves your listing of the visibility it needs to rank and convert.

1. Break-Even CPC vs Target CPC

Break-even CPC is the highest bid you can pay before a sale stops being profitable — it uses your full pre-ad contribution margin. Target CPC builds in a buffer for your desired net margin after ads, so it's the safer number to actually set as your default bid.

2. Why Conversion Rate Moves Your Bid Ceiling More Than Price

Because Maximum CPC = Revenue per Click × Break-Even ACoS, and Revenue per Click = Selling Price × CVR, a 1 percentage-point gain in conversion rate has the same effect on your bid ceiling as raising price by the same relative amount — but without touching your competitiveness on the page.

3. Match Type and Effective CPC

Broad match without a mature negative-keyword list tends to push your effective (blended) CPC above your account's stated bid, because it also captures loosely related, lower-converting search terms. Moving proven keywords to exact match and layering in product targeting is usually the fastest way to bring effective CPC back toward the category benchmark.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Amazon CPC Calculator FAQs

What is the minimum budget for Amazon PPC in India? +
The absolute minimum is around ₹100/day per campaign, but we recommend at least ₹500–1,000/day (₹15K–30K/month) to gather meaningful data and see results. For competitive categories like electronics, budget ₹2,000–5,000/day.
How much does Amazon PPC cost per click in India? +
Average CPC ranges from ₹2–35 depending on category. Low-competition categories like books average ₹2–5, while electronics can reach ₹15–35. The platform-wide average is roughly ₹8–12 per click.
What is a good ACoS for Amazon India? +
A good ACoS depends on your profit margin. Generally, 15–25% ACoS is considered good for most categories. During product launch, 30–40% ACoS is acceptable to build velocity. Our PPC management team targets under 20% ACoS for established products.
How is maximum CPC calculated? +
Maximum (break-even) CPC is calculated by multiplying Revenue per Click (Selling Price × Conversion Rate) by Break-Even ACoS. Target CPC uses your target ACoS instead.
Should I hire an Amazon PPC agency? +
If you're spending over ₹50K/month on ads, a professional Amazon PPC agency typically pays for itself through better ACoS optimization and higher ROAS versus a DIY approach managed part-time.
