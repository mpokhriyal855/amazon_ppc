How to Estimate Your Amazon PPC Budget

Setting an ad budget based on arbitrary numbers leads to budget exhaustion or underfunded campaigns. By tying budget to revenue targets and ACoS targets, you maintain controlled growth.

Formula:

Monthly Ad Budget = (Target Monthly Sales × PPC Share %) × (Target ACoS %)
