What does this mean?
TACoS measures how heavily your total Amazon brand sales rely on PPC ad dollars. Lower or stabilizing TACoS over time indicates strong organic keyword ranking velocity.

What Is Amazon TACoS?

Total Advertising Cost of Sales (TACoS) measures advertising spend relative to total account revenue on Amazon (both organic and paid sales combined).

Formula:

TACoS = (Total Ad Spend ÷ Total Amazon Revenue) × 100
