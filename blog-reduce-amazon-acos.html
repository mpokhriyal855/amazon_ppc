Why Is My Amazon PPC ACoS So High and How Can I Reduce It?

If your Amazon PPC campaigns are getting clicks and spending money but generating fewer orders than expected, high ACoS is usually a sign of a deeper problem—not simply a bidding problem. High ACoS can come from irrelevant search terms, high CPCs, poor keyword targeting, weak conversion rates, inefficient campaign structure, poor placements, incorrect budget allocation, or even your product listing. This guide breaks down where your ad spend may be losing efficiency, why shoppers aren't converting, and what you should optimize before simply lowering your bids.

What Is Amazon PPC ACoS & Why Does It Matter?

Amazon PPC ACoS — Advertising Cost of Sales — measures how much you spend on advertising compared with the sales generated from those ads.

ACOS FORMULA
ACoS = (Ad Spend ÷ Ad Sales) × 100
₹10,000
Ad Spend
÷
₹40,000
Ad Sales
× 100 =
25% ACoS

This means advertising spend represents 25% of the attributed advertising sales in this example. ACoS is useful for measuring advertising efficiency, but it should not be judged alone.

Also consider: ROAS · CTR · CVR · Impressions · Profit Margin · Business Objective

💡 KEY TAKEAWAY

A lower ACoS is not automatically a better business outcome. Campaign decisions should also consider profitability, scale, organic sales, product lifecycle and the objective of the campaign.

What Is a Good Amazon PPC ACoS?

There is no single ACoS percentage that is good for every Amazon seller.

PRODUCT MARGIN How much room does the product have for ad costs?
SELLING PRICE Higher and lower ticket products require different economics.
CATEGORY Competition varies significantly between Amazon categories.
COMPETITION More aggressive auctions can increase CPC.
CONVERSION RATE Better conversion makes paid traffic significantly more efficient.
PRODUCT LIFECYCLE Launch products and established products have different goals.
ADVERTISING OBJECTIVE Profitability, ranking, launch and growth require different strategies.
ORGANIC SALES Paid and organic performance should be understood together.
BRAND GROWTH Sometimes advertising supports goals beyond immediate attributed sales.
💡 IMPORTANT

A new product may intentionally operate at a higher ACoS while building visibility and collecting keyword data. An established product with strong organic rankings may have a very different target.

YOUR BREAK-EVEN ACOS MATTERS

Your break-even ACoS helps determine how much advertising cost your product can support before advertising spend consumes the available profit margin.

Can a Poor Amazon Listing Increase PPC ACoS?

Yes.

A weak product listing can contribute to poor PPC conversion performance and therefore higher ACoS. Amazon PPC can bring shoppers to your product page, but PPC cannot force the shopper to buy.

📋 Listing Conversion Audit

05 — REVIEWS

06 — PRICE

When paid traffic is relevant but conversion remains weak, don't automatically keep changing the PPC campaign. Investigate the product page too. Your PPC strategy and listing strategy should support each other.

2. You Are Targeting the Wrong Keywords

Another common reason for high ACoS is keyword relevance. Suppose you're selling a wireless gaming mouse, but broad targeting is bringing shoppers whose search intent is only loosely related to your product.

Irrelevant / Weak Search Intent
Impression → Click → Advertising Spend → No Order ✕

That spend increases ACoS without generating meaningful revenue.

WHAT SHOULD YOU LOOK FOR IN SEARCH TERM REPORTS?

🔴 HIGH-SPEND TERMS Spending significantly without generating orders.
⚫ IRRELEVANT TERMS Search intent doesn't match the product.
🟢 WINNING TERMS Generating consistent conversions.
🟡 HIGH CTR + LOW CVR People click, but something stops them from buying.
⭐ NEW OPPORTUNITIES Search terms worth investing in further.
🎯 EXACT-MATCH CANDIDATES Strong search terms that deserve dedicated targeting.
💡 KEY TAKEAWAY

Keyword optimization should be based on actual customer search behaviour — not only the keywords originally added to the campaign.

3. Your Bids Are Too High

High bids can increase CPC and consume advertising budget quickly. But there is an important distinction: A HIGH BID IS NOT AUTOMATICALLY BAD. If a keyword generates profitable sales, a higher bid may be justified.

THE QUESTION YOU SHOULD ASK

Is the keyword generating enough value to justify the CPC you're paying?

Bid → CPC → Clicks → Orders → Sales → ACoS → Profitability
⚠️ COMMON MISTAKE

Reducing every bid by the same percentage because overall ACoS is high. Different keywords produce different results. They should not automatically receive identical bid adjustments.

How Can I Reduce Amazon PPC ACoS?

There is no single ACoS reduction trick that works for every account. Use a structured optimization process.

STEP 1

Analyze Campaign Performance

Start by identifying: Highest-Spend Campaigns, Highest-ACoS Campaigns, Highest-ROAS Campaigns, Low-Converting Campaigns, Campaigns Running Out of Budget, and Campaigns With Significant Unused Budget.

STEP 2

Analyze Search Terms

Search-term analysis is one of the most important parts of PPC optimization.

Optimize Bids

Don't reduce every bid just because ACoS is high. Evaluate performance at the individual target level.

STEP 4

Add Negative Keywords Where Appropriate

Negative keywords prevent ad spend from going toward irrelevant searches. Base negatives on DATA + RELEVANCE, not emotion.

STEP 5

Reallocate Your Budget

Move budget toward campaigns demonstrating stronger performance and meaningful growth potential.

STEP 6

Optimize the Listing

Images → Title → Bullets → A+ Content → Reviews → Price → Product Positioning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my Amazon PPC ACoS suddenly high?

A sudden increase in ACoS can result from higher CPC, lower conversion rates, changing competition, weak search-term performance, budget allocation, placement changes or listing-related conversion issues.

What is a good ACoS on Amazon?

There is no universal good ACoS. Your target should depend on your product margin, category, competition, product lifecycle and business objective.

How can I reduce Amazon PPC ACoS?

Start by identifying high-spend and low-converting search terms, optimizing bids, improving negative targeting, reallocating budgets, reviewing placement performance and checking whether your product listing converts paid traffic effectively.

Why do my Amazon PPC campaigns get clicks but no sales?

Possible causes include: Irrelevant traffic, Poor keyword targeting, High product price, Weak listing content, Low reviews, or Poor conversion rate.

Should I reduce bids if my ACoS is high?

Not necessarily. First determine why ACoS is high. A profitable keyword with a relatively high CPC may still deserve investment, while an inefficient keyword may require a bid reduction.

Can Amazon listing optimization help reduce PPC ACoS?

Yes! When weak listing conversion contributes to PPC inefficiency, optimizing main images, titles, bullets, A+ content, pricing, and reviews directly improves CVR and lowers ACoS.

Conclusion & Next Steps

Reducing Amazon ACoS is an ongoing process of search-term analysis, bid optimization, budget allocation, placement review and conversion improvement.

FINAL TAKEAWAY

Your goal shouldn't be to spend less on Amazon PPC.

Your goal should be to make your advertising spend work better.
