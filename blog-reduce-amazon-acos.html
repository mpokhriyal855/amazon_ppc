How to Reduce Amazon ACoS Without Killing Sales

Lowering your Advertising Cost of Sales doesn't mean slashing ad budgets blindly. Here is the step-by-step PPC optimization framework to cut wasted spend while protecting organic rank.

Anmol Pokhriyal Amazon PPC Growth Expert · Updated August 2026
✦ TL;DR — KEY SUMMARY
  • Lowering ACoS requires eliminating non-converting search terms, not pausing profitable high-volume keywords.
  • Isolate branded keywords from generic campaigns to prevent false ACoS deflation.
  • Optimize bids by placement (Top of Search vs Product Pages) using placement-modifier adjustments.
  • Always track TACoS alongside ACoS to ensure total contribution margin grows.

What is Amazon ACoS & Why Does It Matter?

Amazon ACoS (Advertising Cost of Sales) is calculated by dividing your total ad spend by your total ad-generated revenue. For example, spending ₹1,800 to generate ₹10,000 in sponsored sales results in an ACoS of 18%.

Many Amazon sellers make the critical mistake of trying to lower ACoS by simply cutting campaign budgets across the board. However, doing so starves high-converting ASIN campaigns of impressions, causing organic ranking to drop alongside ad sales.

💡 KEY TAKEAWAY

Lower ACoS does not automatically mean higher profit. Campaign decisions must evaluate TACoS, organic keyword velocity, and contribution margin.

Why Your Amazon ACoS Keeps Increasing

In our audit of over 150+ Amazon Seller Central accounts, we consistently see four primary root causes for spiraling ad spend:

  • Unflagged Negative Search Terms: Auto and Broad campaigns spending money on irrelevant buyer queries.
  • Over-Bidding on Rest of Search: Bidding aggressively for middle-of-page placements with low conversion rates (CVR).
  • Blended Branded Spend: Mixing branded search queries with generic keywords, masking poor generic campaign ROAS.
  • Listing CVR Leakage: Driving paid traffic to listing pages with weak main images or unoptimized bullet points.
⚠️ COMMON MISTAKE

Pausing every keyword with an ACoS above target will destroy your search term discovery pipeline. Instead, isolate winning queries into dedicated Exact match campaigns first!

Step 1 — Search Term Analysis & Negative Harvesting

The fastest way to reduce wasted ad spend without impacting sales is to add negative exact match keywords for search terms that generate clicks without conversions.

🔥 PPC EXPERT TIP

Set a strict harvesting rule: Any search term with 8+ clicks and 0 orders should immediately be added as a Negative Exact keyword in that campaign.

Step 2 — Placement Bid Optimization

Top of Search (Page 1) placements consistently deliver 2x-4x higher conversion rates compared to Product Pages or Rest of Search.

⚡ QUICK WIN

Lower your base CPC bids by 25-30%, and apply a +50% to +100% placement modifier for Top of Search. This ensures you only pay premium bids when buyer intent is highest.
