What Is an Amazon ASIN-Wise Report Generator?

An Amazon ASIN-Wise Report Generator is an analytics tool that combines advertising and business reports to analyze the performance of individual Amazon products. It helps sellers understand product-level ad spend, sales, ROAS, ACoS, TACoS, and overall revenue performance, making it easier to identify growth opportunities and optimize Amazon PPC campaigns.

      ACCURACY BY DESIGN

      How the Reconciliation Engine Guarantees Clean ASIN Numbers

      Manual Excel VLOOKUP sheets break on multi-campaign SKUs, duplicate rows, and averaged ratios. This engine follows strict accounting rules before calculating a single metric.

      🔑

      Strict ASIN-Key Matching

      Advertising reports are matched using Advertised ASIN / Promoted ASIN directly against the Business Report’s Child ASIN. Product titles are never used as join keys, and Purchased ASIN (cross-sell conversions) or Parent ASIN columns require explicit confirmation.

      🧮

      Per-ASIN Pre-Aggregation & Dedup

      A single ASIN often appears across dozens of campaigns, ad groups, dates, or Seller SKUs. Each report is deduplicated row-by-row for identical lines and summed per ASIN before SP, SD, and Business data are joined — preventing double counting.

      📐

      Weighted-Total Ratio Math

      CPC, CTR, ROAS, and TROAS are never averaged across rows or campaigns. The generator sums Impressions, Clicks, Spend, Ad Sales, and Business Sales per ASIN (and for account totals) first, then divides numerators by summed denominators.

      METRICS & FORMULAS

      Column Definitions & Ratio Formulas

      Every ratio in both the dashboard and exported Excel sheet is computed from exact summed numerators and denominators (shown as N/A when the denominator is zero).

      TRAFFIC COST CPC

      Cost Per Click

      Ad Spend ÷ Clicks

      Measures blended cost per click across Sponsored Products and Sponsored Display campaigns.

      Split SP vs. SD in Expanded mode
      ENGAGEMENT CTR %

      Click-Through Rate

      (Clicks ÷ Impressions) × 100

      Percentage of ad impressions generating clicks. Accounts for view-optimized SD vs. click-driven SP traffic.

      Weighted by total ASIN impressions
      AD EFFICIENCY ROAS

      ROAS & ACoS

      Ad Sales ÷ Ad Spend

      Measures direct attributed ad revenue returned per unit of currency spent on ads.

      ACoS = Spend ÷ Ad Sales (in KPIs)
      BUSINESS IMPACT TROAS

      TROAS & TACoS

      Total Sales ÷ Ad Spend

      Compares Total Business Revenue (Paid + Organic) against Ad Spend for true portfolio lift.

      TACoS = Spend ÷ Total Sales
      RECONCILIATION NOTE 01

      Standard vs. Expanded Formats & Grey Cells

      01
      Standard Format (13 Executive Columns)

      Blends SP and SD metrics when both files are uploaded into one clean client-ready layout:

      ASIN Product Name Impression Clicks CPC CTR Ad Spend Ad Sales ROAS Ad Orders Total Sales Total Orders TROAS
      Expanded Format (Separate SP & SD Blocks)

      Outputs dedicated 8-column blocks for Sponsored Products and Sponsored Display alongside Combined Totals so channel attribution stays transparent.

      Grey-Shaded Cells (0 / N/A)

      Flags ASINs absent from a source report (e.g. organic-only SKUs missing from Ads, or advertised SKUs missing from Business Reports) so absent rows are never mistaken for measured zeros.

      RECONCILIATION NOTE 02

      Why Ad Sales Can Exceed Total Business Sales

      02

      An ASIN can occasionally report higher Ad Sales than Total Ordered Product Sales for the same dates. The diagnostics panel automatically flags these common causes:

      1. Attribution Window Spillover

      Amazon Ads attribute 7-day / 14-day conversions back to the click date, whereas Business Reports record the exact calendar timestamp of checkout.

      2. Brand Halo & Other-SKU Purchases

      Total Sales in SP and SD reports include sibling ASINs purchased by shoppers after clicking the advertised product's ad.

      3. SP + SD Date Window Alignment

      Both advertising reports must be exported over the identical calendar range — the validator blocks mismatched SP & SD date periods automatically.

      FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

      ASIN-Wise Reporting FAQs

      Technical answers on data privacy, ASIN matching, currency formats, and attribution nuances.

      How does the tool combine Sponsored Products, Sponsored Display, and Business Reports?+
      It aggregates each uploaded file independently by normalized 10-character ASIN first (matching Advertised ASIN or Promoted ASIN to Child ASIN), removes exact duplicate rows, and then joins the full union of ASINs into a single table with recalculated totals and ratios.
      Is my Seller Central CSV or XLSX data uploaded anywhere?+
      Never. Everything runs 100% client-side in your browser tab using local memory. Even the formatted .xlsx workbook export is generated directly inside your browser without transmitting any file to a backend server.
      What currency formats and column headers are supported?+
      The parser handles Indian locale commas (1,23,456.78), Western commas, parenthesized negative accounting values, and currency prefixes/suffixes (INR, Rs., ₹, USD, $, €, £, AED, SAR, GBP) — including Seller Central India headers such as Spend (INR), 7 Day Total Sales (INR), and Ordered Product Sales (INR).
      What do grey cells mean in the table and Excel export?+
      A grey-shaded cell indicates that the ASIN did not appear in the source report behind that metric. For instance, an ASIN only present in the Business Report displays greyed-out 0s and N/A ratios under advertising columns so you know no ads were recorded rather than assuming ad spend was measured at zero.
      Why can an ASIN have Ad Sales higher than Total Sales?+
      Amazon Ads reports credit orders over a 7-day or 14-day attribution window to the ad click date and can include brand-halo cross-ASIN orders, whereas the Business Report only captures exact Child ASIN orders placed within the selected calendar dates. These ASINs are flagged in the post-run diagnostics panel.
      What is included in the downloaded Excel workbook?+
      The exported .xlsx preserves the bright yellow header bar (#FFFF00), frozen top header row, Excel AutoFilter, Indian comma number formatting, grey source-missing cells, plus a complete second worksheet named Notes documenting file names, date range, attribution headers, portfolio totals, formulas, and unmatched ASINs.
