      FAQ

      Frequently asked questions

      Answers covering product image requirements, keyword strategy, marketplace limits, editing and privacy.

      What is an AI Amazon listing generator?

      +
      An AI Amazon listing generator is a tool that creates the content fields of an Amazon product page, such as the title, bullet points, description and backend search terms, using artificial intelligence. This tool starts from your product images and the details you confirm, so the copy is based on what the product actually is.

      How many product images should I upload?

      +
      Upload five or six images for the best result: a main image, front, side and back views, a feature close-up and a lifestyle shot. You can generate a listing with fewer images, but more angles give the AI more visible detail to work with and leave fewer gaps for you to fill in.

      What image formats and sizes are supported?

      +
      JPG, JPEG, PNG and WEBP files up to 10 MB each, with up to six images per listing. Images are validated in your browser before sending so invalid or corrupted files are caught immediately.

      How does the AI analyze my product images?

      +
      A multimodal AI model looks at every image and extracts visible information: product type, category, colour, shape, visible components, possible usage and any printed text or packaging details. It fills in what it can observe cleanly and marks missing details for you to confirm.

      Will the AI invent specifications I did not provide?

      +
      It is instructed not to. Dimensions, weight, material, certifications, ingredients and compatibility are only used when they are visible or entered by you. Missing details are listed for confirmation instead of being guessed. You should still review every claim before publishing, because AI output can contain mistakes.

      Is this tool good for Amazon SEO?

      +
      It helps you apply Amazon SEO fundamentals: relevant keywords in the first 80 characters of the title, benefit-led bullets, a clear description and well-used backend search terms. SEO results also depend on price, reviews, images, advertising and competition, so no tool can promise a ranking.

      Does it show keyword search volume?

      +
      No. Keywords are AI suggestions based on your product and marketplace, and they are labelled that way. The tool does not show search volume or ranking difficulty because it does not claim unverified volume numbers. Verify priorities using Amazon Brand Analytics or dedicated keyword research.

      Which Amazon marketplaces are supported?

      +
      Amazon India, USA, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE and Germany. Amazon India is selected by default. Each marketplace has its own spelling conventions and notes. Always confirm category-specific rules in Seller Central.

      What are Amazon backend search terms?

      +
      Backend search terms, or generic keywords, are hidden keywords you add in Seller Central to help your product be found for synonyms, alternate names and spelling variants that do not appear in your visible listing. The tool de-duplicates terms and trims them to the byte limit.

      Can I edit the generated listing?

      +
      Yes. Every section is editable: titles, each bullet, the description and the backend terms. Scores, character counts and compliance checks update automatically as you edit, and you can regenerate the listing anytime.

      How do I download or export my listing?

      +
      Copy any individual section with one click, click Copy everything, or download the complete listing as TXT or CSV. The CSV organizes content into clean Seller Central fields such as title, bullet_point1 to bullet_point5, product_description and generic_keywords.

      Can it publish directly to Seller Central?

      +
      No. There is no direct Seller Central integration, so you copy or export the content and add it to your product listing inside Seller Central after reviewing compliance.

      What does the SEO score mean?

      +
      It is an internal content-quality score out of 100 covering title structure, keyword coverage, bullet points, description depth, backend search terms and accuracy/compliance. It is a guide for improving your copy — not an official Amazon score or ranking prediction.

      How is my data and are my images handled?

      +
      Images are resized and validated inside your browser and sent to the AI service only to create your listing. Neither your images nor your API key are stored permanently unless you explicitly tick Remember on this device in your local browser.

      What are the limitations I should know about?

      +
      AI can misread a fine packaging detail or miss non-visible specifications. It cannot verify regulatory certifications, restricted category approvals, or trademarks. Treat the output as a fast first draft and review it against Amazon policies and your verified product specs.

      How much does it cost?

      +
      The AI Amazon Listing Generator tool itself is 100% free to use. When using client-side direct browser generation, usage draws only on the standard API billing of your own Anthropic or OpenAI key.
