FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our one-on-one Amazon e-commerce training.

What is Amazon E-Commerce Training? +

ATES E-Commerce Training is a live, one-on-one online course offered independently by PPC Growth Expert. It covers Amazon selling basics, product research, listing optimization, Amazon PPC fundamentals and profitability.

How can I start an Amazon business in India? +

The usual sequence is to understand the business model, research and validate a product, arrange sourcing, prepare documents, register a seller account, create the listing, choose a fulfilment model, launch, and then manage advertising and operations. The course covers each step.

Do I need GST to sell on Amazon? +

It depends on your business type, turnover and products. The course explains GST requirements and the applicable exceptions. For your own filing decisions, consult a qualified tax professional and check current rules on official government and Amazon pages.

How much investment is needed to start an Amazon business? +

It varies widely. The course shows three hypothetical scenarios and explains each cost head: sourcing, inventory, packaging, GST and compliance, shipping, Amazon fees, advertising, returns and working capital.

What is Amazon FBA? +

Fulfilment by Amazon is a model where you send stock to Amazon's fulfilment centres and Amazon stores, packs and ships orders for you. Fees apply, and they are covered on Day 2.

What is the difference between FBA and Easy Ship? +

With FBA, Amazon stores and ships the stock. With Easy Ship, you hold the stock while Amazon's courier collects and delivers orders from your location. The comparison table on this page sets out the differences.

Will I learn Amazon PPC advertising? +

Yes. Day 3 covers Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, match types, bids, budgets and the core metrics such as ACoS, ROAS and TACoS.

Will I learn product listing optimisation? +

Yes. Day 2 covers titles, bullet points, descriptions, backend search terms, images, variations and listing SEO basics.

Yes. Beginners who want to understand how Amazon selling works receive structured guidance, with the learning path adjusted to their starting point.
