FINAL TAKEAWAY

Conversion Rate Connects Traffic Cost to Sales

A low conversion rate forces every order to consume more paid clicks. Raising conversion reduces your cost per order (CPA) and improves ACoS/ROAS without requiring lower CPC bids — provided traffic volume and order values remain constant.

Goal: Qualify traffic so paid clicks turn into profitable Amazon orders.

How to Use the Amazon PPC Conversion Rate Calculator

Start with clicks and attributed orders to calculate observed PPC conversion. Add ad spend to understand CPC and cost per order. Add ad sales to unlock revenue-per-click, ACoS and ROAS. Use target and product-economics inputs to calculate the conversion rate required for a specific goal.

Amazon PPC Conversion Rate Formula

Conversion Rate = Attributed Orders ÷ PPC Clicks × 100

Conversion Rate vs CTR

CTR (Click-Through Rate) measures how effectively an ad generates a click from impressions, while Conversion Rate measures how effectively clicked traffic turns into attributed orders on your product detail page.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Amazon PPC conversion rate?
Amazon PPC conversion rate (CVR) measures the percentage of paid ad clicks that generate attributed orders. Formula: CVR % = (Attributed Orders ÷ PPC Clicks) × 100.
How do I calculate Amazon PPC conversion rate?
Divide attributed ad orders by total PPC clicks for the same reporting period and multiply by 100. For example, 40 attributed orders divided by 500 clicks equals 8.00% CVR.
What is a good Amazon PPC conversion rate?
There is no universal 'good' conversion rate across all products. A sustainable CVR depends on your CPC, average order value, target ACoS, and product economics.
