What Is Amazon PPC Wasted Spend?

Wasted spend refers to ad budget consumed by search terms and keywords that produce zero sales or operate at an ACoS far higher than your product margin can sustain.

Why Does PPC Waste Occur?

  • Unfiltered Broad and Phrase match targeting causing search term leakage
  • Failure to add negative exact match keywords regularly
  • Overbidding on low-converting competitive keywords
  • Listing conversion issues (low reviews, poor imagery, high price)

How Often Should You Audit Wasted Spend?

For active Seller Central accounts spending over ₹50,000/month ($1,000/month), search term audits should be performed weekly or bi-weekly.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Amazon PPC Waste FAQs

What is Amazon PPC wasted spend? +
Amazon PPC wasted spend consists of advertising expenditure on non-converting search terms (zero-order clicks) and excess spend on converting keywords operating above your target ACoS or product contribution margin.
How do you calculate recoverable PPC waste? +
Recoverable waste equals total zero-order search term spend plus excess spend on converting keywords (Spend minus Allowed Target Spend). This non-overlapping sum represents total budget leakages that can be recovered through negative keywords and bid optimizations.
Are zero-order search terms always wasted spend? +
Not always. Low-click queries (1–3 clicks) may still be undergoing statistical discovery. However, search terms with 8+ clicks or high spend and zero orders represent confirmed wasted spend and prime negative keyword candidates.
Is my CSV data kept private? +
Yes, 100%. Our analyzer processes your Amazon Search Term Report entirely client-side inside your browser. No campaign or revenue data is uploaded to any server.
