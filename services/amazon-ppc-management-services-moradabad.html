SCOPE OF WORK

What Does Amazon PPC Management Include?

Here's exactly what professional Amazon PPC management covers. Think of these as the layers of work that happen every month to keep your campaigns efficient.
Initial Work

PPC Account Audit & Analysis

We review your existing campaigns, identify structural issues, spot wasted spend, analyze your current targeting, and benchmark performance against your product margins and business goals.

Why it matters: You can't optimize what you don't understand. An audit reveals exactly where money is leaking.
Setup Phase

Campaign Setup & Restructuring

We design campaign architecture tailored to your products, budget, and objectives. This might mean splitting auto campaigns, creating separate exact-match structures, or organizing by product category—whatever makes sense for your business.

Why it matters: Good structure gives you control. Poor structure creates noise.
Ongoing

Keyword Research & Targeting

We identify high-intent keywords your customers are searching for, evaluate search volume and competition, and build targeting that attracts qualified traffic without overpaying.

Why it matters: Targeting determines everything. Wrong keywords = wasted budget.
Daily Work

Search-Term Analysis & Optimization

We review search terms regularly, identify high-performers to scale, zero-order terms to evaluate or negate, and build negative keyword lists that protect your budget from irrelevant searches.

Why it matters: Search terms are where you discover waste. One analysis can save thousands.
Ongoing

Bid Optimization

We adjust keyword and product target bids based on performance, placement, conversion rate, and profitability. Some keywords deserve higher bids. Others should be paused. Adjustments are data-driven, not arbitrary.

Why it matters: Bids directly control your visibility and cost. Wrong bids kill your ROAS.
Strategic

Budget Management & Allocation

We don't just increase budgets everywhere. We analyze which campaigns are delivering profit, which are exploring new opportunities, and how to allocate budget to maximize total return—not just spending more.

Why it matters: Budget without strategy is just spending. Strategy turns budget into revenue.
Protection

Negative Keyword Management

We build negative keyword lists at campaign and ad group level, exclude non-converting product targets, and use exact-match negatives to prevent budget waste on irrelevant searches.

Why it matters: Negatives protect your ACOS by filtering out traffic that won't convert.

Why it matters: Product targets can be goldmines—or budget black holes.

We provide clear, honest reporting on what's working, where money is going, what changed month-to-month, and what decisions we made and why. You understand your data.

THE 6 ECONOMIC DRIVERS

What Actually Shapes the Strategy Is the Account Itself

Bids, budgets, and placement multipliers are never based on generic guesswork. Every campaign decision is governed by these 6 core account variables:

LEVER 01 · TICKET SIZE

Product Category & Price

A ₹499 item cannot absorb the same CPC as a ₹4,999 handcrafted statement decor piece. Target CPCs and bid thresholds must scale strictly with unit price absorption.

LEVER 02 · PROFIT CEILING

Gross Margin & Break-Even

Direct manufacturing costs determine your true break-even ACOS. We calibrate SKU-level bid ceilings so advertising never cannibalizes net bottom-line earnings.

LEVER 03 · SERP DENSITY

Competition & Top Placements

How many established category incumbents hold the top search placements. We calibrate placement multipliers surgically—favoring product page steals when Top of Search bids are overheated.

LEVER 04 · TRAFFIC YIELD

Conversion Rate & Reviews

The exact same click is worth far more on a listing that converts. High CVR acts as an advertising multiplier, allowing profitable bidding where competitors bleed cash.

LEVER 05 · SUPPLY CHAIN

Inventory & Restock Coverage

There is no point scaling spend on an ASIN that is about to stock out. Campaigns feature dynamic bid throttling tied to warehouse and FBA days-of-cover.

LEVER 06 · ASIN LIFECYCLE

Search Demand & Business Stage

A launch needs aggressive keyword discovery data and algorithmic indexing; a mature hero ASIN needs disciplined harvest bidding and negative search-term isolation.

The Fundamental Law of Amazon Bidding

"Raising bids is the fastest lever available in Amazon advertising and usually the least useful one on its own. Higher bids buy more of the same traffic, including the traffic that was already losing money. Sustainable scale comes from search-term discipline, negative filtration, and SKU-level margin protection."

BID DECISIONS

When Should Amazon PPC Bids Go Up or Down?

These are directional signals, not fixed rules. The same data can point either way depending on margin, stage, and objective.
Scale & Harvest

Increase or test a higher bid

When unit economics confirm profitable room for aggressive impression capture.

  • ▲
    Strong Conversion Efficiency: Conversion efficiency is strong relative to break-even ACOS.
  • ▲
    Room in Unit Economics: Unit economics leave room above the current CPC.
  • ▲
    Statistically Trustworthy: Enough clicks and orders to trust the result.
  • ▲
    Unclaimed Demand: Impression share suggests real unclaimed demand.
  • ▲
    Strategic Dominance: The term is strategically important to defend or own.
Protect & Prune

Reduce or control

When spend accumulates past break-even limits without driving sales velocity.

  • ▼
    Spend Leaking Past Break-Even: Spend is accumulating well past break-even without orders.
  • ▼
    Weak Conversion Velocity: Conversion rate is weak against category norms.
  • ▼
    Unsustainable CPC Floor: CPC has risen to a level the product can't support.
  • ▼
    Poor Search Query Relevance: Relevance is poor — the query isn't really your product.
  • ▼
    Margin / Price Disconnect: Margin or price doesn't justify competing at that level.
💡

The Strategic Bid Rule: Never adjust bids in isolation. A high bid on a 35% margin brassware piece can print scalable profit, while the exact same bid on an 18% margin SKU bleeds capital daily. We audit every target at the SKU-profit level.
