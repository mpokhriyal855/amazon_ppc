COMMERCIAL METRICS GLOSSARY

What We Track: Beyond Vanity Numbers

We monitor 12 critical commercial levers that explain why revenue moves and how profit is preserved.
METRIC 01
Sessions & Traffic Quality

Unique shopper visits to product detail pages. We verify whether traffic is genuine purchase-intent shoppers or wasted browsing clicks.

METRIC 02
Unit Session Percentage (CVR)

Percentage of shoppers who visit and buy. Benchmarking category averages to ensure listings convert traffic into orders.

METRIC 03
TACoS (Total ACoS)

Ad spend divided by TOTAL revenue. The ultimate barometer of whether advertising is creating genuine organic brand momentum.

METRIC 04
Organic vs. Paid Share

Measuring what percentage of sales comes from ads vs. organic search. A healthy brand strives for 60%+ organic revenue contribution.

METRIC 05
ACoS & Return on Ad Spend

Campaign-level efficiency tracking to ensure every advertising rupee generates profitable, targeted revenue.

METRIC 06
Cost Per Click (CPC) Trends

Tracking auction bid inflation across exact, phrase, and broad targets to prevent margin erosion from aggressive competitors.

METRIC 07
Click-Through Rate (CTR)

The efficiency of main images, pricing badges, and titles in compelling shoppers to click on search results.

METRIC 08
Days of Cover (Inventory DoC)

Current inventory divided by average daily unit sales. Essential for synchronizing advertising with warehouse lead times.

METRIC 09
Buy Box Ownership %

Percentage of time your listing owns the Featured Offer. Essential for detecting rogue third-party sellers or pricing penalties.

METRIC 10
Customer Return Rate

Monitoring product return reasons in Voice of the Customer to identify packaging flaws, sizing confusion, or listing discrepancies.

METRIC 11
Stranded Inventory Units

Units in Amazon fulfillment centers without an active listing link, costing storage fees without generating sales.

METRIC 12
Net Contribution Margin

Profit remaining after Amazon referral fees, FBA fulfillment costs, advertising spend, and product cost-of-goods (COGS).
