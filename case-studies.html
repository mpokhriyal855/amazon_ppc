FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Amazon PPC Optimization & Case Study Insights

Common questions regarding our advertising strategy, account audits, and performance timelines.

❓ How quickly can Amazon PPC ACOS be reduced?

Initial wasted spend reduction occurs within 14 to 30 days by harvesting negative exact keywords. Full ACOS optimization and campaign scaling stabilize within 60 to 90 days.

❓ Do you handle both Seller Central and Vendor Central?

Yes, we manage Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, and Amazon DSP ads across both Seller Central and Vendor Central accounts.

❓ How do you separate branded vs non-branded campaigns?

We isolate branded terms into dedicated campaigns to protect brand search share, while running structured non-branded campaigns to capture active category shoppers without inflating overall ACOS.

❓ What marketplaces do you optimize Amazon PPC for?

We manage campaigns across Amazon India (IN), Amazon US, UK, Europe, UAE, and Canada marketplaces with localized search-term harvesting.
