              FAQ

              Frequently asked questions

              What is an AI Amazon listing generator?

              An AI Amazon listing generator is a tool that creates the content fields of an Amazon product page, such as the title, bullet points, description and backend search terms, using artificial intelligence. This tool starts from your product images and the details you confirm, so the copy is based on what the product actually is.

              How many product images should I upload?

              Upload 4 to 6 images for the best result: a main image, front, side and back views, a feature close-up and a lifestyle shot. More angles give the AI more visible detail to work with and leave fewer gaps for you to fill in.

              What image formats and sizes are supported?

              JPG, JPEG, PNG and WEBP files up to 10 MB each, with up to six images per listing.

              Does it show keyword search volume?

              Yes, when you import a CSV report from Helium 10 or Amazon Brand Analytics, verified search volumes and competitor densities are displayed. Without imported research data, metrics are labeled as unavailable rather than presenting unverified estimates.

              Which Amazon marketplaces are supported?

              Amazon India, USA, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE and Germany. Each marketplace has its own length limits and backend byte restrictions.

              Can I edit and regenerate sections independently?

              Yes. Every title variation, individual bullet point, description paragraph, and backend keyword string is fully editable with live character counters. You can also regenerate titles, bullets, description, or keywords individually.
