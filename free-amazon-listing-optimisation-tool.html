              Amazon Listing Optimization Built Around Visual Product Data

              An Amazon product listing is your single most important digital sales asset. Shoppers decide whether to click your listing within two seconds of glancing at the title and main image in the search results. Once on the product detail page, conversion hinges on the first three bullet points, feature imagery, customer reviews, and the structured product description.

              Meanwhile, Amazon's search algorithm (A9 / Cosmo) scans your product title, five bullet points, description, and backend search terms to index your ASIN for thousands of relevant customer queries. If your title lacks high-intent keywords, or if your backend keywords repeat words already present in your title, you leave substantial organic traffic on the table.

              This Free AI Amazon Listing Optimisation Tool begins with your actual product imagery. By analyzing visible materials, colors, physical components, and printed packaging text, the AI crafts copy that authentically describes what customers will receive. You retain full control: confirm key product facts, insert priority focus keywords, edit every section in real time, and download verified Seller Central files ready for upload.

              ✦ CLEAR ANSWERS

              Frequently Asked Questions

              Everything you need to know about creating optimized Amazon listings with AI.

              What is an AI Amazon listing generator?

              An AI Amazon listing generator is an automated copywriting and SEO tool that creates optimized product detail page assets: titles, bullet points, descriptions, and backend search terms using artificial intelligence. This tool analyzes your product photos and user-confirmed facts to produce grounded, high-converting copy without hallucinating claims.

              How many product images should I upload?

              We recommend uploading 4 to 6 photos: a pure white background main photo, front angle, side angle, back angle, a feature or packaging close-up, and a lifestyle in-use photo. More angles give the AI deeper visible context to extract features and materials accurately.

              What image formats and sizes are supported?

              JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WEBP image files up to 10 MB each are supported. Images are processed directly within your browser, ensuring quick validation and immediate preview generation.

              How does the AI analyze my product images?

              The vision analysis system examines color, geometry, visible materials (e.g. stainless steel, silicone, mesh), physical components, and printed text on packaging or labels. It pre-fills confirmed attributes and excludes unverifiable specifications from the copy.

              Will the AI invent specifications I did not provide?

              No. The generator follows strict anti-hallucination rules. Critical details like exact dimensions, technical certifications, chemical compositions, or battery ratings are only included if visible on the packaging or explicitly entered by you in the confirmed facts box.

              Is this tool effective for Amazon SEO in 2027?

              Yes. It applies proven marketplace SEO fundamentals: placing highest-volume search phrases in the first 80 characters of the title, building 5 scannable bullets with keyword-rich headlines, and maximizing backend search term capacity with unique synonyms.

              Does it display verified keyword search volumes?

              No. Keywords are algorithmic suggestions based on customer search intent and marketplace taxonomy. For definitive monthly search numbers, cross-reference in Amazon Brand Analytics or specialized keyword trackers.

              Which Amazon marketplaces are supported?

              Supported marketplaces include Amazon India, Amazon USA, Amazon UK, Amazon Canada, Amazon Australia, Amazon UAE, and Amazon Germany. Each marketplace profile automatically applies regional character standards, metric vs imperial units, and backend byte allowances.

              What are Amazon backend search terms?

              Backend search terms (generic keywords) are hidden keywords entered in Seller Central to index your product for synonyms, alternate spellings, and related phrases that do not appear in your visible copy. Repeating words already present in your title wastes valuable byte capacity.

              Can I edit the generated listing?

              Yes! Every single field (all 3 title options, each of the 5 bullets, the description, and the backend keywords) is directly editable in the dashboard with live character and byte counters.

              How do I export my listing to Seller Central?

              You can copy individual fields with one click, copy everything formatted with one click, download a formatted TXT file, or export an Amazon-compliant CSV file with standard columns (`item_name`, `bullet_point1` through `bullet_point5`, `product_description`, and `generic_keywords`).

              Can this tool publish directly to Seller Central?

              No. For maximum security, we do not require your Amazon Seller Central credentials. You simply copy or export the generated text and paste it into your Seller Central catalog edit page.

              What does the 100-point SEO score mean?

              It is an internal listing health diagnostic that evaluates title length, brand positioning, primary keyword visibility in the first 80 characters, bullet count, description depth, backend byte utilization, and compliance checks against prohibited promotional phrases.

              How is my data and imagery handled?

              All image processing and listing compilation happens securely in your browser. Your images and brand details are never stored on external databases or sold to third parties.

              What are the key limitations I should keep in mind?

              AI copywriting cannot verify FDA approvals, laboratory certifications, or intellectual property rights. You should always conduct a final review against your exact product specs and Amazon's category guidelines before publishing.
