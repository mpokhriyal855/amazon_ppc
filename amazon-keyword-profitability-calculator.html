KEYWORD ECONOMICS FRAMEWORK

How to Analyze Amazon Keyword Profitability

Why Campaign ACoS Hides Keyword-Level Loss

Evaluating Amazon advertising solely at the campaign or account level creates dangerous blind spots. A campaign achieving a comfortable 25% ACoS often conceals individual high-spend keywords running at 80%+ ACoS alongside ultra-profitable brand terms. By auditing unit economics at the individual keyword level, Amazon sellers can eliminate loss-making search terms while aggressively scaling high-margin winners.

Understanding Break-Even ACoS at Keyword Level

Break-Even ACoS represents the exact advertising cost percentage where ad revenue equals your product contribution margin after accounting for Product Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), Amazon Referral Fees (typically 8% to 15%), and FBA Fulfillment & Shipping fees.

Product Contribution Margin % = [(Selling Price - COGS - Referral Fee - FBA Fee) ÷ Selling Price] × 100

The Organic Halo Effect in Keyword Bidding

Sales generated through PPC ads directly boost your product's organic rank on Amazon's search engine. When calculating true keyword ROI, sophisticated Amazon advertisers account for the Organic Spillover (Halo Effect)—the additional organic orders generated as a result of top-of-search PPC placement.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Keyword Profitability FAQ

What is Amazon keyword profitability? +

Amazon keyword profitability evaluates the net profit or loss generated by an individual target keyword after deducting product COGS, Amazon referral fees, FBA fulfillment costs, and PPC ad spend from attributed revenues.

How do you calculate net profit for a keyword on Amazon? +

Keyword Net Profit = Total Attributed Revenue (PPC Sales + Organic Halo Sales) minus Total Product Costs (COGS + Referral Fee + FBA Fee per unit × Total Units Sold) minus Keyword PPC Ad Spend.

What is the Organic Halo Effect in keyword bidding? +

The Organic Halo Effect is the increase in organic rank and organic sales resulting from paid PPC orders on a keyword. When PPC drives orders on a target term, organic rank improves, generating additional 'free' organic sales.

What is Break-Even ACoS for a keyword? +

Break-Even ACoS is the exact ACoS percentage at which your keyword advertising revenue equals total product costs and ad spend. Break-Even ACoS = Product Contribution Margin %.

How do I determine maximum supported CPC for a keyword? +

Maximum CPC = Product Selling Price × (Contribution Margin % - Target Net Profit Margin %) × Conversion Rate %. This ensures your CPC bid leaves your required net profit margin per unit.
