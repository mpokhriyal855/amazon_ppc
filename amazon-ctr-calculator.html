What does this mean?
CTR measures ad relevance and main image appeal. Higher CTR signals strong search term alignment to Amazon's algorithm.

What Is Amazon CTR?

Click-Through Rate (CTR) measures the ratio of shoppers who click your ad after seeing it on search results pages or product detail pages.

Formula:

CTR % = (Total Ad Clicks ÷ Total Ad Impressions) × 100
