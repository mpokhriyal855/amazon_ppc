— THE PROBLEM

Most Amazon PPC Accounts Don't Have a Traffic Problem. They Have a Strategy Problem.

Ad spend keeps climbing while profitability quietly erodes. These are the patterns behind it.

Poor campaign structure

Auto, broad and exact match competing against each other, muddying attribution and wasting budget.

$

Wasted ad spend

Budget flowing to search terms that generate clicks but never convert into profitable orders.

?

Irrelevant search terms

Broad match and auto campaigns pulling traffic with no real purchase intent behind it.

Weak keyword harvesting

High-converting search terms sitting inside auto campaigns instead of being isolated and scaled.

Incorrect bidding

Bids set once and left untouched, ignoring placement, day-part and conversion-rate data.

No negative keyword strategy

The same wasted search terms draining budget month after month with no exclusion process.

Poor placement optimization

Top-of-search, product page and rest-of-search bid the same, despite very different conversion behavior.

B

Overdependence on branded terms

Ad sales look strong on paper but are largely cannibalizing sales that would happen organically anyway.

Scaling without profitability

Spend increases every month, but ACOS and TACOS creep up right alongside it.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers to common questions about campaign optimization, ACOS management, scaling strategies, and working together.

01
Can you help me scale my Amazon brand without simply increasing ad spend? +

Yes. Scaling isn't just about spending more. We first identify which campaigns, keywords, products and placements are actually driving profitable sales. Once we know what's working, we gradually increase budgets, expand winning keywords and look for new growth opportunities while keeping efficiency under control.

02
My Amazon sales have stopped growing. Can PPC help me break through that plateau? +

In many cases, yes. When sales stop growing, the problem isn't always the budget. It could be campaign structure, keyword coverage, bidding, conversion rate, competition or simply putting spend behind the wrong products. We look at the account as a whole and build a strategy around what's holding the brand back.

03
I am already running Amazon Ads. Why should I hire someone to manage them? +

Running campaigns is easy. Knowing what to change, when to change it and when to leave something alone is where the real work comes in. We look at search-term performance, bids, budgets, placements, campaign structure and product-level performance to make decisions based on data rather than assumptions.

04
Will you focus only on reducing ACOS? +

No. A low ACOS doesn't automatically mean the account is healthy. If reducing ACOS also reduces sales and growth, it may not be the right strategy. We look at ACOS alongside ROAS, TACOS, sales growth, ad orders and overall account performance to find the right balance between efficiency and scale.

05
Can you scale a brand if we have a limited advertising budget? +

Yes, but the strategy has to be different. With a limited budget, we prioritize the products, keywords and campaigns that have the highest potential instead of spreading the budget too thin. The goal is to get the most useful data and sales possible from every dollar spent.

06
What if my product is new and has very little sales history? +

That's common with new launches. We start by understanding the product, competition, pricing and relevant search demand. From there, we build campaigns to generate initial traffic and conversions, collect search-term data and gradually move more budget toward the targets that show potential.

07
How do you decide which campaigns should get more budget? +

We don't increase budgets just because a campaign is spending. We look at sales, conversion rate, ACOS/ROAS, search-term quality, available impression share and the campaign's role in the overall account. Strong campaigns get room to grow, while inefficient spend is controlled or redirected.

08
Will Amazon PPC help increase my organic sales too? +

PPC and organic growth can work together. When your products start generating more relevant traffic and conversions for important search terms, it can support your overall visibility on Amazon. However, we don't promise specific organic rankings because Amazon's algorithm and marketplace competition are outside anyone's complete control.

09
How often do you optimize Amazon PPC campaigns? +

Optimization is ongoing rather than something we do once a month and forget about. We regularly review search terms, bids, budgets, placements, targeting and campaign performance, then make changes based on the data. The frequency of changes depends on the size and activity of the account.

10
What happens if my current Amazon PPC account is poorly structured? +

We don't expect you to fix everything before working with us. We start with an account audit to understand what's currently running, where the money is going and what is generating results. If the structure needs improvement, we gradually reorganize campaigns and targeting without making unnecessary changes just for the sake of rebuilding everything.

11
How long does it take to see improvement in Amazon PPC performance? +

It depends on the account, category, competition, budget and starting point. Some issues can be identified and improved quickly, while meaningful scaling usually takes longer because we need enough data to make confident decisions. We focus on building sustainable improvement rather than promising overnight results.

12
Do you guarantee a specific ACOS or sales number? +

No. We don't believe in promising a fixed sales or ACOS number before understanding the account and market. Amazon performance depends on factors such as competition, pricing, conversion rate, inventory and demand. What we can control is the strategy, execution, optimization and how efficiently we manage the advertising budget.

13
Will I know where my Amazon advertising money is being spent? +

Absolutely. Transparency is a big part of how we work. You should be able to understand which campaigns are spending, what is generating sales, where money is being wasted and what we are changing. Our reporting focuses on the numbers that actually matter for your business, not just activity reports.

14
Can you manage PPC for multiple products and ASINs? +

Yes. We can manage accounts with multiple products and different product lines. Instead of treating every ASIN the same, we segment campaigns based on product performance, demand, competition and growth potential so that the budget is allocated where it can have the biggest impact.

15
What is the first step if I want to work with you? +

We start by understanding where your Amazon business currently stands. If you're already running ads, we review the account and identify the biggest opportunities and problems. If you're launching a new product, we build the strategy from the ground up. From there, we recommend what we believe should be done rather than selling you a fixed package that may not fit your business.
