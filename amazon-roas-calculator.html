Add a Target ROAS to evaluate the exact revenue lift or budget adjustments needed to reach your performance goal. If you enter your unit product economics (COGS, referral fee, FBA fee), the engine automatically calculates your Break-Even ROAS boundary.

Amazon ROAS Formula & Exact Math

Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) calculates attributed advertising revenue relative to advertising spend:

ROAS = Attributed Ad Sales ÷ Ad Spend

For example, if an Amazon India seller spends ₹25,000 on Sponsored Products campaigns and generates ₹1,00,000 in attributed ad sales, the calculation is:

₹1,00,000 Ad Sales ÷ ₹25,000 Ad Spend = 4.00x ROAS

ROAS vs ACoS: The Mathematical Conversion

ROAS and Advertising Cost of Sales (ACoS) describe the exact same revenue efficiency relationship in opposite directions:

METRIC FORMULA EXAMPLE
ROAS Ad Sales ÷ Ad Spend 4.00x
ACoS (Ad Spend ÷ Ad Sales) × 100 25.00%

To convert ROAS to ACoS: ACoS % = 100 ÷ ROAS. To convert ACoS to ROAS: ROAS = 100 ÷ ACoS %.

ROAS vs ROI: Why 4x ROAS Is NOT 300% Profit

CRITICAL RULE: A 4.00x ROAS does NOT mean 300% profit.

ROAS measures gross attributed revenue returned per ad rupee, completely ignoring product manufacturing cost (COGS), Amazon referral fees, FBA fulfilment costs, storage, GST, and shipping. True return on investment (ROI) requires deducting all variable product costs before calculating net contribution return on ad spend.

What Is a Good Amazon ROAS?

There is no universal "good" ROAS that applies to all Amazon sellers. A sustainable ROAS target depends entirely on your product's pre-ad contribution margin. If your profit margin before advertising is 25%, your break-even ROAS is 4.00x; any ROAS below 4.00x results in a loss. However, if your pre-ad margin is 40%, a 4.00x ROAS yields significant net profit.

How Can You Improve Amazon PPC ROAS?

  1. Eliminate Inefficient Traffic: Audit search term reports weekly and add non-converting search queries as negative exact keywords.
  2. Control Keyword Bids: Lower bids on broad and phrase keywords that exceed your target ACoS, and shift budget toward high-converting exact match terms.
  3. Improve Listing Conversion Rate: Enhance your main product image, bullet points, price competitiveness, and A+ content. A higher conversion rate raises revenue per click without changing CPC.
  4. Reallocate Budget: Shift spend away from low-ROAS campaign placements toward proven Top of Search or high-performing competitor ASIN targets.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Amazon ROAS Calculator FAQs

What does ROAS mean in Amazon advertising? +
ROAS stands for Return on Ad Spend. It measures attributed ad revenue generated per ₹1 of advertising spend. A 4.00x ROAS means ₹4 of attributed sales were generated for every ₹1 spent on Amazon ads.
How is Amazon ROAS calculated? +
Amazon ROAS is calculated as Attributed Ad Sales ÷ Ad Spend. For example, ₹1,00,000 in ad sales divided by ₹25,000 in ad spend equals 4.00x ROAS.
Is 4x ROAS good for Amazon PPC? +
There is no universal "good" ROAS. Whether 4x is profitable depends on your product margin. If your pre-ad contribution margin is 25% (break-even ROAS 4.00x), 4x is break-even. If your margin is 40% (break-even ROAS 2.50x), 4x is highly profitable.
What is the difference between ACoS and ROAS? +
ROAS and ACoS describe the exact same revenue-to-spend ratio in opposite directions. ACoS = (Ad Spend ÷ Ad Sales) × 100, while ROAS = Ad Sales ÷ Ad Spend. ROAS = 100 ÷ ACoS %.
What is break-even ROAS? +
Break-even ROAS is the minimum ROAS multiplier required so that ad spend equals your product's pre-ad contribution margin. Formula: Break-Even ROAS = 1 ÷ (Break-Even ACoS % / 100) or Selling Price ÷ Pre-Ad Contribution.
Is ROAS the same as profit? +
No. ROAS measures attributed revenue efficiency, not profit. A 4x ROAS does NOT mean 300% profit because product cost, Amazon referral fees, FBA fees, shipping, returns, and taxes are excluded.
How can I improve my Amazon PPC ROAS? +
You can improve ROAS by eliminating non-converting search terms, controlling keyword bids, raising listing conversion rates (better main image, title, price, A+ content), and reallocating budget toward top-performing targets.
