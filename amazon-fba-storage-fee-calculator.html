Amazon FBA Storage Cost Formula

For a static inventory model, monthly storage fees equal Total Inventory Volume (ft³) × Storage Rate (₹/ft³/month) × (1 + 0.18 GST). Under realistic sell-through conditions, storage fees decline over time as monthly sales reduce average daily stored volume.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are Amazon FBA storage fees calculated in India? +

Amazon India FBA storage fees are calculated based on the daily average volume (in cubic feet) occupied by your packaged inventory stored in Amazon fulfilment centers, multiplied by the monthly storage rate per cubic foot plus 18% GST.

What product dimensions should I enter for FBA storage calculation? +

Always enter the packed, sellable unit dimensions (Length × Width × Height in cm) including polybags, boxes, or retail packaging, as Amazon measures the total warehouse space your package occupies.

What is Months of Cover in Amazon FBA inventory management? +

Months of Cover estimates how long your current FBA inventory will last based on your expected monthly sales velocity (Current Inventory ÷ Monthly Sales).

Why is sell-through velocity important for FBA storage costs? +

Storage fees accumulate over time. As products sell through, stored inventory decreases, reducing average monthly cubic volume and lowering total storage fees compared to a static inventory model.

Is GST charged on Amazon India FBA storage fees? +

Yes, an 18% Goods and Services Tax (GST) is levied on all Amazon FBA storage charges in India. Registered GST sellers can claim Input Tax Credit (ITC) on these charges.

How does packaging size reduction lower FBA storage costs? +

Volume scales multiplicatively (L × W × H). A 10% reduction across all three packaging dimensions results in a 27.1% reduction in total cubic volume, significantly lowering storage costs per unit.

What is the difference between static inventory and sell-through storage models? +

A static model assumes your initial inventory quantity stays in FBA for the entire duration, representing a worst-case scenario. A sell-through model dynamically depletes inventory monthly as sales occur, providing a realistic operational storage projection.

What are Amazon aged inventory storage surcharges? +

Aged inventory surcharges apply to units stored in Amazon fulfilment centers for extended periods (typically over 270 or 365 days), adding extra per-unit or per-cubic-foot monthly fees on top of standard storage rates.
