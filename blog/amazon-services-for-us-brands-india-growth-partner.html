1. The Broken US Agency Model: High Retainers, Junior Staff, and Vanishing Margins

Selling on Amazon US has never been more competitive. With rising Cost Per Click (CPC) across mature categories, strict inventory storage limits (FBA capacity quotas), and relentless algorithmic changes, US brands cannot afford inefficient marketing.

Yet when US brand founders and marketing directors seek external expertise, they routinely encounter the standard domestic agency model:

  • Exorbitant Retainers: Domestic US agencies charge fixed monthly fees of $4,000 to $10,000, often bundled with a 10% to 15% tax on your total ad spend.
  • The Bait-and-Switch Team Dynamic: You are pitched by charismatic senior agency founders or VP-level executives. Once the contract is signed, your account is promptly handed down to an entry-level junior account manager juggling 15 to 25 other brands.
  • Misaligned Incentives: When agencies charge a percentage of ad spend, they are financially rewarded when your advertising budget expands—regardless of whether that spend generates incremental profit or bleeds into organic cannibalization.
  • Superficial Reporting: Monthly slide decks highlight vanity metrics like total ad impressions and top-line ad sales, while masking the metrics that actually determine business health: Blended TACoS, Net Contribution Margin, and Organic Rank Velocity.

This structural inefficiency has led hundreds of forward-thinking US e-commerce brands to reconsider how they resource marketplace growth.
