Case Study: How an Indian D2C Brand Scaled from ₹60L to ₹2.78Cr/Month in 90 Days

Trapped on an ad-spend plateau with rising CPCs and stagnating organic rankings? Discover the exact 4-phase playbook used to scale an Indian D2C consumer goods brand by 4.6x in 90 days while slashing TACoS from 18.4% down to 9.2%.

Anmol Pokhriyal Amazon PPC & Growth Specialist · Updated September 2026
💡 Real scale happens when non-branded PPC wins systematically lift organic search rank.
✦ TL;DR — KEY SUMMARY
  • The Challenge: An established Indian D2C kitchen & home brand was stagnating at ₹60L/month with a bloated 38% ACoS and declining Buy Box share.
  • Phase 1 (The Waste Purge): Eliminated ₹3.2 Lakhs in non-converting search term bleed by enforcing the 10-click negative rule across 45 campaigns.
  • Phase 2 (CVR Engineering): Overhauled main images to pure white 2000px zoom-enabled renders, added video walkthroughs, and added A+ brand story carousels, lifting conversion rate from 11.2% to 17.8%.
  • Phase 3 (Top-of-Search Multipliers): Funneled top 18 search terms into Exact SKAGs with +45% Top-of-Search multipliers, winning organic ranking #1 on 7 high-volume category keywords.
  • The Outcome: Scaled to ₹2.78 Crores in monthly revenue in 90 days while cutting TACoS in half (18.4% down to 9.2%).

1. The Baseline Audit: A 7-Figure Brand Trapped on a Plateau

In May 2026, an Indian manufacturing brand selling in the home & kitchen category approached us. Despite selling quality, Made-in-India products, they had been stuck at ₹58 Lakhs to ₹62 Lakhs per month for seven consecutive months.

Our deep diagnostic audit uncovered three critical structural bottlenecks:

  • Unfenced Auto Campaigns: 42% of total advertising spend was running inside loose Auto and Broad campaigns with no negative keyword pruning.
  • Brand Cannibalization: 52% of all recorded ad sales were coming from their own brand name keywords at a vanity 9% ACoS.
  • Conversion Leakage: Detail pages lacked video assets, lifestyle infographics were low resolution, and competitor conquest ads were prominently displayed on their own listings.
📊 DAY 0 BASELINE METRICS

Monthly Revenue: ₹60.4 Lakhs · Monthly Ad Spend: ₹11.1 Lakhs · ACoS: 38.2% · TACoS: 18.4% · Organic Rank on Primary Keyword: Position #19 (Bottom Page 1).

2. Phase 1 (Days 1–15): The Wasted Ad Spend Purge

The first rule of scaling is to stop bleeding capital before you add fuel.

We exported a 60-day Search Term Report and identified ₹3.2 Lakhs in ad spend consumed by 412 customer search terms that had accumulated 10+ clicks with exactly zero orders.

⚡ IMMEDIATE CORRECTION

We bulk-uploaded all 412 bleeders as Negative Exact keywords across every Auto and Broad campaign. Within 14 days, daily ad spend dropped by 24% with zero loss in daily orders, instantly freeing up ₹3,00,000+ in working capital for high-converting targets!

3. Phase 2 (Days 16–45): Detail Page CVR Overhaul

Never scale traffic into a leaky bucket. Before increasing ad bids, we re-engineered the product detail pages for their top 5 hero ASINs:

  • Main Image Render: Replaced flat packaging photos with high-contrast, 3D-modeled 2000px zoom-enabled renders showing the product in action.
  • 30-Second Video Walkthrough: Created crisp product demonstration videos highlighting the unique USP within the first 4 seconds.
  • A+ Content Comparison Charts: Built custom A+ carousels cross-linking complementary catalog items to boost Average Order Value (AOV).

Result: Overall listing conversion rate increased from 11.2% to 17.8% (+58.9% lift). Every paid click was now 58% more likely to produce an order!

4. Phase 3 (Days 46–70): Automated Harvesting & Placement Multipliers

With conversion rates stabilized, we initiated the Broad-to-Exact Funneling Engine.

We harvested 18 proven customer queries that had generated 3+ orders at < 25% ACoS. We isolated each into a dedicated Single Keyword Ad Group (SKAG) with:

Top-of-Search Multiplier: +45% | Product Pages Multiplier: 0%

By focusing 70% of ad impressions directly on Top of Search Page 1, the Amazon A10 algorithm detected an overwhelming surge in organic sales velocity.

Within 21 days, their primary category keyword jumped from Position #19 to Position #2 organically! Organic sales exploded from 48% of total revenue to 68%.

5. Phase 4 (Days 71–90): Defensive ASIN Rings & Prime Day Surge

In the final phase, competitors began targeting our hero ASIN detail pages.

We launched Defensive Product Attribute Targeting (PAT) campaigns targeting our own ASINs with Sponsored Products and Sponsored Display. This completely filled the "Products related to this item" carousel with our own catalog items, blocking rival conquest attempts.

7. 5 Key Scaling Principles for Indian D2C Brands

1. Fix CVR Before Increasing Bids:
A 50% lift in conversion rate reduces effective CPC requirements by 33% across all placements.
2. Stop Celebrating Low Branded ACoS:
Isolate branded keywords. True scaling only happens when generic non-branded rank improves.
3. Ruthlessly Prune 10-Click Bleeders:
Reclaiming 20% to 30% of wasted budget creates the free cash flow required to bid aggressively on winners.
4. Dominate Top-of-Search on Exact Keywords:
Top-of-search placements carry 2.5x to 4x higher CVR, directly influencing the organic algorithm.

10. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can this playbook work for brands in any product category? +

Yes. The fundamental principles—eliminating wasted spend, raising detail page conversion rate, isolating high-converting generic search terms, and defending product detail pages—apply universally across home, kitchen, beauty, apparel, electronics, and health supplements.

Did this brand run out of stock during this 4.6x growth sprint? +

No, because manufacturing lead times were integrated directly into the PPC scaling model. Before boosting Top-of-Search multipliers, we ensured at least 60 days of inventory cover was available in FBA warehouses to avoid the devastating out-of-stock de-ranking penalty.

How long does it typically take to see first results? +

The negative keyword waste purge shows immediate ad spend savings within 48 to 72 hours. Detail page image and video CVR gains reflect within 7 to 10 days, while organic keyword ranking gains from harvested Exact match campaigns typically compound over 30 to 60 days.
