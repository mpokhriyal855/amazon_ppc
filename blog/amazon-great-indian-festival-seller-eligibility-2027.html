Every year ahead of Diwali, tens of thousands of Indian entrepreneurs, manufacturers, and direct-to-consumer (D2C) founders ask a fundamental question: Can anyone sell during the Amazon Great Indian Festival, or does Amazon require special invitation, account tenure, or exclusive administrative approval? The short, definitive answer is that any legally registered seller on Amazon.in with an account in good standing and approved product listings can sell products during the festival. However, there is a profound operational difference between having your catalog active on Amazon during the sale and qualifying for featured promotions such as Lightning Deals, 7-Day Deals, Prime Early Access, and digital coupons. This definitive, practitioner-grade guide clarifies every layer of seller eligibility, document requirements, GST mandates, category gating, and promotional prerequisites for the 2027 festive season.

1. Introduction: Can Anyone Sell During the Great Indian Festival?

The Amazon Great Indian Festival (GIF) is the premier shopping festival of the year in India, driving tens of thousands of crores in gross merchandise value across consumer electronics, home decor, apparel, packaged foods, and festive gifting. Because the visibility during this single event can transform the trajectory of an Indian business, a common myth persists among new entrepreneurs: Does Amazon invite only hand-picked corporate brands, or can any everyday business owner or individual sell?

To give a direct, legally accurate answer: Yes, virtually any legal business entity or individual residing in India who completes Amazon seller registration, fulfills tax compliance requirements, and lists products in compliant retail categories can sell during the Great Indian Festival 2027.

Amazon India does not charge a special "event entry fee," nor does it require a secret invitation just to keep your product detail pages active, searchable, and purchasable. If your account is in Good Standing and your products are in stock, you can participate.

However, sellers must understand a critical marketplace reality: selling eligibility is not the same as promotional eligibility. Simply being an active seller does not entitle your product to appear on the Amazon homepage banner, receive a Lightning Deal badge, or participate in Prime Early Access blitz promotions. To win during the festival, you must navigate a structured 5-tier eligibility hierarchy.

2. Can Anyone Sell During Amazon Great Indian Festival 2027?

Amazon India operates as an open marketplace under Indian Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) e-commerce regulations. As a marketplace intermediary, Amazon facilitates transactions between third-party sellers and end customers across India. Consequently, a broad range of business models can participate:

  • Individual Sellers & Sole Proprietorships: Small business owners operating under their personal PAN or local business trade license can register and sell across eligible categories.
  • Private Limited Companies & LLPs: Corporate entities, D2C startups, and established brands with formal GST registration and corporate bank accounts.
  • Manufacturers & Traditional Wholesalers: Domestic producers transitioning from offline distribution channels (such as Surat textiles, Moradabad brassware, or Ludhiana knitwear) to direct digital retail.
  • Private-Label Brand Owners: Brands that manufacture or white-label proprietary products protected under Indian Trademark law and registered with Amazon Brand Registry.
  • Third-Party Resellers & Distributors: Authorized distributors who purchase branded goods in bulk and hold valid brand authorization letters or commercial invoices to resell on Amazon.
  • Local Artisans & Women Entrepreneurs: Micro-enterprises participating through subsidized development programs such as Amazon Karigar and Amazon Saheli.
⚠️ IMPORTANT DISTINCTION: SELLING PERMISSION VS. GUARANTEED TRAFFIC

While anyone meeting basic statutory criteria can list and sell products, Amazon does not guarantee sales volume, Buy Box ownership, or visibility. In an event where 14+ lakh sellers compete for customer attention, discoverability depends on product listing quality, competitive pricing, customer review ratings, Prime delivery speed, and targeted advertising strategy.

4. Basic Eligibility Requirements to Sell on Amazon India

To establish an active seller account on Amazon.in, every prospective seller must fulfill basic statutory identity and operational requirements:

  • Permanent Account Number (PAN): A valid Indian PAN card in the name of the registered business or individual proprietor. For companies and LLPs, the corporate PAN is mandatory.
  • Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN): Under Section 52 of the Central GST Act, e-commerce operators must collect Tax Collected at Source (TCS), requiring marketplace sellers to provide a state-specific GSTIN corresponding to their pickup dispatch address.
  • Commercial Bank Account: An active bank account (savings account for sole proprietors or current account for corporate entities) matching the legal business entity name for weekly financial disbursements.
  • Operational Physical Pickup Address: A verifiable address within India where Amazon Easy Ship couriers or third-party logistics partners can collect packed orders and process customer returns.
  • Primary Contact Information: A dedicated commercial email address and mobile phone number for two-factor authentication (2FA) and critical Seller Central notifications.

6. Can New Sellers Participate in Amazon Great Indian Festival 2027?

A frequent concern among aspiring sellers is whether Amazon imposes a minimum tenure requirement—such as having an account open for 6 months or achieving 500 prior orders—before allowing participation in festive sales.

The reality: There is zero minimum account age or historical order threshold required to sell during the Great Indian Festival. If you register your account in July or August, complete document verification, and successfully list compliant products, your items will be live and purchasable on Day 1 of the sale.

9. Is GST Mandatory to Sell During Amazon Great Indian Festival?

One of the most widely debated legal questions among emerging sellers is whether Goods and Services Tax (GST) registration is universally required.

9.1 The General Statutory Rule: Mandatory GSTIN

Under Section 52 of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017, every e-commerce marketplace operator is legally required to collect Tax Collected at Source (TCS) on taxable commercial transactions. To enable this mandatory tax deduction and reporting, Amazon requires third-party sellers to provide a verified 15-digit GSTIN corresponding to their pickup address state.

9.2 The Statutory Exemption: 100% GST-Exempt Products

Under current Indian tax notifications and Amazon platform policies, a specific legal exception exists: Sellers who exclusively deal in 100% GST-exempt commodities can register and sell on Amazon.in using only their Permanent Account Number (PAN).

Examples of eligible GST-exempt product categories include:

  • Printed books, academic publications, maps, and manuscripts.
  • Unbranded agricultural produce (such as fresh fruits, raw vegetables, unbranded seeds, and unpasteurized milk).
  • Specific traditional handloom fabrics and khadi garments covered under specific GST exemption schedules.
⚖️ LEGAL TAX DISCLAIMER

If you sell even a single taxable SKU (such as a plastic book cover, a bookmark, or branded packaging), your entire catalog becomes subject to GST registration. Tax laws are subject to periodic state and central notifications. Always consult a qualified Chartered Accountant (CA) or certified tax advisor regarding your specific tax liability.

However, fulfillment method exerts a massive influence on conversion rates and deal access during peak shopping periods:

11. Promotional Eligibility: Separate Requirements for Deals and Coupons

While any seller can have their products visible at standard retail prices, participating in curated promotional programs requires meeting specific algorithmic benchmarks:

11.1 Self-Service Digital Coupons (Easiest Entry Point)

Digital Coupons (which display the prominent green badge in search results) are the most accessible promotional tool for new and growing sellers.

  • Eligibility Benchmark: Product rating of at least 3.0 stars (or 0 reviews for brand-new ASINs).
  • Minimum Discount: Minimum 5% off the current lowest price (or minimum ₹10/₹50 off).
  • Budget Requirements: Minimum campaign budget of ₹500 to ₹1,000 for redemption fees (₹0 redemption fee during specific seller promotional windows).
  • Buy Box Condition: You must own the active Buy Box for the coupon to appear.

11.2 Lightning Deals & 7-Day Deals (High-Volume Flash Events)

Lightning Deals run for 4 to 12 hours during peak festival days and appear on the dedicated Amazon Deals Page. Sellers cannot simply apply for a Lightning Deal manually; deals are algorithmically recommended by Amazon's retail systems based on:

  • Customer Rating: ASIN must possess at least a 3.5-star rating with a verified review history.
  • Pricing Rule: Discount must be at least 20% below the 30-day lowest selling price (LSP).
  • Fulfillment Rule: Prime fulfillment (FBA) is almost universally required for high-velocity sale days.
  • Deal Fee: Amazon charges a non-refundable deal participation fee per promoted ASIN.

12. Can Sellers Without Amazon PPC Advertisements Participate?

A common question from budget-conscious sellers is: Must I run paid Amazon Ads to sell during the Great Indian Festival?

No, running Amazon PPC ads is not legally or technically mandatory. Any active ASIN will appear in organic search results. If you sell a highly unique, patented, or niche product with minimal competition, you can generate sales purely through organic search discovery.

However, in competitive categories (such as wireless audio, kitchen appliances, or ethnic apparel), search result pages are dominated by paid placements. During peak festive hours, the first 4 to 6 search results on mobile devices are Sponsored Products or Sponsored Brands banners. Without a modest advertising budget to defend your brand and harvest high-intent generic searches, relying solely on organic visibility drastically limits your sales ceiling. 13. How to Check Your Great Indian Festival Eligibility in Seller Central

Do not guess your eligibility status. Execute this 5-step diagnostic audit directly inside Amazon Seller Central India:

STEP 1: Audit Account Health Dashboard

Navigate to Performance > Account Health. Verify that your Account Health Rating (AHR) is in the green zone (> 200). Confirm that your Order Defect Rate (ODR) is strictly below 1.0% and that you have zero unresolved Intellectual Property (IP) or Product Authenticity complaints.

STEP 2: Review Deal Recommendations

Go to Advertising > Deals > Create a New Deal. If Amazon's algorithm has qualified any of your ASINs for festive promotions, they will appear under the "Eligible ASINs" tab with recommended discount percentages and available deal timeframes.

STEP 3: Check Pricing & Buy Box Health

Navigate to Pricing > Pricing Health. Ensure none of your SKUs have been suppressed due to external reference pricing violations (e.g., lower pricing detected on Flipkart or Meesho). Suppressed listings cannot win the Buy Box during festive traffic surges.

STEP 4: Verify Inventory & Restock Limits

Go to Inventory > FBM / FBA Inventory. Confirm your Inventory Performance Index (IPI) score is above 400 to avoid storage capacity volume limitations ahead of the August/September inbound deadline.

STEP 5: Check Growth Recommendations

Navigate to Growth > Growth Opportunities to view Amazon's algorithmic suggestions regarding high-demand search keywords, B2B festive pricing opportunities, and catalog enhancement tips.

18. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Below are clear, direct, and fact-checked answers to the most common questions regarding seller eligibility, documentation, and promotional criteria for the Amazon Great Indian Festival 2027:

1. Who is eligible to sell during Amazon Great Indian Festival 2027? +

Any individual, sole proprietor, partnership firm, LLP, or private limited company legally registered as an active seller on Amazon.in with compliant KYC, verified banking credentials, and active listings in approved product categories is eligible to sell during the Great Indian Festival 2027. General selling does not require prior event pre-approval; however, access to promotional badges, Lightning Deals, and Prime Early Access requires fulfilling separate product-level performance criteria.

2. Can new Amazon sellers participate in Great Indian Festival 2027? +

Yes. Newly registered Amazon India sellers can participate in the Great Indian Festival. There is no mandatory account age or historical sales volume threshold required simply to have your listings active and purchasable during the event. However, new sellers must complete KYC identity verification, GSTIN setup (where applicable), and product listing approval at least 30 to 45 days before the sale to inbound inventory to FBA and build initial customer review momentum.

3. Is GST registration mandatory to sell on Amazon during the festive sale? +

In most commercial categories, yes. Under Section 52 of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, e-commerce operators must collect Tax Collected at Source (TCS), requiring marketplace sellers to possess a valid GSTIN. The sole exception applies to sellers dealing exclusively in 100% GST-exempt categories (such as printed books, unbranded agricultural produce, and handloom textiles under specific statutory notifications) who may register using an Indian PAN card, subject to Amazon's current platform policies.

4. Can small businesses and local artisans sell during the Great Indian Festival? +

Absolutely. Amazon India heavily promotes small businesses, local artisans, handloom weavers, and women-led enterprises through dedicated storefronts such as Amazon Karigar, Amazon Saheli, and Launchpad. Eligible small businesses with valid Udyam MSME registration and local artisan certifications receive subsidized referral fees, featured promotional placement, and dedicated festive marketing support.

5. What documents are required to register as an Amazon seller in India? +

Standard registration requires: (1) Permanent Account Number (PAN) card of the individual or business entity; (2) Valid GSTIN certificate corresponding to the pickup address state; (3) Active bank account statement or cancelled cheque matching the business legal name; (4) Registered commercial email address and mobile number; and (5) Valid physical pickup address for courier dispatches and return processing.

6. Do existing Amazon sellers need special approval to sell during festive sales? +

No special application or administrative approval is needed to keep your catalog live during the festival. If your Seller Central account is in Good Standing (Order Defect Rate < 1%, Cancellation Rate < 2.5%, Late Dispatch Rate < 4%), all active listings remain automatically purchasable. However, if you wish to participate in curated event promotions—such as Lightning Deals or 7-Day Deals—Amazon's algorithm generates invitation-only recommendations based on ASIN sales velocity and review ratings.

7. Are all product categories eligible for the Great Indian Festival? +

Most major retail categories—such as Consumer Electronics, Home & Kitchen, Fashion, Books, Beauty, Toys, Sports, and Packaged Grocery—are eligible. However, gated categories (including Health & Personal Care, Jewellery, and Automotive) require category-specific document approvals (e.g., FSSAI licenses, BIS certifications, and brand authorization letters). Restricted items (such as hazardous chemicals, counterfeit goods, or uncertified electronics) are strictly prohibited.

8. Can sellers using Easy Ship or Self-Ship participate in the festive sale? +

Yes. Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) is not legally or contractually mandatory to sell during the Great Indian Festival. Sellers utilizing Amazon Easy Ship or Seller-Fulfilled Self-Ship can fulfill orders during the event. However, FBA sellers enjoy a significant competitive edge because FBA products automatically receive the Prime badge, guaranteed 1-day/2-day delivery promises, and preferred placement in festival deal filters.

9. Is Amazon PPC advertising mandatory to participate in the sale? +

No, advertising is completely optional. Any registered seller with active inventory can generate sales organically. However, because search traffic surges by 4x to 8x and competitors invest heavily in Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands, relying exclusively on organic ranking during festive peaks limits visibility. Running targeted PPC campaigns helps secure top-of-search placements and capture surge shopper intent.

10. Are Lightning Deals and Coupons available to every seller? +

No. Deals and Coupons have separate qualification rules. Coupons are self-service and accessible to most Professional sellers with active Buy Boxes and products rated 3.0+ stars. Conversely, Lightning Deals and 7-Day Deals are algorithmically restricted: products must generally possess at least a 3.5-star rating, strong historical sales velocity, compliant pricing history (discounted against the 30-day lowest price), and FBA or Prime fulfillment.

11. How can I check my Great Indian Festival eligibility in Seller Central? +

Sign in to Amazon Seller Central India. Navigate to: (1) Performance > Account Health to confirm your metrics are in 'Good Standing'; (2) Inventory > Manage All Inventory to verify active Buy Box status and price health; (3) Advertising > Deals to view eligible ASIN recommendations for Lightning Deals and 7-Day Deals; and (4) Growth > Growth Opportunities for personalized festive catalog recommendations.
