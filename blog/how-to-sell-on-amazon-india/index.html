How to Sell on Amazon India: Registration, GST and Documents Required

Every month, I speak with Indian business owners—from retail shopkeepers running family stores in Chandni Chowk and Chickpet to brassware manufacturers in Moradabad and young entrepreneurs—who want to start selling on Amazon India. Almost all of them face the same initial roadblock: they are confused about GST rules, unsure which legal business structure to pick, and worried that a simple documentation mistake will lead to immediate account rejection. This comprehensive guide walks you through the exact paperwork, GST provisions, and 10-step registration process you need to launch with confidence.

Quick Answer · What Do You Need to Sell on Amazon India?

To register as an Amazon seller in India, you fundamentally require: an active PAN (Personal or Entity), a bank account with matching name and IFSC (savings for individuals, current for firms), a registered business address with valid utility or rental proof, an active mobile number and email ID, and either a valid GSTIN or a GST Enrolment ID (for eligible intra-state unregistered suppliers under Notification No. 34/2023). You do not need to incorporate a Private Limited company—a Sole Proprietorship is completely sufficient for most Indian sellers.

SECTION 01 · GETTING STARTED

1. Introduction: What Do You Need Before Selling on Amazon India?

When I speak to business owners across India who are eager to expand online, their enthusiasm is often halted by bureaucratic confusion.

Take a typical example: a retail merchant who has run a successful apparel shop in a tier-2 city for nearly 20 years. They have strong supplier relationships, proven inventory, and healthy local demand. Yet, the moment they consider Amazon, they are bombarded by conflicting advice: "You must form a Private Limited company," "GST is compulsory for every single product," "You need a CA audit before opening Seller Central."

None of these assumptions are universally true.

Creating an Amazon seller account is fundamentally a procedural KYC (Know Your Customer) process. Amazon wants to verify that you are a legitimate merchant, that your tax identity aligns with Indian statutory mandates, and that customer disbursements will reach a verified Indian bank account without disruption.

SCENARIO 01

Traditional Offline Retailer

Profile: Running a shop for 15+ years; possesses an active local GSTIN and shop trade license.
Approach: Use existing business GST and current account. Start with Easy Ship courier pickups directly from the store counter.

SCENARIO 02

Factory Manufacturer

Profile: Manufactures home decor or garments; currently supplies regional wholesalers.
Approach: Enroll own brand in Amazon Brand Registry, protect factory designs, and deploy FBA to capture direct retail margins.

SCENARIO 03

First-Time Entrepreneur

Profile: No existing registered firm; starting private label with modest savings.
Approach: Begin as a Sole Proprietorship using personal PAN. Obtain a single-state GSTIN once product sourcing is confirmed.

KEY TAKEAWAY

Your starting point determines your paperwork. A long-standing manufacturer with an existing GSTIN has a different onboarding path from an individual testing handicrafts. Never apply a one-size-fits-all checklist before auditing your legal entity and product category.

SECTION 02 · SELLER ELIGIBILITY

2. Who Can Sell on Amazon India?

Amazon.in is open to virtually any commercial entity legally capable of entering into a contract under Indian law. The marketplace does not discriminate by business scale, but your documentation must reflect your chosen legal entity:

Legal Business Structure Who It Represents Primary Entity Identifier Amazon Onboarding Feasibility
Individual / Sole Proprietorship Solo founders, freelance sellers, home businesses, and traditional shopkeepers Proprietor's Personal PAN Easiest & Most Popular: 80%+ of Amazon India sellers start here. No complex corporate filings required.
Partnership Firm Two or more co-owners pooling capital and sharing local profits Firm PAN + Partnership Deed Requires a registered Partnership Deed, separate firm PAN card, and dedicated firm bank account.
Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) Professional partnerships seeking limited personal liability protection LLP PAN + MCA Incorporation Certificate Combines corporate limited liability with partnership tax treatment. Requires annual MCA and ROC filings.
Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd) Funded startups, scaled consumer brands, and large corporate manufacturers Company PAN + Certificate of Incorporation Mandatory board resolution authorizing the signatory. Carries statutory annual audit and ROC compliance burdens.
Family-Run & HUF Businesses Multi-generational wholesale or retail trade entities Karta PAN / HUF PAN / Trade License Can register under the Karta or as a formal partnership between family members with matching GSTIN.
PRACTITIONER OBSERVATION

One misconception I encounter regularly is the belief that you must hire an agency to register a Private Limited company before Amazon will take your business seriously. In reality, Amazon’s search algorithm and Buy Box do not award ranking preference based on whether you are a Pvt Ltd or a Sole Proprietor. They rank listings on delivery speed, price competitiveness, customer reviews, and conversion rates.

SECTION 04 · TAX COMPLIANCE

4. Is GST Mandatory to Sell on Amazon India?

This is by far the most searched and misunderstood question regarding Amazon selling in India.

Under Section 24(ix) of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017, anyone supplying goods through an electronic commerce operator was historically required to obtain mandatory GST registration, regardless of turnover thresholds.

However, the GST Council introduced a significant reform via Notification No. 34/2023–Central Tax (effective 1 October 2023), designed to ease marketplace onboarding for micro and small enterprises.

GSTIN RULE: PAN-INDIA SELLING
Inter-State Supply = Mandatory GST Registration

If you intend to ship products from your home state to buyers in Mumbai, Bangalore, or Delhi, regular GSTIN registration is non-negotiable under Indian tax law.

EXEMPTION: INTRA-STATE SUPPLY
Turnover < ₹40 Lakhs + Same-State Delivery = Enrolment ID

Under Notification 34/2023, eligible unregistered suppliers can sell strictly within their home state using a PAN-generated Enrolment ID from the GST Portal.

Selling Scenario Is Full GSTIN Required? Legal Basis & Practical Operational Reality
Selling Pan-India (All Metros & States) YES (Mandatory) Inter-state supply requires a regular 15-digit GSTIN. Enables Amazon Easy Ship national delivery and Amazon FBA nationwide.
Selling 100% GST-Exempt Goods (e.g. Printed Books) NO Books are exempt from GST under Indian law. Sellers can onboard using their PAN card alone under Amazon’s exempt workflow.
Micro Seller (Intra-State Delivery Only) NO (Eligible for Enrolment ID) Requires a GST Enrolment ID obtained from gst.gov.in. Limitation: Orders are restricted to buyers inside your home state; cannot use inter-state FBA.
Using Amazon FBA Warehouses Across States YES (+ State APOB/VPOB) Stocking inventory in Amazon fulfillment centers outside your home state requires Additional Place of Business (APOB) / Virtual POB registration.
OUR CONSULTING RECOMMENDATION

While the law technically allows intra-state unregistered selling with an Enrolment ID, over 95% of e-commerce buyers reside outside a single state's borders. Restricting your catalog to intra-state orders severely limits your sales potential and prevents you from deploying Amazon FBA (Prime 1–2 day shipping). If you are building a serious commercial business, obtaining a regular GSTIN is an inexpensive, essential first step. Always consult a qualified Chartered Accountant for personalized tax advice.

SECTION 05 · ONBOARDING WALKTHROUGH

5. How to Register as an Amazon Seller in India – Step-by-Step

Follow this exact chronological 10-step walkthrough on the official Amazon India onboarding portal:

STEPS 01 TO 03

Account Creation & Authentication

Navigate to sell.amazon.in (refer to the official Amazon Seller Registration Guide). Click Start Selling. Log in using your dedicated business email and mobile number. Set up Two-Step Verification (2SV) via an Authenticator App or SMS OTP.

STEPS 04 TO 06

Tax Details, Store Naming & Pick-Up Address

Enter your Legal Business Name exactly as recorded on your PAN card. Input your 15-digit GSTIN (or select the unregistered seller pathway if eligible). Choose your customer-facing Store Name (this can be changed later). Configure your pickup address where Amazon ATS couriers will collect daily shipments.

STEPS 07 TO 08

Bank Account Linking & Identity Verification

Provide your bank account number and IFSC code. Upload your bank proof (cancelled cheque or statement). Complete identity verification by uploading color scans of your Aadhaar card or Passport. Amazon’s automated verification algorithms cross-reference your name against the Income Tax PAN database and GST Portal.

STEPS 09 TO 10

Category Ungating & Launching Seller Central

If selling in gated categories (packaged food, personal care, baby products), submit your FSSAI license or authorized distributor tax invoices. Once approved (typically within 24–48 hours), you receive full access to your live Seller Central dashboard.

Choosing the right legal structure avoids unnecessary compliance overhead while protecting your personal assets. Here is an honest practitioner comparison for Indian sellers:

Entity Structure Estimated Setup Cost Annual Compliance Overhead Personal Liability Recommended For
Sole Proprietorship ₹0 – ₹1,500 (Free via Udyam MSME) Minimal (Normal personal income tax return + monthly GST returns) Unlimited First-time entrepreneurs, small retail shop owners, and solo testing
Partnership Firm ₹2,500 – ₹6,000 (Deed stamp duty & registration) Moderate (Firm tax return + GST filings) Joint & Unlimited Family businesses and partners pooling working capital locally
Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) ₹6,000 – ₹12,000 (MCA incorporation) Moderate to High (ROC Form 8, Form 11 + Tax Audit if turnover crosses threshold) Limited to contribution Growing brands and e-commerce partnerships desiring corporate liability shields
Private Limited Company ₹8,000 – ₹18,000 (MCA incorporation) High (Statutory CA audit mandatory, Board resolutions, ROC filings) Limited to share capital Venture-backed D2C brands, large manufacturing factories, and high turnover
PRACTITIONER ADVICE

If you are starting with less than ₹5 Lakhs in initial capital, do not waste ₹15,000 and ongoing CA retainers on a Private Limited company. Start as a Sole Proprietorship using your personal PAN and an Udyam registration. You can seamlessly migrate your Amazon listings and brand registry to a Private Limited company later once your revenue reaches ₹50L+ annually.

SECTION 07 · TROUBLESHOOTING REJECTIONS

7. Common Amazon Seller Registration Problems and How to Avoid Them

Over 60% of delayed or rejected registrations on Amazon India stem from predictable, preventable errors. Here is how to resolve the top six roadblocks:

ISSUE 01

Name Discrepancies Across Documents

Cause: Having "Ramesh Kumar Sharma" on your PAN card, "Ramesh Sharma" on your bank cheque, and "Sharma Enterprises" on your GSTIN.
Fix: The legal name entered during registration must match your PAN card character-for-character. For sole proprietorships, the bank account holder name must be identical to the proprietor's PAN name.

ISSUE 02

Unserviceable Pick-up Address Pincode

Cause: Entering a remote factory or warehouse pincode where Amazon ATS Easy Ship does not operate daily courier pickups.
Fix: Check ATS pincode serviceability in Seller Central. If unserviceable, configure the account for Self-Ship via third-party logistics (Delhivery, BlueDart, DTDC) with valid tracking.

ISSUE 03

Blurred or Cut-Off Scanned Uploads

Cause: Uploading low-resolution mobile phone camera photos with flash glare, shadows, or cropped document corners.
Fix: Use a flatbed scanner or a dedicated scanner mobile app. Export as a crisp, full-bleed color PDF showing all four borders clearly.

ISSUE 04

Duplicate Account Association (Section 3)

Cause: Attempting to create a new seller account using a phone number, bank account, or PAN that was previously associated with a suspended or closed seller account.
Fix: Never create a new account to bypass a past suspension. Resolve the original account issue or ensure all credentials are completely fresh and unassociated.

ISSUE 05

Category Gating Rejections

Cause: Submitting retail cash memos or proforma estimates instead of authentic GST tax invoices when applying to sell in beauty, food, or electronics.
Fix: Amazon requires legitimate B2B tax invoices from authorized distributors showing both buyer and seller GSTINs, dated within the last 180 days, covering at least 10 units.

ISSUE 06

Mismatched Bank Account Proof

Cause: Providing a passbook photo without the bank's official branch stamp, or a cheque leaf where the name is not printed.
Fix: Download an official computer-generated PDF e-statement directly from your bank's net banking portal showing your address, account number, and IFSC.

SECTION 08 · OPERATIONAL INDEPENDENCE

8. Can You Register and Manage an Amazon Seller Account Yourself?

Yes, absolutely. The registration wizard on sell.amazon.in is designed so that any computer-literate business owner can complete the process independently.

However, there is a fundamental difference between completing registration and managing a profitable Amazon store:

Task Area Can You Do It Yourself (DIY)? When Professional Help Is Valuable
Initial Account Registration Yes (100% DIY) — Just prepare matching documents and follow the wizard. If you face repeated automated KYC rejections or complex multi-state GST registrations.
Product Listing & Cataloging Possible — Easy to upload basic titles and photos via Seller Central. When structuring high-converting A+ Content, keyword-rich backend search terms (<249B), and mobile infographics.
Fulfilment (FBA vs Easy Ship) Yes — Easy Ship pickups can be run from any local shop or home. Managing multi-state FBA warehouse inbound appointments, box labels, and freight logistics.
Advertising (Amazon PPC) Risky for Beginners — Easy to click "Auto Campaign" and lose money. Building isolated 4-tier campaign architectures, negative keyword harvesting, and TACoS governance.
Account Health & Claims Yes — Monitoring daily orders and dispatch deadlines. Drafting formal Plans of Action (POA) for policy violations, IP disputes, or Buy Box suppressions.
OUR CORE PHILOSOPHY

Even if you eventually hire an agency or full-time e-commerce manager, as the business owner, you must understand how Seller Central works and how fees erode your margins. When a founder understands their own unit economics, they make far smarter decisions regarding stock replenishment, advertising budgets, and agency performance.

SECTION 11 · PRACTICAL FAQ ACCORDION

11. Frequently Asked Questions About Selling on Amazon India

Clear, authoritative answers to the 15 most frequent registration, tax, and documentation queries:

1. How can I sell on Amazon India? +

To sell on Amazon India, gather your PAN, bank account details, business address proof, and active phone and email ID. Obtain a GSTIN (or Enrolment ID for eligible intra-state unregistered sellers), navigate to sell.amazon.in, complete the online registration wizard, pass identity verification, and configure your shipping and catalog settings in Seller Central.

2. What documents are required to sell on Amazon? +

The core documents are: PAN card (personal or entity), GSTIN certificate (or GST Enrolment ID), bank account proof (cancelled cheque or recent bank statement showing matching account holder name and IFSC), registered business address proof (utility bill, rental agreement, or trade license), and government identity proof of the authorized signatory (Aadhaar or Passport).

3. Is GST mandatory for Amazon seller registration? +

In general, yes for selling taxable goods across state lines and utilizing Amazon's pan-India FBA logistics. However, under Notification No. 34/2023–Central Tax, eligible micro-enterprises with turnover under ₹40 Lakhs (or ₹20 Lakhs for special states) selling intra-state only can operate using a GST Enrolment ID without a full GSTIN. Certain 100% GST-exempt goods (like books) also do not require GST registration.

4. Can I sell on Amazon without GST? +

Yes, under two specific legal conditions: 1) If you sell exclusively 100% GST-exempt products (such as printed books), or 2) If you qualify as an unregistered supplier making only intra-state deliveries within your home state, have obtained a valid Enrolment ID from the GST portal, and do not ship inter-state.

5. Can I sell on Amazon as an individual? +

Yes. You can register on Amazon India as an individual or sole proprietorship using your personal PAN card. You do not need to incorporate a company or form an LLP to begin selling.

6. Is a private limited company compulsory to sell on Amazon? +

No. A private limited company is completely optional. Over 80% of sellers on Amazon India operate successfully as sole proprietorships or individual businesses. A private limited structure is only necessary if you are raising institutional venture capital or have complex equity partnerships.

7. Can I use my existing GST number for Amazon seller registration? +

Yes. If you already run an offline retail shop, manufacturing unit, or wholesale trading business with an active GSTIN, you can use that same GSTIN for your Amazon seller account, provided the legal business name and PAN match exactly.

8. Can a traditional retail shop owner sell products on Amazon? +

Absolutely. Traditional retail shop owners can leverage their existing stock, supplier relationships, and shop GSTIN to expand nationwide on Amazon. You can start with Amazon Easy Ship, where Amazon couriers pick up packed orders directly from your shop counter.

9. Can a manufacturer sell directly on Amazon India? +

Yes. Manufacturers can capture direct-to-consumer retail margins by selling on Amazon. They should ensure their factory GSTIN is used, register their brand trademark with the Amazon Brand Registry, and prepare factory-invoiced products with proper Legal Metrology packaging.

10. How long does Amazon seller registration take? +

The online registration form takes about 15 to 25 minutes to complete if all documents are ready. Identity and bank verification by Amazon typically takes between 24 and 48 hours for full approval.

11. Is a current bank account required for Amazon seller registration? +

For registered corporate entities, LLPs, and partnership firms, a current bank account in the entity's name is mandatory. For individual sellers and sole proprietors, Amazon allows an individual savings bank account, provided the account holder name matches the PAN card exactly.

12. Can I register for Amazon selling myself? +

Yes. Any business owner can complete registration independently at sell.amazon.in by following the official step-by-step onboarding wizard. If you find documentation, category approvals, or fee math confusing, structured training or account management can provide hands-on assistance.

13. What happens if my seller registration is rejected? +

Registration rejections are almost always caused by name mismatches across PAN, GSTIN, and bank accounts, or blurred document uploads. Check the specific error message in Seller Central, re-upload clear scanned PDF copies with matching details, or open a ticket with Amazon Seller Support.

14. What should I do after creating my Amazon seller account? +

After account approval, complete product research, draft search-optimized listings with 249-byte backend keywords, choose between FBA and Easy Ship, verify your unit economics and Break-Even ACoS, and set up your initial Sponsored Products PPC campaigns.

15. Do I need professional help to start selling on Amazon? +

Professional help is not compulsory, but it is highly valuable if you want to avoid costly mistakes on inventory, fees, and ad spend. Before clicking "Register" on Seller Central:

  • Confirm that your business structure matches your long-term capital and liability needs.
  • Ensure that your PAN card, GSTIN certificate, and bank statement match character-for-character.
  • Verify whether your product category requires mandatory statutory licenses (FSSAI, BIS, Legal Metrology).
